Target: R_L(beta) = log[Z(L,beta)^2/Z(2L,beta)] <= K(d) + k M f(d) beta/L for every nearest-neighbour bond, vacuum-compatible L and all beta, which implies A <= (4k/pi) M f(d). C118 proved it for XX and for every classical bond (Cauchy–Schwarz on the nonnegative spatial transfer matrix gives R_L <= log d), and found the exact identity R_L = beta<V>_rho + I(A:B)_rho + D(rho_A||gamma_L) + D(rho_B||gamma_L), whose relative entropies are not bounded by dimension. Idea: replace relative entropy by distinguishability measures that ARE bounded by the seam's Hilbert-space dimension or by constants, and find the corresponding exact identity or inequality for R_L. Candidates: (i) max- and min-relative entropies and conditional min-entropy (bounded by log of the seam dimension, d^2 per cut across a time slice or d^k for a seam region of k sites), using that Z(L)^2 and Z(2L) differ by reconnecting two bonds; (ii) Petz and sandwiched Rényi divergences of order alpha < 1, and the alpha -> 0 limit, which are controlled by supports and dimensions, together with their relation to ratios of partition functions (log Tr rho^alpha sigma^(1-alpha) type expressions are themselves ratios of traces); (iii) trace distance and fidelity, which are bounded by constants, combined with Fuchs–van de Graaf; (iv) a quantum version of the classical proof: Hilbert projective metric and Birkhoff contraction for positive (not necessarily completely positive) maps, applied to a spatial transfer operator of the Euclidean path integral, or a quantum Cauchy–Schwarz for Tr[T^L]^2 versus Tr[T^(2L)] where T is the column transfer operator of the Trotterized path integral, keeping track of how non-positivity enters and whether it can be controlled at order beta M/L. Test every candidate inequality numerically on XX, Heisenberg and SU(3) rings by exact diagonalization, and on XX at large L via free fermions.
