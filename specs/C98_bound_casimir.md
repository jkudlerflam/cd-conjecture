# C98: The bound from the Casimir energy
Your folder is `../work_C98/`. Read `../../specs/_bound_background.md` first. Route: A is the 1/L coefficient of the ring ground energy. Look for a lattice inequality E_0(L) - e_0 L >= -(pi/6) pi M log_2 d / L + o(1/L), or any inequality of the form E_0(L) - e_0 L >= -C M log d / L, using only locality, the bond scale M and the local dimension: for example by comparing the ring of length L with rings of length 2L or with the open chain via a variational state built from an MPS or a purification, by relating the cost of closing a ring to the entanglement across the closing bond (bounded by log of the Schmidt rank, hence by multiples of log d per site), or by a reflection-positivity or convexity argument in L. Test any candidate inequality on exactly solvable chains (XX, XXZ, Heisenberg, SU(N), the SU(N) symmetric representations) before trying to prove it, and find the weakest extra hypothesis under which it holds if it fails in general.
