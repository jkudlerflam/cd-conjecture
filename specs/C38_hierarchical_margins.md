**Task.** Route A needs, for every n, distinct Fermi-sea centres and a full set of Fermi points that misses every relevant or marginal non-oscillating quartic resonance (pair transfer, umklapp, exchange) by a margin larger than the generated corrections. In the 23-letter cell the band scales shrink by a factor of about 50 to 100 per flavour, so the flavours live at strongly separated energy scales. Turn that hierarchy into a proof: show that with an explicit parameter schedule (for example geometrically separated couplings, or the natural exponential separation of the resolvent), each flavour's Fermi momentum and centre are fixed at its own scale up to relative corrections that are exponentially small in the separation, and that geometric or lacunary sequences of this kind satisfy every resonance inequality with explicit margins (lacunary sequences with ratio above 2 have distinct pair sums, for example). Handle the cross-scale terms of the effective chain explicitly. Give the result for the frozen chain and for C32's mobile chain. If the hierarchy argument fails, say exactly where.
