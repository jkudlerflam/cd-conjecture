**Task.** Decide at the physics standard whether h_24 has, on rings of every large length L, a low-energy theory that is one CFT with c = n + 1 (n payload flavours plus the vacancy fluid), in particular c = 24 at n = 23.
1. Squeezed-space factorization at J → 0 and its first-order form: show the payload is the frozen 23-letter chain on the squeezed ring of M occupied sites, with the Ogata–Shiba twist from the vacancy positions.
2. The key issue: the payload spontaneously breaks squeezed translation to period b = n + 1. Work out what this becomes once the payload is glued to the vacancy fluid: whether the b squeezed offsets turn into twisted (flux) sectors of the vacancy boson, so the physical ring has a unique ground state and a conformal tower structure, and what happens when M is not a multiple of b (the vacancy number can adjust; compute the energetics). State the exact conditions on t, J, μ and vacancy density.
3. Mixed vertices between the vacancy fluid and the payload at finite J/t: classify them, give their momenta and dimensions, and show they are irrelevant or merely marginal and Gaussian on an open set.
4. Conclude whether a careful physicist should accept c = n + 1 at fixed n, and what remains for all n.
