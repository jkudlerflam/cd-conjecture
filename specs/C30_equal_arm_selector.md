# C30: A selector for symmetric switch cells (route A)

Read `../../specs/_routeA_background.md`, `../../contrib/routeA_assembly/README.md` §7 and `../../contrib/routeA_compile/README.md` first (a fixed-alphabet route-A interface with exact fermion number, and a central-switch cell P_L L^m M R^m P_R whose effective chain has c = n at n = 1 to 4). If `PROGRESS.md` exists in your folder, continue from it.

**Your task.** The central-switch cell needs its two arms to have equal length m, a single central switch M, and ports at both ends, and the cell length must encode n. Design a translation-invariant nearest-neighbour selector on a fixed alphabet that makes such symmetric cells spontaneously, with an energy margin against every asymmetric or malformed segment, and that selects m by one chemical potential (as C7's walker does for plain segments, `../work_C7/C7_result.md`). Consider walkers that bounce between the ports and the centre, two coupled walkers, or a "mirror" constraint built from local rules. Then decide how it combines with C8's mobile wrapper (`../work_C8/`, C13, C20, C25), where cells of two adjacent lengths must coexist: a symmetric cell can only change length by 2, so say what the length fluid looks like. Prove the selection with explicit margins, check it by exact diagonalization of the segment problem for small m, count the alphabet, and give d0 (frozen and mobile). If symmetric cells cannot be selected locally, find the weakest asymmetry the switch cell tolerates (`../../contrib/routeA_compile/` reports that a 5% arm asymmetry is harmless at n = 2) and design for that instead.

Rules: theoretical-physics standard, finite-dimensional statements proved. Tag every claim as proved, numerical (with sizes), heuristic, or assumed. Do not put probabilities, odds or percent-confidence estimates in any file. Python (numpy and scipy) only, inside your folder; no network; never modify files outside your folder; keep each computation to minutes. Write prose plainly, without em-dashes. Keep `PROGRESS.md` updated and write `RESULT.md` at the end.
