## Setting

C143 found a singular qubit bond missing from C141's list, h = X⊗Z (Z⊗X is its mirror). After a Hadamard on odd sites it is the isotropic quantum compass chain. Its spectrum is a critical Majorana chain (c = 1/2, velocity 4 in units where the bond has unit couplings) times an exactly degenerate space of dimension 2^(L/2−1). I confirmed this by ED at L = 8, 10, 12 (degeneracy 8, 16, 32, gap times L near 3.16, Casimir c v ≈ 2.03). On the family h = XZ + α ZX + β YY the chain is two commuting Majorana chains, A (the critical one) and B (the degenerate one), with hopping ratio α and energy-energy coupling β, an anisotropic Ashkin–Teller chain. For generic small (α, β) the B chain gets first-order hopping of order α + β⟨a⟩ and c_tot = 1 (C143 numerics give 0.98 to 1.01). On the tuned cone α + β⟨a⟩ = 0, α ≈ (2/π)β to leading order, the B chain gets dynamics only at second order and becomes an interacting Majorana chain with long-range four-fermion tails, of the Rahmani–Zhu–Franz–Affleck type, whose phase diagram contains Ising (c = 1/2), tricritical Ising (c = 7/10) and an incommensurate c = 3/2 phase. ED to L = 16 on the cone gave c estimates falling from about 2.5 to 1.1–1.5, not converged, because the B crossover length (hopping of order β²) exceeds the sizes reached.

Two different questions ride on this, and the answer to each should be reported separately.

(Q1) For C(2) < ∞. What matters is that Q_L(h) = L(E_{2L} − 2E_L)/M is bounded uniformly in L for h near XZ. Below the B crossover length, C131's cover lemma controls the B part by the size of the perturbation. Beyond it, the B sector contributes c_B v_B with v_B = O(δ M) or O(δ² M), which is small unless c_B is unbounded. So it is plausible that Q_L is bounded near XZ whatever c_B is, and this should be settled first.

(Q2) For the sharper c_tot ≤ 1 and for the physics question of whether any nearest-neighbour qubit bond has c > 1. This needs c_B on the tuned cone.
