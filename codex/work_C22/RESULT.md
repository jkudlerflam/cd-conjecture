# C22. A spatial walker cell and its exchange obstruction

## Outcome

[Proved] There is a computation-free, one-bond spatial cell with local dimension d0 = 17, an exactly 2^n-dimensional code, exact on-site charge and parity, zero active first-order port compressions, and at least n+1 accessible excitation energies. Its selector is a modification of C7 in which the selector walker itself carries the virtual excitation. No initialized program, work tape, or history computation is used.

[Proved] This cell does not finish route A. For its symmetric rails, second-order quadratic exchange cancels without flux. Uniform rail flux restores exchange but does not supply independent flavor phases. At n=2, the leading quadratic exchange has a strictly gapped Bloch symbol at zero logical chemical potential, for every nonzero uniform flux that generates hopping. The mechanism is unequal exchange between the two endpoint flavors, which forms dimers. The number of poles is sufficient; their operator-valued weights are unsuitable.

[Numerical] Full legal-cell diagonalization was done for two and three cells at n=1,2, including all vacuum-walker excited states and all three charged/neutral excitation sectors. The largest Hilbert space has dimension 110592. These checks verify the second-order calculation and its small finite-coupling corrections. They do not measure a central charge.

[Proved, scope] A separate tree-bus theorem excludes a broader class of simple wires at second order. Neither that theorem nor the 17-state example excludes all spatial designs. In particular, nonuniform flux generated by additional local structure, more bus channels, and noncommuting ground-space codes remain open. No successful fixed-d0 family with c >= n is obtained here. The value 17 is the alphabet of the fully specified, unsuccessful candidate, not the local dimension of a counterexample.

## 1. Three spatial mechanisms and their limitations

### 1.1 A unitary wire on a tree

[Proved] Let a one-excitation bus have vertices x and carry a logical register F. Suppose

    H_exc = sum_x e_x |x><x| tensor I
          + sum_(xy) t_xy |x><y| tensor U_xy + h.c.,

where the graph is a tree, U_xy is unitary, and U_yx = U_xy^dagger. Choose a root o. Set W_o=I and choose W_x recursively so that

    W_x^dagger U_xy W_y = I.

There is no consistency condition on a tree because there is a unique path from the root to each vertex. Consequently the block-diagonal unitary

    W = sum_x |x><x| tensor W_x

satisfies W^dagger H_exc W = A tensor I, where A is the scalar weighted graph Hamiltonian. For a source S=|o> tensor I,

    S^dagger f(H_exc) S = <o|f(A)|o> I.                 (1)

The same result holds at any fixed source vertex after changing root. It applies to resolvents and heat kernels, including all repeated outward-and-return excursions inside one cell. Position-dependent scalar energies and arbitrarily many scalar poles do not change it.

[Proved] Use three orthogonal source channels with no mixing, and couple two cells through a sum of corresponding port products. Every same-port spectral weight is scalar. Thus

    H_eff^(2) = -lambda^2 sum_ab integral_0^infinity
                  C_R,ab(tau) tensor C_L,ab(tau) d tau

is scalar. It cannot contain a logical hopping term or a chemical potential. Unitary SWAP transport of stored bits does not help, because each return path undoes its transport.

[Proved, scope] This is a second-order obstruction to same-port return designs, not a theorem about every perturbative order. At higher inter-cell order, a cell can be visited through both ports, and the Wilson line between distinct source vertices need not be scalar. If all used source maps also become logical identities under the same gauge, the complete model factorizes and the obstruction extends to every order. That stronger hypothesis must be checked, not assumed.

[Concrete implementation] The 17-state cell below with conversion g=0 is a repeated-bond realization of this tree wire. It has n spatial data qubits, n+1 poles, the exact selector, and scalar exchange. This is more than an abstract graph example.

### 1.2 Repeated sub-blocks and commuting frustration-free parents

[Concrete limit] In the 17-state alphabet below, set g=0 and set all excited-species hoppings to zero while retaining the vacuum selector hopping. The code remains (4), the endpoint amplitude remains (9), and the source excitation has one energy d. Equation (12) gives -3 lambda^2 alpha_n^4/(2d) times identity. In physical variables every data bit is a spectator of the entire Hamiltonian, so no order generates hopping. This is an explicit repeated-bond version of independent spatial storage.

[Proved] Independent finite sub-blocks carrying free data have a 2^n code, but a port attached to a bounded number of sub-blocks only accesses those blocks. If the remaining register is a spectator in H0 and V, it remains a spectator at every order. Length and degeneracy alone do not produce n transport channels.

[Proved] There is also a useful second-order restriction on commuting positive nearest-neighbor parents. Let H_cell=sum_j h_j, all h_j commute, and h_j G=0. For a boundary source F, every term whose support misses F commutes with F and annihilates F G. Since the h_j commute, it continues to annihilate the entire source cyclic subspace. Therefore

    exp(-tau H_cell) F G = exp(-tau H_near) F G,

where H_near consists only of terms intersecting the original support of F. For a one-site endpoint operator this is a bounded-size region, independent of cell length. Every source weight G^dagger F_a exp(-tau H_cell) F_b G is the compression of an operator in that fixed region. Its span is bounded by the dimension of that region's operator algebra. It cannot supply an unbounded number of linearly independent odd hopping factors. This statement also covers entangled codes of commuting parents; it does not require a product code.

[Proved, scope] Noncommuting frustration-free Hamiltonians are outside this argument. The looped cell below has positive-energy virtual dynamics with noncommuting terms and is outside this commuting-parent obstruction. It is also outside C18's narrower static-Bell-check obstruction, because its port creates a mobile excited token, not a Bell syndrome left unchanged by port-idle propagation.

[Proved, restricted Gaussian check] A fully quadratic cell with zero modes and purely quadratic inter-cell coupling has another elementary obstruction. If each active linear port operator has zero projection onto every zero mode, it contains only massive modes. The full quadratic Hamiltonian then leaves all logical zero modes decoupled at every order. This does not cover interacting buses or bilinear port operators whose products make an interacting microscopic bond.

### 1.3 A looped, charge-conserving bus

[Proved] A bus with several internal species and local species-changing conversions has loops in its excitation configuration graph. Equation (1) no longer applies. The following construction realizes such loops without a history-state computation. Its failure is a calculation of its actual exchange matrix, not an application of the tree theorem.

## 2. The 17-state repeated bond

### Alphabet and legal cells

[Definition] The physical basis consists of

    L_a, R_a                       a=0,1                     4 states
    X_(s,a)                        s=v,0,+,-; a=0,1         8 states
    M_empty, M_s                   s=v,0,+,-                5 states.

The symbols L,R,X carry a data qubit. Markers M do not. The token species v is the ground, or vacuum, species; 0 is an excited neutral species; + and - are excited species of charge +1 and -1. Data bits stay at their physical sites when the token moves.

[Definition] Ignore data and species when assigning arrows:

    L = (L,L), R = (R,R), X_s = (L,R),
    M_empty = (R,L), M_s = (L,L).

A pair is allowed when its arrows concatenate. Put a penalty K on every forbidden pair. Marker status means either M_empty or M_s, and is conserved.

[Proved] Between successive markers, a legal cell with n data sites has one of the forms

    L^j X_s R^(n-j-1) M_empty,       j=0,...,n-1,
    L^n M_s.

There is exactly one token, with m=n+1 possible positions, and 2^n independent data states. This is the complete legal-cell Hilbert space, of dimension

    D_n = 4(n+1) 2^n.                                      (2)

It is not a one-particle truncation of a larger legal bus Fock space. The grammar enforces one token per cell.

### Local moves

[Definition] The forward token moves are

    X_(s,a) R_b  -> L_a X_(s,b),
    X_(s,a) M_empty -> L_a M_s.

Include each with its Hermitian conjugate. Their amplitudes are -t_s exp(i phi_s), multiplied by (-1)^a for s=0 only. Take t_s=t and phi_v=0 throughout this report. The vacuum hopping has no data operation. The neutral hopping has a Z on the departure data qubit. The charged hoppings have no data operation.

[Definition] On every bulk token site include

    g |X_(+,0)><X_(0,1)|
  + g |X_(-,1)><X_(0,0)| + h.c.                           (3)

There is no conversion on a marker because it carries no data. Give every excited token, s=0,+,-, energy Delta_bare. Give every marker chemical potential mu_sel. All on-site terms are split equally over the two neighboring copies of h. Together with the arrow penalty and the moves above, these prescriptions specify one repeated nearest-neighbor Hermitian bond on C^17 tensor C^17.

[Proved] Each move preserves its external arrows, so every forbidden-pair projector commutes with the Hamiltonian. Marker positions are also conserved. Data do not acquire a kinetic energy in the vacuum-token sector. The n logical bits are stored directly in n physical qubits.

### Exact charge and parity

[Definition] Let the charge of a bulk letter be its data occupation a plus the charge of its token, if present. L,R and v,0 tokens have zero bus charge. An occupied marker has only its bus charge; M_empty has charge zero.

[Proved] Both transitions in (3), all hopping moves, and all diagonal penalties conserve the sum Q of these on-site charges. Thus

    U = product_sites (-1)^(Q_site)

is an exact product on-site parity. On the ground code it is

    P = product_(i=0)^(n-1) Z_i.

This stronger U(1) symmetry forbids pairing at every order. It does not impose separate flavor U(1)'s after the logical hopping is diagonalized.

## 3. Ground code, selector, and gap

[Proved] Put b=n+1=m. In the vacuum sector the token has the open-path Hamiltonian with b vertices. Its ground energy and normalized wavefunction are

    E_b = mu_sel - 2t cos(pi/(b+1)),
    v_j = sqrt(2/(b+1)) sin(pi(j+1)/(b+1)), j=0,...,b-1.

The code is

    G psi = sum_j v_j |j,v> tensor psi,                    (4)

and has dimension 2^n. The token ground wavefunction carries no computation or data-dependent evolution.

[Proved] Excited-species hopping is a direct sum of paths with unitary link operators. Its smallest eigenvalue is -2t cos(pi/(b+1)). The conversion operator has norm at most |g| in the one-token sector: at each data position it consists of two disjoint two-state transitions. Hence every excited-token state lies at least Delta_bare-|g| above the vacuum ground. If Delta_bare>|g|, the code in (4) is the complete ground space. A cell-gap bound is

    Delta_cell >= min{
      2t[cos(pi/(b+1))-cos(2pi/(b+1))],
      Delta_bare-|g|}.                                    (5)

The first entry is omitted when b=1.

[Proved, adapted C7 selector] Set mu_sel=2t-delta and

    a_b=2t[1-cos(pi/(b+1))],
    e_b=(a_b-delta)/b,
    delta_b=(b+1)a_b-b a_(b+1).

The function a(x) is strictly decreasing and strictly convex for x>=1. The C7 difference calculation gives

    e_j-e_(j+1)=(delta_j-delta)/(j(j+1)).

Therefore delta_b<delta<delta_(b-1) selects length b uniquely. For b>=3 the midpoint has mu_sel>0. For b=2 one can use delta=1.9t, which lies between delta_2=(2sqrt(2)-1)t and delta_1=3t and keeps mu_sel>0.

[Proved] Define gamma_b=min_(k!=b) k(e_k-e_b)>0. Excited-token sectors cannot lower a legal cell energy by (5). Marker-free legal rings are all-L or all-R, with energy zero. In an illegal sector, remove complete legal cells; each remaining fragment has at most one excess token and its hopping is bounded below by -2t per token. Conversion is bounded below by -|g| per excited token and is covered by Delta_bare. The same two-end-fragment estimate as C7 gives a defect cost at least K-4t when mu_sel>=0. Thus K=8t suffices. On compatible rings a lower gap bound above the complete unperturbed selected manifold is

    min{gamma_b, omega_b, |e_b|b, K-4t, Delta_bare-|g|},    (6)

where omega_b is the vacuum-path gap in (5). Each of the b translated marker patterns carries the full product of logical codes.

[Proved, asymptotic check] At a midpoint selection,

    omega_b = Theta(t b^-2),
    gamma_b = Theta(t b^-4),
    delta_(b-1)-delta_b = Theta(t b^-3).

The shift b to b+1 changes the exact selector energies but not these powers. The construction remains a frozen selector. It does not implement C8's mobile wrapper or solve the all-ring ground-state issue.

## 4. Ports and the exact second-order answer

[Definition] On either an occupied marker or a bulk X site, define token operators

    F0 = |0><v| + |v><0|,
    F+ = |+><v| + |v><-|,
    F- = F+^dagger.

They vanish on the other letter types and act as identity on any data bit. On every oriented marker-to-bulk bond put

    lambda V = lambda [F0^M F0^X
        + exp(i phi) F+^M F-^X + exp(-i phi) F-^M F+^X].    (7)

Take phi=pi/2 in the checks. It is Hermitian and charge-conserving. The operator norm of its active token bond is sqrt(3). Its left factor acts on the right endpoint of one cell; its right factor acts on the left endpoint of the next.

[Proved] Every active port changes a vacuum token to an excited token, so

    G^dagger F0 G = G^dagger F+ G = G^dagger F- G = 0.     (8)

These are the active port compressions required for PVP=0. The claim is not that every traceless observable of a bulk physical site has zero compression: its stored data qubit remains logical.

[Proved] The source amplitude at each endpoint is

    alpha_n = sqrt(2/(n+2)) sin(pi/(n+2)).                  (9)

In the excited neutral sector make the gauge change

    U_j = Z_0 ... Z_(j-1),   j=0,...,n.

Leave both charged sectors unchanged. The neutral Z-hopping becomes scalar. The local conversions become the Jordan-Wigner fermions

    c_j = Z_0 ... Z_(j-1) sigma_j^-,
    H_(+,0)(j)=g c_j,  H_(-,0)(j)=g c_j^dagger.           (10)

The source maps, after dividing by alpha_n, are

    S_L,a = |0,a> tensor I,                     a=0,+,-,
    S_R,0 = |n,0> tensor P,
    S_R,+ = |n,+> tensor I,   S_R,- = |n,-> tensor I.      (11)

Thus the parity string on the right port is obtained from a local spatial wire. It is not inserted as a nonlocal microscopic term.

[Proved] Measure excited energies relative to the vacuum ground and write

    d = Delta_bare + 2t cos(pi/(n+2)),
    A_a = d I - t sum_(j=0)^(n-1)
            [exp(i phi_a)|j+1><j| + h.c.].

Let H1 be the three-rail matrix with these A_a and the conversions (10). Define

    B = S_R,0 tensor S_L,0
      + exp(i phi) S_R,+ tensor S_L,-
      + exp(-i phi) S_R,- tensor S_L,+.

Then the exact coefficient at second order in lambda, with g arbitrary, is

    H2 = -lambda^2 alpha_n^4
          B^dagger (H1 tensor I + I tensor H1)^-1 B.        (12)

All excitation energies in this formula are positive. No excited-state approximation is made in (12).

[Proved] Distinct inter-cell bonds have zero second-order cross term. An outer cell is acted on once, is left in an excited-token species, and cannot return to the vacuum code under H0. Thus the second-order chain Hamiltonian is the sum of (12), with no three-cell term. At finite lambda, the Hamiltonian still has every state in (2); returning an excited token to v can populate excited vacuum-walker modes. Those modes are retained in the full ED below.

### Poles and the pole-count bound

[Proved] At g=0 there are exactly n+1 distinct accessible positive energies,

    epsilon_r = d - 2t cos(pi r/(n+2)), r=1,...,n+1,        (13)

with endpoint weights 2 sin^2(pi r/(n+2))/(n+2)>0. These are spatial standing-wave energies. The number is not supplied by a flavor label in the alphabet. For sufficiently small nonzero g, their separated clusters persist, and may split further. The checked parameters give 6 distinct accessible energies at n=1 and 23 at n=2; these counts use eigenvalue grouping tolerance 1e-8 and source-overlap tolerance 1e-8, so they are numerical counts.

[Proved] At g=0 all source weights are scalar and (12) reduces to

    -3 lambda^2 alpha_n^4 sum_(r,s)
        w_r w_s/(epsilon_r+epsilon_s) I.                  (14)

This demonstrates why a sufficient pole count alone does not establish exchange.

[Proved] The physical token port has dimension four, rather than two. Nevertheless the same counting argument behind 2n<=4(k-1) applies to its three active Hermitian source channels F0, (F++F-)/sqrt(2), and (F+-F-)/(i sqrt(2)). Their source inner products are alpha_n^2 delta_ab I, with one even and two odd channels. There are four odd ordered source slots and each has zero sum over the energy projectors. Their odd span is at most 4(k-1). Equation (13) meets the necessary count, 2n<=4n. It does not meet the additional spectral-weight design requirements.

## 5. Compute the leading exchange, rather than infer it from pole count

[Definition] For the equal-magnitude rails define the real scalar heat kernel

    K(tau)=exp[-tau(d I-t A_path)],
    f_s(i,tau)=integral_0^tau K_(s,i)(tau-u) K_(i,s)(u) du,
    F_ij=integral_0^infinity f_n(i,tau) f_0(j,tau) d tau.   (15)

Here i,j=0,...,n-1 and the endpoint labels are 0,n. Every F_ij is strictly positive for t>0 and d>2t. Let

    kappa = phi_0-(phi_++phi_-)/2,
    chi = (phi_--phi_+)/2.

[Proved] The first Duhamel insertion converting neutral to positive charge at source s is

    -g sum_i f_s(i,tau)
          exp[i(phi_0-phi_+)(s-i)] c_i^dagger,

with c_i replacing c_i^dagger for negative charge and phi_- replacing phi_+. The right neutral source adds the P in (11). Contract the neutral-pair source in B with each charged-pair source and then add the Hermitian conjugates. Moving P past an odd c gives the difference, not the sum, of the two conversion amplitudes. Consequently

    H2 = scalar + lambda^2 alpha_n^4 g^2
           [sum_ij T_ij c_i^dagger P tensor c_j + h.c.]
           + O(lambda^2 alpha_n^4 g^4),

    T_ij = 2i exp(i phi) F_ij exp[i chi(n-i+j)]
                         sin[kappa(n-i-j)].               (16)

T has units of energy^-3. The overall coefficient lambda^2 g^2 T has energy units.

[Proved] At this order the same-cell terms are scalar. To see the cancellation, pair a two-conversion excursion starting in the neutral rail with the time-reversed excursion starting in its charged rail. Their scalar propagators coincide after exchanging the two conversion positions and reversing the time variables. Their logical products are c_i^dagger c_j and c_j c_i^dagger. Summing the occupied source species in (7) gives the anticommutator delta_ij I. This works for each of the positive and negative rails, including the uniform phases. There is no induced logical chemical potential at order g^2. At order g^4, nonquadratic terms generally remain.

[Proved, independent algebraic formula] The coefficient in (16) can also be evaluated without a small finite difference. Write the two-cell excited Hamiltonian as A+g C and use

    [g^2] H2/(lambda^2 alpha_n^4)
       = -B^dagger A^-1 C A^-1 C A^-1 B.                 (17)

The code computes (17) and (15) independently. The scalar same-cell pieces are included in (17).

### Why real rails fail

[Proved] When kappa=0 mod pi, every sine in (16) vanishes. In particular real equal-sign rails produce no quadratic hopping at order lambda^2 g^2. This cancellation is the fermion-parity sign in (11), not a missing pole. The full order-lambda^2 result at nonzero g need not be scalar: quartic and higher terms appear at g^4. Calling those terms a free-flavor target would be incorrect.

### Why the uniform-flux version also fails the target test

[Proved] The phase factor in (16) is an overall phase times a spatial gauge:

    T = exp[i(phi+pi/2+chi n)] D^dagger R D,
    D_ii=exp(i chi i),

where R is real. Thus uniform rail phases do not give n independent Fermi-sea centers. If R were normal and reducible to independent real hopping flavors, their centers would coincide modulo pi. For a general real nonnormal R, the Bloch eigenvalues still have a common reflection center. Total U(1) removes pairing, but does not forbid inter-flavor Cooper transfer at such a common center.

[Proved] At n=2 write the hopping matrix as

    T = [[a,b],[c,0]].

Its bottom-right entry is zero because n-i-j=0. With zero logical chemical potential, the Bloch symbol is h(k)=exp(ik)T+exp(-ik)T^dagger, and

    det h(k) = -|exp(ik)b+exp(-ik)c*|^2.                  (18)

For this path, |c|>|b| whenever sin(kappa) is nonzero. This inequality holds at every d>2t, not only at the numerical point. Reflection of the three-vertex scalar path gives

    F_10-F_01 = integral_0^infinity
                 f_0(1,tau)[f_0(0,tau)-f_0(2,tau)] d tau >0.

Indeed K_00(tau)=exp(-d tau)[1+cosh(sqrt(2)t tau)]/2 is strictly larger than K_02(tau)=exp(-d tau)[cosh(sqrt(2)t tau)-1]/2. Their convolutions in (15) inherit that strict inequality. The remaining factor f_0(1,tau) is positive. The sine factors in T_10 and T_01 have the same absolute value. This proves the claim.

[Proved] Equation (18) is strictly negative for every k. One band is strictly positive and one strictly negative, with no Fermi point. For example a lower bound on the absolute single-particle gap is

    (|c|-|b|)^2 / (2|a|+|b|+|c|) >0.

If sin(kappa)=0, all entries vanish instead. Thus this n=2 leading quadratic family gives neither two independent conducting flavors nor a robust half-filled c=2 reference point.

[Numerical] At d=4,t=1, phi=pi/2, (phi_0,phi_+,phi_-)=(0.41,0.31,-0.07), the nonzero entries of T are approximately

    a = -4.35158339e-6 + 1.73807776e-6 i,
    b = -3.11689716e-7 + 1.99783306e-7 i,
    c = -8.99063829e-5 + 1.72907810e-5 i.

Sampling 20001 momenta gives bands

    [-9.67293916e-5, -8.73576913e-5],
    [ 8.73576913e-5,  9.67293916e-5],

before multiplying by lambda^2 alpha_2^4 g^2. The nonzero gap follows from (18); the sampled values only estimate its size. At n=3 the same check finds two separated bands and one band crossing zero, rather than three independent gapless bands.

[Proved, scope] A uniform physical density perturbation can supply a common logical chemical potential. This report does not supply independently adjustable spatial chemical potentials or prove impossibility under additional local density or flux mechanisms. Such changes can alter (18). The failure established here is for the stated equal-rail family and its controlled expansion around zero logical field, not for every Hamiltonian on the 17-letter alphabet.

## 6. Scale hierarchy and infrared requirements

[Proved] At each fixed n the selector, cell gap, and endpoint visibility are positive. Their asymptotic sizes are

    Delta_cell = Omega(t n^-2)  when Delta_bare-|g| stays positive,
    alpha_n = Theta(n^-3/2),
    alpha_n^4 = Theta(n^-6),
    gamma_b = Theta(t b^-4).

Here the first formula also has a matching O(t n^-2) upper bound from the vacuum-walker excitation. Let eta_n be the smallest of (6), and let j_n denote the scale of a desired nonzero hopping coefficient in T. The intended logical scale would be

    J_n = lambda^2 alpha_n^4 g^2 j_n.                      (19)

[Proved, finite-cluster perturbative bounds] A sufficient conservative choice is |lambda| sqrt(3)<<eta_n and |g| below the excited-sector gap, followed by the stronger comparisons

    C_n lambda^2 alpha_n^4 g^4/delta_exc^5 << J_n epsilon_n,
    C'_n lambda^4/eta_n^3 << J_n epsilon_n.                (20)

The first controls terms beyond the quadratic g^2 exchange. The second controls the next inter-cell order. Constants may depend on the fixed cell, not on total chain length. All odd lambda orders vanish on open chains and on bipartite rings: conjugation by (-1)^(number of excited tokens on alternating cells) sends V to -V and is identity on the code. On an odd ring this is only a local-order statement below a process winding around the ring. An extensive isolated-code-band argument is not a thermodynamic proof.

[Proved] Distances impose another cost beyond alpha_n. At large d/t, with d_R=n-i and d_L=j,

    f_s(i,tau) = t^(2|s-i|) tau^(2|s-i|+1)
                 exp(-d tau)/(2|s-i|+1)! + higher powers of t,

so

    F_ij = t^(2(d_R+d_L)) (2d_R+2d_L+2)!
        /[(2d_R+1)!(2d_L+1)!(2d)^(2d_R+2d_L+3)]
        + higher powers of t.                             (21)

Thus weak channels can be much smaller than the bus gap, and in general shrink exponentially with spatial distance. Taking lambda small relative to the gap alone does not control errors relative to those weak channels. Nothing in (19)-(20) cures a wrong leading hopping matrix.

[Source-reported, conditional infrared input] To use ir_control, a repaired design would have to give all intended simple Fermi crossings, minimum velocity v_min>0, and finite quadratic and quartic mismatch margins delta_2,delta_4. In logical energy units, a sufficient perturbative requirement has the form

    epsilon_n << v_min delta_2,
    epsilon_n log(1/delta_4) << 1,

together with positive Gaussian stiffness and small shifts of the Fermi points. Exact parity is present here, and exact U(1) removes the pairing warning in routeA_numerics. The remaining non-Gaussian conversion channels still need detuning or an exact symmetry. These are standard IR conditions from the supplied notes, not a new lattice-CFT theorem.

[Source-reported, conditional mobile input] C20 additionally requires the complete allowed vertex lattice and its length-fluid mismatch

    q(m,a,b)=-2pi m nu + sum_alpha(b_alpha theta_alpha-a_alpha k_alpha).

Near the free metric only squared-norm shells 4 and 8 need be excluded. Its candidate count is

    M(n)=1+8n+32 binom(n,2)+96 binom(n,3)+128 binom(n,4),

with an available normalized mismatch margin 1/[4M(n)] of order n^-4. Momentum shifts below that margin divided by 16 preserve a positive margin. The Gaussian metric must remain positive, with the neighborhood in C20 bounded by less than 1/3. Physical translation and gluing must still be supplied for a microscopic mobile implementation.

[Proved, negative conclusion for this candidate] The n=2 bus has no reference Fermi crossings at its stated field. Its uniform phases also leave a common-center resonance in any putative independent-flavor version. Therefore it cannot be placed in the requested n-flavor stable region merely by making lambda and g smaller. Subleading g^4 and lambda^4 terms are parametrically smaller than the n=2 leading gap in the controlled regime. They do not rescue that reference point. Larger couplings or modified rails define a different, unverified problem.

[Scope] The frozen selector has no length fluid. Lengths b and b+1 in this unmodified architecture store n and n+1 data bits, so they are not the equal-payload mobile pair required by C8/C20. A padding or transport construction would be needed in addition to repairing the exchange. No mobile scale hierarchy is asserted for this cell.

## 7. Exact diagonalization and consistency checks

### Algebra and source resolvents

### Finite-lambda full-cell ED

[Numerical, method] verify_bus.py builds the full product of legal cells, without discarding excited vacuum-token modes. It splits by the exact total charge and obtains every one of the 2^(n M) low code levels, plus the next eigenvalue in each relevant charge sector. QR and Rayleigh-Ritz steps handle near-degenerate eigenvectors. The polar factor of the overlap with the original code gives the canonical effective Hamiltonian. It is compared with the full-in-g expression (12), not only its g^2 truncation. These are fixed frozen-marker-sector calculations, not ED of all 17^L ring states. Charge sectors outside 0,...,n M contain at least one excited token. Their energies are bounded below by Delta_bare-|g|-|lambda|sqrt(3)(M-1), which exceeds the next levels in the table, so they cannot conceal an omitted low eigenvalue.

[Numerical] Parameters are t=1, d=4, g=0.2, phi=pi/2 and rail phases (0.41,0.31,-0.07). Delta_bare is chosen as 4-2cos(pi/(n+2)) so that d=4 after subtracting the vacuum ground energy. Selector and marker scalar energies are also subtracted. At lambda=0.08:

[Scope] These finite-cluster couplings resolve the very small logical bandwidth. They are not certified to preserve the globally selected marker period at those same numerical values. The conservative selector hierarchy in (20) calls for smaller couplings. Marker positions are exactly conserved, and the ED intentionally verifies the fixed-sector exchange.

[Proved and numerical, consistency checks] The code degeneracy follows from the grammar and vacuum path, and matches ED. Right-port parity follows from the explicit Z-string gauge and matches the ungauged matrix. Formula (12) is Hermitian and negative semidefinite before subtraction of its scalar. The g=0 result is scalar. Distinct bonds have zero second-order cross terms. The power lambda^2 g^2 T in (16) has the correct energy units. Halving g and lambda tests the predicted next powers. None of these checks establishes c=n.

## 8. Files, reproduction, and the remaining problem

[Reproduction] All scripts use Python with NumPy and SciPy only. Run from this folder with one BLAS thread:

    OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 literal_bond.py
    OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 structural_checks.py
    OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 verify_bus.py --algebra
    OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 verify_bus.py --n 1 --cells 2 --lam .08
    OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 verify_bus.py --n 1 --cells 3 --lam .08
    OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 verify_bus.py --n 2 --cells 2 --lam .08
    OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 verify_bus.py --n 2 --cells 3 --lam .08

[File map] literal_bond.py builds the 17-state microscopic bond. spatial_bus.py contains the excited-sector Hamiltonian, exact resolvent, exact g^2 coefficient, and complete walker cell. structural_checks.py checks local grammar, the gauge, and full second-order clusters. verify_bus.py performs the heat-kernel and finite-coupling checks. The matching JSON files record numerical outputs. bus_diagnostics.json contains earlier exploratory parameters and is not the final verification table.

[Proved, final assessment] Spatial storage, an exact degenerate code, exact parity, a small repeated alphabet, and enough excitation poles can coexist without a controller. What is still missing is a local spatial mechanism producing the required operator-valued spectral weights. In the tested looped bus, locality concentrates the exchange on opposite endpoint flavors, while a parity cancellation fixes its matrix zeros and uniform phases leave a common reflection center. The obstruction is already visible in the leading second-order exchange. Higher orders are not a universal no-go, but they cannot repair this example while remaining perturbatively smaller than its leading n=2 gap.

[Open] The most concrete next changes are (i) additional loop species or local boundary structures that break the common phase relation without introducing uncontrolled fields, (ii) a spatial filter making the left and right spectral readouts yield normal, full-rank hopping matrices, and (iii) only after that, fixed-payload padding for mobile cells. None is supplied here. A universal obstruction to computation-free route A has not been proved.
