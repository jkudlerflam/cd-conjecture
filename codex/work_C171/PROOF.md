# C171 derivations behind REFEREE.md

Conventions of C159. V_{p,n} = e^{i(pθ + 2nφ)} has dimension p²/(4K) + n²K and conformal spin pn. The T host is the half-floating boson with T1: φ → φ + π/2, θ → θ + π + α (α generic), and Θ: φ → −φ + π/2, θ → θ + π.

## 1. Counting invariant Hermitian couplings

[proved, linear algebra] Let G be generated by a unitary automorphism T1 and an antiunitary involution A of the operator algebra, with [T1, A] = 0. Restrict to a finite set of operators closed under T1, A and †. The invariant Hermitian couplings form a real space whose dimension equals the complex dimension of the joint +1 eigenspace of T1 and L = † ∘ A. Both are complex-linear. The reason is as follows. W = ker(T1 − 1) is a complex subspace preserved by A and †. On W, A and † are commuting antilinear involutions, and {w : A w = w = w†} is the +1 eigenspace of the linear involution †A on W, a real form of it. For a boson with A(θ) = t_θ θ + d and A(φ) = t_φ φ + c,

    L V_{p,n} = e^{i(pd + 2nc)} V_{t_θ p, t_φ n},      T1 V_{p,n} = e^{i(pb + 2na)} V_{sp, sn},      (1.1)

with s = −1 when T1 acts by charge conjugation. opcount.py builds these matrices on all products of relevant operators, including currents and discrete-sector primaries, and returns the null space of [T1 − 1; L − 1]. Spin-0 invariants are the tunings, as in C154 4.1. Spin-1 invariants are listed separately.

## 2. Potts × half-floating boson

[derived] Let T1 act on a three-state Potts sector as the Z3 generator ω. Commutation with Θ gives Θ σ Θ⁻¹ ∝ σ†, because T1 (ΘσΘ⁻¹) T1⁻¹ = ω* ΘσΘ⁻¹. The relevant Potts primaries are ε (4/5, Z3-neutral), σ, σ† (2/15) and Z, Z† (4/3), the last four with charge ±1. A product O_Potts × V_{p,n} is T1-invariant only if ±2π/3 + p(π + α) + nπ = 0 mod 2π. For p = 0 this is impossible. For p ≠ 0 it fixes α, a resonance surface. So at generic α the only invariant relevant operator is ε, and k = 1. It is Θ-even, since it is an S_3 singlet with a real OPE coefficient in σ × σ†. The boson stays half-floating, and the Potts sector is anomaly-free, so LSM is satisfied by the boson.

On the ordered side ⟨σ⟩ ≠ 0 carries momentum 2π/3, it cannot couple to the boson for the same reason, and the neighbouring phase is a c = 1 liquid with threefold period-3 symmetry breaking. opcount.out case S3 gives k = 1 at K = 0.6 to 3.0.

## 3. A boson on which translation acts by charge conjugation

[derived] Take T1 = C ∘ shift on boson 2, so T1 reflects each plane of Hermitian combinations of V_{±(p,n)}. Θ either reflects the plane (p, 0), with p even or odd, or rotates the plane (0, n) by 2nc. Commuting with a reflection forces that rotation to be by 0 or π. Commuting reflections are equal or opposite. Equal reflections have a common invariant line and opposite ones have none. At p = 2 (and n = 2) the doubled angles always make the two equal. So cos 2θ2-type (1/K2) and cos 4φ2-type (4K2) couplings are always allowed, and at least one of them is relevant.

The T1-odd line of each plane is a Θ eigenvector, and it pairs with whichever of cos 2φ1, sin 2φ1 (both at momentum π) has the matching Θ parity. The resulting dimensions are K1 + 1/(4K2) and K1 + K2. Codimension 1 therefore needs K1 > 2 − min(K2, 1/(4K2)) ≥ 3/2, with the bound approached at K2 = 1/2. opcount.out case S4, which minimizes over every admissible shift and Θ action, agrees: k = 1 first appears at K1 = 1.6 for K2 = 0.6, only from K1 = 1.85 (K2 = 1.0) or 1.9 (K2 = 0.1, 1.9), and never up to K1 = 1.9 at K2 = 2.5.

## 4. Θ exchanging two bosons

[derived] Θ: φ1 → −φ2, φ2 → −φ1, θ1 → θ2, θ2 → θ1 preserves the canonical commutators, given antilinearity. Commutation with T1 forces a1 = a2 and b2 = −b1, with a and b otherwise free. Both bosons are then fully floating, not half-floating, and Θ forces K1 = K2 = K. The invariant vertices are e^{±2i(φ1 − φ2)} (dimension 2K) and e^{±i(θ1 + θ2)} (dimension 1/(2K)). Θ fixes the fields φ1 − φ2 and θ1 + θ2, so both the cos and the sin of each are invariant, two real couplings each. One of the two pairs is always relevant, so k ≥ 2.

## 5. Lemma L of C163

[derived; numerical lemmaL_check.out] In the setting of C163, boson 2 is half-floating with V_c2 at π, and its θ2 shift is n(π + α) + δ. The candidate lockers and their dimensions are as follows.
- δ = 0: O_conv = e^{i(θ2 − nθ1)}, of dimension n²/(4K1) + 1/(4K2), is invariant. It is relevant for n = 2 at every K1 > 1 and K2 > 1/(4(2 − 1/K1)). For n = 3 it is irrelevant at K1 = 1.2, and for n = 4 up to K1 = 1.8.
- δ = π: O_conv carries momentum π. O_conv cos 2φ1 has dimension n²/(4K1) + 1/(4K2) + K1 > 2 for n ≥ 2. O_conv² has dimension n²/K1 + 1/K2, which for n = 2 exceeds 2 whenever K1 ≤ 2. O_conv e^{2iφ2} is momentum-allowed but has spin pn = 1. The scan finds no spin-0 invariant relevant operator at all, for n = 2 and every (K1, K2) tested from (1.2, 0.8) to (2.5, 2.0), and none for n = 1 at (1.2, 0.8).
So two bosons whose wavevectors lie in Zα + Zπ are kinematically stable at codimension 0 in these ranges.

## 6. Repulsive host with a θ-commensurate second boson

[derived; numerical S5] If the second boson's density is Θ-even, Lemma A makes Θ reverse θ2. Its commensurate vertex is then e^{iθ2} at momentum π, with x2 = 1/(4K2). The invariants are e^{2iθ2} (1/K2, needing K2 < 1/2), the umklapps, and V_c1 e^{±iθ2} (K1 + 1/(4K2)). At K1 = 0.9 and K2 = 0.2, and at K1 = 0.7 and K2 = 0.15, opcount.py finds k = 0. At K2 = 0.3 (respectively 0.2) the cross term becomes relevant and k = 2. This matches the x-language row x1 + x2 > 2 of C159, with a repulsive host.
