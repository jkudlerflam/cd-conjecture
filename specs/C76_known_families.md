# C76: Simple counterexamples outside route A
Your folder is `../work_C76/`. Read `../../specs/_simplest_background.md` first. Task: look for a much simpler mechanism, at small d (3 to 12), than spontaneous cells. Survey and analyse (i) integrable nearest-neighbour chains with several gapless sectors (graded SU(N|M) and OSp chains, Hubbard-type and t–J-type chains at generic filling where spin and charge both stay gapless, spin-S chains with several Bethe seas, multi-component Fermi gases on a lattice, Haldane–Shastry-type models are excluded if not nearest-neighbour), computing c against d for each and identifying the largest ratio; (ii) multi-band nearest-neighbour fermion models with interactions, where the free-fermion bound (C1) is saturated or exceeded once interactions open extra channels; (iii) nearest-neighbour chains whose effective theory has several incommensurate Luttinger liquids from distinct particle species with local hard-core constraints (each site holding at most one particle of one of several species, so d = species + 1, with species-dependent hopping), where c can equal the number of species with gapless densities and spin-charge separation may add sectors. For (iii) in particular decide whether a site with q species and a hole (d = q + 1) can carry c = q + 1 or more, which would exceed d − 1. Give explicit Hamiltonians, the c count with mechanism, and small exact diagonalization checks. Report the best c − (d − 1) found and its status.
