## Background (everything you need)

A "cell gadget" is the following finite-dimensional object. A cell Hilbert space contains a q-dimensional port p, and a ground isometry G : C^N -> H_cell (the "code"). Let F_a, a = 1..q^2-1, be traceless Hermitian port operators with tr(F_a F_b) = q delta_ab. A cell Hamiltonian H_A >= 0 has kernel exactly ran G and gap Delta. Two cells (A on the left, B on the right) couple through lambda v, v = sum_a F_a^(A,right port) tensor F_a^(B,left port). If G^dag F_a G = 0 for all a, the first-order effective Hamiltonian vanishes and the second-order one is

  K2 = - sum_{e,f} [1/(E_e + E_f)] sum_{a,b} M^A_e[a,b] tensor M^B_f[a,b],   M_e[a,b] = G^dag F_a P_e F_b G,

where P_e are the excited spectral projectors of H_A with energies E_e. Equivalently K2 = - int_0^inf R^A(t) tensor R^B(t) dt (indices contracted), with the matrix-valued boundary function R_ab(t) = G^dag F_a e^{-t H_A} F_b G, or the boundary resolvent R_ab(z) = G^dag F_a (H_A + z)^{-1} F_b G. Known result (take as given): by choosing H_A suitably (an arbitrary matrix on a cell of dimension poly(N)), K2 can be made equal to a scalar plus any prescribed Hermitian logical bond, e.g. alpha*1 + beta*P_N (P_N the swap on C^N tensor C^N), for every N, with a qubit port. Example code: G|i> = |Omega>_{p,s} tensor |i>_logical tensor |0>_aux, where |Omega> = q^{-1/2} sum_k |k>_p |k>_s is maximally entangled between the port p and a "partner" q-dimensional register s. Then G^dag F_a G = 0 and the "syndrome" isometries S_a = (F_a tensor 1)G are orthonormal; H_A is designed on the syndrome-times-logical-times-aux space.

The open problem is to realize such a gadget inside a translation-invariant nearest-neighbour chain of FIXED local dimension. The standard tool for putting complex, N-dependent structure into a fixed-dimension chain is a Feynman-Kitaev history-state construction (a clock, a computation, penalties). The worry, from an earlier analysis, is that a history state dilutes boundary matrix elements: if the circuit acts on the port during T_act of the T+1 clock times, the port compression W^dag F W is the time average of U_t^dag F U_t and is off by up to 2||F|| T_act/(T+1), and spectral matching alone does not preserve R(z).

## The idea to analyse: port-idle cells

Abstract Feynman-Kitaev model. Input space H_in = C^q_p tensor C^q_s tensor H_w (port, partner, workspace; the workspace contains the logical register, aux register and ancillas). Clock space C^{T+1} with basis |t>. Gates U_1..U_T act on H_w ONLY (never on p or s); W_t = U_t...U_1, W_0 = 1. Ancilla initialization: H_init = J_c |0><0|_clock tensor Pi_bad, Pi_bad a projector on H_w onto non-initialized ancillas. Propagation:

  H_prop = (J_c/2) sum_{t=1}^T [ |t><t| + |t-1><t-1| ] tensor 1 - |t><t-1| tensor U_t - |t-1><t| tensor U_t^dag.

Penalties: H_pen = sum_t |t><t| tensor (T+1) h_t, where each h_t acts on (p, s, and one designated workspace qubit/qudit c), i.e. the port appears only in ENERGY terms, never in gates. The leading effective Hamiltonian on the input space is H_eff = sum_t W_t^dag h_t W_t (restricted to initialized ancillas).
