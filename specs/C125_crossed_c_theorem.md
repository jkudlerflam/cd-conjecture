## Continuum input (from a parallel project; physics-level rigor, not refereed)

For a unitary Lorentz-invariant QFT on a circle assume finite heat traces, Euclidean rotation invariance of the twisted torus Tr_L exp(-bH + i theta P), the spectrum condition H - E_0 >= |P - P_0| on every circle, and a unique gapped ground state on every circle. Write b = r cos(phi), theta = r sin(phi) at fixed (L, r). Both sides of the real-angle rotation identity Tr_L exp(-r cos(phi) H + i r sin(phi) P) = Tr_r exp(-L(cos(phi) H + i sin(phi) P)) are analytic on the strip |Re phi| < pi/2 (the spectrum condition gives domination by a heat trace at inverse temperature r cos(Re phi) exp(-|Im phi|)), so at phi = i eta, with b = r cosh(eta), a = r sinh(eta),

    Tr_L exp(-bH - aP) = Tr_r exp(-(L/r)(bH - aP)),   r^2 = b^2 - a^2.

Ground-state dominance gives F(b, a) = lim (1/L) log Tr_L exp(-bH - aP) = b p(r) with p(r) = -E_0(r)/r. F is jointly convex (log-Laplace transform of the positive joint spectral measure of H and P). Its Hessian at the point boosted by rapidity eta from the rest point is T^2 [[cosh(eta) C_V, sinh(eta) s], [sinh(eta) s, cosh(eta) s]], positive exactly when C_V >= tanh^2(eta) s. The null limit eta -> infinity gives C_V >= s, equivalently Theta(q) = -L E_0(L) convex in q = L^2, hence c(L) = (6L/pi)(e_0 L - E_0(L)) nonincreasing and c_UV >= c_IR. At eta = 0 one gets only C_V >= 0 and s >= 0. Rotation invariance enters in exactly one place: it fixes the tilted free energy F in terms of its untilted slice p, so that convexity at the null edge constrains p.

## Lattice reduction (part of your task is to verify or correct it)

For a chain whose Trotterized Euclidean path integral is reflection positive across bond-centred planes (Dyson, Lieb and Simon), Z(L, beta) = Tr exp(-beta H_L) = Tr T_beta^L on even rings, with a positive spatial transfer operator T_beta (the quantum transfer matrix, acting on the Trotter column, independent of L). Then:

1. R_L(beta) = log[Z(L, beta)^2 / Z(2L, beta)] = S_2(T_beta^L / Tr T_beta^L) exactly, the Renyi-2 entropy of a crossed Gibbs state on the Trotter circle of length beta at crossed inverse temperature L. In the continuum T_beta = exp(-H_beta) and this is the same identity.
2. Renyi entropies of Gibbs states do not increase with inverse temperature (dS_2/dL = 2(<eps>_{2L} - <eps>_L) <= 0), so R_L is nonincreasing in L at every beta, and as beta -> infinity the doubling defect D(L) = E_{2L} - 2E_L is nonincreasing in L.
3. T_beta >= 0 defines E(L) = -lim_{beta -> infinity} beta^{-1} log Tr T_beta^L for real L, interpolating the ring energies. The ratio bound R_L <= K(d) + k M f(d) beta/L (hence A <= (4k/pi) M f(d), C116) is a crossed-channel entropic c-function bound, L R_L/beta bounded. In the regime beta >> L it follows from Theta(q) = -L E(L) convex in q = L^2 for L >= L_0 with L_0 <= g(d), which gives A <= c_A(L_0) <= (12/pi) L_0^2 M.
4. Numerical: on the exact Bethe energies of the SU(N) Sym^2 chains (`../../contrib/casimir_survey/astra_bethe/bethe_exact_energies.json`, N = 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, rings L = N to 120 in steps of N, normalization h = sum_j H_{k-j} P_j, ||h|| = H_k/2, Bethe e_0 as in `../../contrib/casimir_survey/bethe_refit.py`), c_A(L) = (6L/pi)(e_0 L - E_0(L)) is nonincreasing and Theta is discretely convex in L^2 at every length, including L = N where E_0 = 0.
