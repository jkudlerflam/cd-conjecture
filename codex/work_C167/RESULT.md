## Verdict

(A_W) is not derived in full, and no violating bond was found. It is reduced to a strictly narrower residual (A_W1). The harmonics of the spiral parent split by U(1)_m charge, and a bond with no charge-1 part is exactly a parity-class bond. An expansion in the charge-1 part alone, around a parity-class liquid with charge-2 of any size, keeps the infrared one boson (Proposition R). What remains is the regime where the charge-1 harmonic (Gamma-type exchange in T, the in-plane field in K_I) is comparable to the exchange at a twist of order one. The one window point that read c above 1 at L = 256, hi_K_1, is a gapped period-2 state reached through the boson's own KT instability, not an Ising × boson point.

## Claims

1. Lemma R0 [proved; numerical check 1.2e-13]. Write the polar factor S = S_0 + S_1 + S_2 by charge about m, and the field f = f_m + f_1. The charge-1 parts S_1, f_1 are exactly the parts odd under the pi rotation about m. A T bond with S_1 = 0 is XYZ + D_m (parity cap T). On K_I, S_1 = 0 always and the charge-1 harmonic is the in-plane field alone. In both strata the charge-1 amplitude is 2 of the 5 dimensions.
2. Proposition R [derived; inputs C152 G1 at the base, Pi-parity and momentum bookkeeping, multiscale RG]. To every order in the charge-1 harmonic, around a parity-class liquid with any charge-2 harmonic, the infrared is one boson with renormalized (K, v, alpha). The phases are the liquid, KT-ordered period-2 states, clock-locked tongues and their boundaries, all with c <= 1. So both cases of (A_W) are excluded for small charge-1 and any charge-2. That part of (A_W) now rests on A4 (c <= 1 on the parity class), which the C159 theorem already assumes. C163 Proposition P is the special case of small charge-2.
3. Lemma C [derived; the constant C_0 not computed]. The charge-1 harmonic couples to the boson's own e^{i vartheta} at the twist theta_F, and the liquid survives only for g < C_0 v (K theta~)^{2 - 1/(4K)}, where theta~ is the distance of theta_F from 2 pi Z (Dzyaloshinskii's theta^2 law at K -> infinity). A liquid carrying a lattice-size charge-1 harmonic therefore needs theta~ of order one. Near theta_F = pi the resonant vertex e^{i vartheta} cos 2 phi is irrelevant for K > 1.866, so there the charge-1 harmonic does not lock the liquid at first order.
4. Lemma Θ2, case (b) [derived]. The antiunitary pairs any composite minimum P* with -P*, the only emergent charge is Q = N_+ - N_-, and case (b) yields an open c = 2 phase of one of two kinds. Either the antiunitary is unbroken, the second boson has e^{2 i phi_2} at momentum zero and needs K_2 > 2, so a local operator at +-P* outside Lambda has quasi-long-range order with eta_2 < 1/4. Or the antiunitary is broken and the phase has a spontaneous uniform magnetization along m at zero field.
5. Lemma O, case (a) [derived]. The ordered side of an independent staggered Ising mode carries a staggered m-moment (antiunitary-odd) or dimerization (even). The test that separates it from the boson's own ordering is the dimension x_pi of the leading momentum-pi operator. It equals K in the liquid and reaches 1/2 at the boson's KT, while it drops to 1/8 next to an independent Ising surface.
6. Negative results [derived or proved]. Lead 3 (the antiunitary forcing a mixing) gives nothing beyond C159, since sigma_I V_c has dimension K + 1/8 and there is no Kramers pairing at momentum pi. The Perron-Frobenius route covers only theta in {0, pi} with no charge-1 part, in the spiral-axis basis. Concavity of E_0 in the harmonic amplitude bounds energies from above only. The C165 SU(2)_1 mechanism has no analogue, since the collinear Neel-m product state costs |s_m| per bond for any harmonics and the parent's nearest special point is h_F.
7. hi_K_1 [numerical, two- and four-site VUMPS, chi <= 96, local]. This is the K_I point with field fraction 1.02, charge-2 fraction 0.77, parent Delta = -0.154 and ring K_S = 2.29, whose open-chain c_prof was 1.40, 1.37, 1.26 and 1.45 at L = 32 to 256. Edge pinning along the order flattens the L = 64 profile at the VUMPS entropy (c_prof 0.62 against 1.37 unpinned).
8. hi_K_1 path [numerical]. The harmonics were scaled by lam. For lam <= 0.98 the chain is a c = 1 liquid, and the staggered moment decays as xi^{-x_pi}. At small lam x_pi = 0.92 to 1.13, matching the parent's K = 1.06. It then falls continuously, to 0.98, 0.81, 0.72, 0.62, 0.56 and 0.47 at lam = 0.9 to 0.98, and the order sets in once it crosses 1/2. ## Residual

(A_W1) [assumed]. Take a half-floating liquid of T or K_I with commensurate-vertex dimension x = K > 1, a charge-1 harmonic comparable to the exchange (fraction of about 0.3 or more), and charge-1 twist theta~ of order one. Then no lattice-scale branch outside Lambda reaches zero energy while the liquid survives. For case (a) (K > 15/8), x_pi stays equal to K, with no surface where it drops to 1/8. For case (b) (K > 1), no open c = 2 phase of type b-sym (eta_2 < 1/4 at +-P* outside Lambda) or b-brk (spontaneous m-magnetization) exists.

Where it bites: parents with -1 < Delta < -0.67 (a) or -1 < Delta < 0 (b), a large charge-1 harmonic (Gamma-type S_{m perp} in T, an in-plane field below the polarization field in K_I) at theta~ of order one, any charge-2, away from h_F and gamma. The method stops there because there is no small parameter (|h_1| is at the lattice gap, and 1/K is not small), no extra symmetry, no exact solution, and no hidden Heisenberg point to play the role it plays in C165.

Practical lesson. Window membership must be read from x_pi on the infinite chain, not from the parent's Delta or from the ring K_S. At large harmonics the boson's K can move far from the parent's, as on the hi_K_1 path, where it went from 1.06 to 1/2.
