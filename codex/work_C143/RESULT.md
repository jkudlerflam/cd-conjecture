## Verdict

[proved] C141's list of three singular strata is not complete as stated. One new singular point and three corrections.

1. New: the XZ point h = X (x) Z (with its on-site rotations and its reflection Z (x) X), the uniform form of the isotropic quantum compass chain. Its spectrum is a critical Majorana chain (c = 1/2, z = 1) times an exactly degenerate manifold of dimension 2^(L/2 - 1) on even rings (2^((L+1)/2) on odd rings). It is singular by the Task 1 criterion, but it is neither of the two structural types C141 assumes: the degenerate manifold is not gapped from the rest, and the conformal part is not empty. So C141's emergence lemma (5.3) and its non-coexistence statement (6.2) do not apply there as written.
2. Stratum 2 (frustration-free gapless) is two-dimensional in B_2, not the one-parameter twisted FM Heisenberg family. By Bravyi-Gosset it is every rank-one psi with |mu_1| = |mu_2| != 0, plus a rank-two saturation family. It includes the XYZ bonds with J_x = -J_y at the Kurmann-Thomas-Muller field, which conserve sum (-1)^j Z_j (a staggered U(1) class outside C129 to C140). All of it has z = 2 (Gosset-Mozgunov).
3. Stratum 3 is not a set of isolated Lifshitz points. Band-onset (Pokrovsky-Talapov) transitions form codimension-one hypersurfaces in every symmetry stratum, including the symmetry-free one, whenever the gap minimum is at an incommensurate momentum. On the free locus they are explicit (PT1: a^2 = g^2 with B^2 < c^2; PT2: B^2 = a^2 - g^2 + c^2), with a one-sided z = 3 curve where they meet the Ising surfaces and no quartic onset anywhere. In the U(1) class the lower critical field h_c1 (Delta > 1) belongs here too.
4. The XYZ points "where the factorized ground state becomes degenerate" are exactly the J_x = -J_y curve of item 2. The generic Kurmann-Thomas-Muller surface is a gapped rank-two frustration-free surface with two product ground states (regular).

[proved] Isolation of the extensive points. A strictly local integral of motion of any range forces rank J <= 1. At range two such operators exist only at classical bonds and at the XZ point. The free locus has a flat band only at XZ, and frustration-free bonds have an extensive ground space only at the Fibonacci point. So exact extensive degeneracy produced by local symmetries or by frustration-free constraints occurs in B_2 only at the Fibonacci points and at XZ.

[proved] Completeness of the amended list holds on: classical bonds; the free locus F (all singular points listed, with their z); all frustration-free bonds; and every extensive degeneracy produced by range-two local IOMs (any range if rank J >= 2). It also holds on the uniform U(1) class, proved in the literature modulo standard Bethe-ansatz completeness. On the staggered U(1) class it is proved on the Bravyi-Gosset curve and derived (bosonization) elsewhere. [heuristic] In the residual strata completeness rests on the statement that every z > 1 point is a single-band onset or a junction of such onsets with z = 1 lines. An "open dense set" version is true but empty, since the singular set itself has codimension exactly one.

## Numerical search (Task 3)

- Criterion: the low-state count N(kappa, L) of states within kappa M/L, or its smooth version S_L. Regular means bounded in L, singular means unbounded. The indicator is g = min over consecutive even L of log S_{L+2} - log S_L. A single-increment indicator is fooled by level crossings in incommensurate phases (7 of 9 version-1 landings had a fourfold accident at L = 12 only). Calibration (L = 8, 10, 12, kappa = 3): Fibonacci 0.96, XZ 0.69, Bravyi-Gosset psi_p 0.47, FM Heisenberg 0.33, XX saturation 0.12, gapped Neel 0.04, CFTs about 0.
- Screens: 900 random bonds locally and 2880 on Typhon at L = 8, 10, 12. g quantiles (50/90/99 percent, max) were -0.04/0.06/0.21/0.28 locally and -0.04/0.06/0.21/0.35 on Typhon. No open region behaves singularly, and no random bond hits an isolated extensive point, as expected for codimension 8.
- Local refinement (12 best screened bonds): g = 0.26 to 0.55. None is classical, frustration free, U(1) or near XZ, and none has an exact degeneracy. All twelve are strongly chiral (|D| of 0.70 to 1.10 at unit norm), where incommensurate onsets are generic. The strongest, point 3, sits next to a Pokrovsky-Talapov surface. Its gapped side has a quasiparticle minimum at k/pi of about 1.3, closing as 0.107, 0.055, 0.025, 0.010 at s = 0.2, 0.15, 0.1, 0.05, identical at L = 12 and 14. The other side is a dense incommensurate liquid. Point 4 first sits next to an ordering transition. Continued maximization carries it toward a nearly rank-one, nearly orthogonal J, with n(<M/L) = 8, 16, 32 at L = 8, 10, 12. Minimizing the width of the lowest 2^(L/2 - 1) states from there converges onto the XZ orbit (width 3e-4, J singular values 0.9975, 0.04, 0.03, |B|/s_1 = 0.05). So the unbiased search rediscovered the new XZ stratum from a random start and found no other zero-width point.
- Family scans confirm the exact predictions. Staggered U(1): every point with g > 0.12 is within one grid step of the Bravyi-Gosset curve B = 2 sqrt(J_z^2 - g^2) or of the FM point (peaks at B = 1.000, 1.375, 1.625, 1.875, 2.000, 2.000 against 0.948, 1.379, 1.664, 1.854, 1.964, 2.000). Uniform U(1): a ridge just below the saturation field and the Fibonacci peak. h_c1 is not resolved at L <= 12.
- Limits: z = 2 onsets give only g of about 0.1 to 0.5 at these sizes, comparable to incommensurate finite-size noise, so the search locates onset surfaces only roughly and cannot certify z. It found nothing outside the amended list.

## Local analysis at the new stratum, and C141's single-species claims (Task 4)

- PT hypersurfaces and h_c1 [derived]: C141 5.4 applies unchanged. There is one species per band minimum, c = 1 (floating or chiral liquid) or 0, v ~ M (delta/M)^(1/2), and no fast sector with a long crossover.
- XZ point [proved structure]: a_j = X_j Z_{j+1} and b_j = Z_j X_{j+1} generate two commuting Majorana chains, and Y_j Y_{j+1} = a_j b_j (verified to machine precision). The family h = XZ + alpha ZX + beta YY is exactly H = sum (a_j + alpha b_j + beta a_j b_j), a self-dual Ashkin-Teller chain with velocity ratio alpha. Only XY and YZ (infinitesimal rotations) preserve the degeneracy; YY and ZX split it at first order, the rest at second order. [proved at first order] The emergent model on the manifold is (alpha + beta <a>) sum b_j with <a> = E_0/L -> -2/pi. The check is that the ratio of the first-order YY and ZX splittings, 0.653, equals |E_0/L| = 0.6533 at L = 8. So v_em = O(delta M), c_em = 1/2 and c_tot = 1 at generic directions. [numerical] c estimates 0.98 to 1.01 at (alpha, beta) = (1, 0), (1, 0.5), (0.3, 0), (0, +-0.3).
- XZ tuned cone [open]: on alpha + beta <a> = 0 the first-order hopping cancels. The emergent B chain is then a strongly interacting Majorana chain with power-law mediated couplings, of the Rahmani-Zhu-Franz-Affleck type, a class that contains tricritical Ising (c = 7/10) and an incommensurate c = 3/2 phase. If the cone reached one of them, a nearest-neighbour qubit bond would have c = 6/5 or 2. ED at L <= 16 shows large, parity-oscillating entropies with decreasing c estimates (about 2.5 down to 1.1 to 1.5), with the excess close to log 2 at small coupling. That is consistent with a B chain still inside its nearly degenerate regime. Not converged, and inconclusive.
- C141's checks: (i) the cluster (n - 1)V argument is right for nearest-neighbour attraction and is confirmed in U(1) by Bethe ansatz (single real-root sea). (ii) p-wave pairing at a z = 2 onset has dimension 2 < 3 and is relevant, giving a gap or an Ising line with v proportional to the pairing. At incommensurate onsets it is momentum-forbidden. (iii) PXP at Fibonacci: the Fendley-Sengupta-Sachdev phases (Ising, tricritical Ising, Potts, floating), all with c <= 1. (iv) The frustration-free stratum has a single magnon band. All of C141's single-species claims hold at the old strata. The non-coexistence claim fails at XZ, where c_tot = 1/2 + c_B.

## Conditional derivation of C(2) < infinity (Task 5)

C(2)_asymptotic = sup A/M < infinity follows (compactness of B_2 plus local boundedness everywhere) from the following assumptions.

- (IR) [assumed] Each bond has finitely many CFT sectors or is gapped.
- (LR) [proved, C109] v_a <= 4eM.
- (WCT) [assumed] Weak-perturbation multi-velocity c-theorem at regular bonds.
- (COMP) [proved on classical, F, FF and local-IOM degeneracies; literature on uniform U(1); partly proved on staggered U(1); heuristic in residual strata] Every singular bond is in the amended list: Fibonacci points, the Bravyi-Gosset gapless set, band-onset hypersurfaces with their junctions, and the XZ point.
- (EM-G) [derived, C141 5.3] At Fibonacci, v_em <= C delta M.
- (EM-Z) [derived] At onsets and frustration-free points, one species per band minimum with v_em ~ M (delta/M)^(1/z).
- (EM-XZ) [derived at first order, open on the tuned cone] At XZ, v_B = O(delta M) (O(delta^2 M) on the cone) and c_B is bounded uniformly in the direction of approach.
- (NEST) [heuristic] Finite nesting depth, now including the Majorana-chain recursion at XZ.

The sharper C141 statement (c_tot <= 1, A <= 4eM at d = 2) needs in addition (NC) [open]: c_B <= 1/2 on the XZ tuned cone. It also needs the absence of c > 1 at regular bonds [numerical, REPORT 9.37]. The finite-size C(2) additionally needs C131's uniform remainder lemmas.
