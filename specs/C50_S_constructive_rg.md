# C50: The constructive RG half of S
Read `../../specs/_S_background.md` first. Route: assume the output of C48 or C49 (a logical multi-flavour fermion chain with exact U(1), distinct velocities, the non-resonance margins of C39 and C42, and quasi-local corrections with stated decay and size) and prove, by extending Mastropietro–Porta (2108.13708) and Porta–Scola–Singh (2509.08665) to this setting (many flavours, extremely separated velocities, general even interactions), that the theory is a multi-channel Luttinger liquid with n + 1 gapless modes: power-law correlations with the right exponents and a vanishing beta function, uniformly as needed. Identify exactly which hypotheses of those theorems hold, which must be extended, and prove the extensions.
