## 3. Search design (search.py, crit.py, refine.py, typhon_c155.slurm)

- Parametrization: the principal-frame 9-vector v on S^8 (contrib/d2_grid convention), Nelder-Mead in a local chart v = normalize(v0 + E y) with E an orthonormal basis of the tangent space (8 directions, or the tangent space of a symmetry stratum Z2, TR, RA when confined).
- Stage A: uniform sample of S^8 (48 x 250 points) for the volume fraction of gapless points and the distribution of c_obj.
- Stage B (gap closings first): minimize crit from random starts (uniform and strata, confined and not), then at the minimum the full estimate, labels (uniform or staggered U(1) charge by solving the commutator equation modulo telescoping terms, free-fermion locus, discrete symmetries), a ball test (12 random points at geodesic radius 0.03 and 12 at 0.08, gapless fraction and max c_obj), and a slide along the critical set maximizing c_obj - 2 crit.
- Stage C (c maximization): restarts with Nelder-Mead (300 evaluations, then a local restart with 150) on c_obj = min(c_S, c_cas) (three quarters) or min(c_S, c_fit) at nondegenerate points (one quarter). Seeds: uniform, strata (free and confined), and 32 known points (XXZ family, Heisenberg AF and FM, saturation fields, Ising lines, the XY multicritical end point, XZ and XZ + YY, the staggered box near u = sqrt(1 + b^2/4) and at negative u, XXZ + DM_z, a Kitaev-Gamma-like point, zero-field XYZ at two equal couplings), each with a 0.05 kick.
- Stage D: every cluster (distance 0.03) with screening value above 1.1, plus the 24 best clusters regardless, refined by ring ED at L = 16, 20 and open-chain DMRG at L = 32, 64, 128 with chi up to 128.
