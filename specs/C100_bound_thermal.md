# C100: Thermal and transport routes
Your folder is `../work_C100/`. Read `../../specs/_bound_background.md` first. Route: find a thermal or dynamical lattice quantity whose CFT value is proportional to sum_a c_a v_a and whose lattice value is bounded by M and log d with no crossover scale. Candidates: the energy-current operator J_i = i[h_{i-1,i}, h_{i,i+1}] (operator norm at most a multiple of M^2) and its thermal correlators and Drude weight in a CFT (compute their c and v dependence), sum rules for the energy-current or energy-density spectral functions, the high-temperature expansion matched to a low-temperature CFT form through a convexity or analyticity argument for the free energy, and modular-invariance relations between the ring ground energy and the thermal free energy. For each, compute the CFT value, the lattice bound, and whether the comparison needs a crossover temperature; prove the best resulting inequality.
