From C120: with X = exp(-beta H_{2L}), Y = exp(-beta(H_L (x) 1 + 1 (x) H_L)), seam region C of dimension q, and mu(W) = inf{Tr Omega : W <= I_C (x) Omega_D},

    R_L(beta) = Hmin(C|D)_sigma - Hmin(C|D)_rho + log[mu(Y)/mu(X)] <= 2 log q + log[mu(Y)/mu(X)].

The entropy part is bounded by the seam dimension; the whole difficulty is log[mu(Y)/mu(X)], which naively is at most beta ||V|| = O(beta M) (the trivial bound, losing a factor L). Target: log[mu(Y)/mu(X)] <= K'(d) + k M f(d) beta/L, which gives R_L <= K + k M f(d) beta/L and hence A <= (4k/pi) M f(d).

Approach: in one dimension, imaginary-time evolution of local operators is local and bounded (Araki 1969; Perez-Garcia and Perez-Hernandez on locality estimates for complex time evolution in 1D; Kuwahara and Saito; Bluhm, Capel and Perez-Hernandez), so the expansional E_beta = exp(-beta(H+V)) exp(beta H) for a local seam term V has norm bounded uniformly in L by a function of beta M. Use this, or its sharper versions, to compare X and Y in the operator order needed for mu, with the seam smeared over a region of width w (so q = d^w) chosen to balance the entropy term 2 w log d against the reconnection cost; determine how the Araki constant grows with beta and whether working at the scaling point beta = tau L (with tau fixed, then tau large) allows a bound linear in beta/L, for example because only the low-energy part of the column, of size controlled by the ring gap of order v/L, matters for mu. Compute mu(X), mu(Y) exactly (it is a semidefinite program) for small XX, Heisenberg and SU(3) rings to test the scaling of the ratio with beta and L. State the best bound obtained and the remaining lemma.
