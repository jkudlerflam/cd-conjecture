# C99: A length-free entanglement-rate route
Your folder is `../work_C99/`. Read `../../specs/_bound_background.md` and `../work_C69/` in full. C69 found that boundary-state quenches give dS/dt = pi A/(12 ell), so the small-incremental-entangling bound (rate at most O(M log d) per cut) yields A <= O(ell_0 M log d) with an unremovable preparation length ell_0. Task: find a protocol or a family of quantities, not necessarily a single quench, in which the length cancels: for example a supremum over states of the entanglement rate times a matching length set by the state itself (local quenches, inhomogeneous or Floquet drives, the entanglement capacity of the Hamiltonian on a cut, or rates of mutual information between distant intervals bounded by Lieb–Robinson), or a bound on the Casimir coefficient obtained by integrating an entanglement rate over a modular or Euclidean flow. Prove A <= C M log d with explicit C if possible; otherwise identify precisely what obstructs every entanglement-rate route and whether the obstruction is a counterexample mechanism.
