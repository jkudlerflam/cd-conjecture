## Reformulation to verify

Let H^(N)_L be N decoupled copies of the L ring, with on-site space (C^d)^(x)N on which S_N permutes the copies. Conjugating the bond (x0, x0+1) of every copy by the on-site cyclic shift sigma_(x0+1) of the copies turns H^(N)_L into H_(NL), one ring of length NL. Hence E_(NL) - N E_L is the energy of a Z_N permutation twist in the N-copy chain. N = 2 gives the doubling defect D(L) = E_(2L) - 2 E_L of `_Cd_target.md`, and (E_(NL) - N E_L)/N -> e_0 L - E_L as N -> infinity, which is the Casimir variant (a). In CFT the twist field has h = hbar = (c/24)(N - 1/N), so E_(NL) - N E_L = (pi A/(6L))(N - 1/N), consistent with both limits.

## The route

For a continuous on-site symmetry a twist can be spread over the ring at a cost of order (stiffness)/L (the Lieb–Schultz–Mattis construction), and the lattice Kohn-stiffness budget sum_alpha D_alpha <= 4M is proved (`../../contrib/fable_bound_pairing/`). In a quasi-free chain the copy permutations lie inside the U(N) rotation of the copies' fermion modes, which is a symmetry of the N-copy quadratic Hamiltonian, so the permutation twist can be spread out. For interacting chains only the discrete S_N survives, and a twist by a finite group can cost order M (a domain wall) in ordered phases. The question is whether the special structure here (identical decoupled copies, ground state psi^(x)N, vacuum-compatible lengths) still allows a spread twist, or some other trial state on the NL ring, at cost C(d) M/L, with C(d) any finite function.
