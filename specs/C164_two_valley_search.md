## Why

C162 proved that where a symmetry protects a two-valley soliton band (the K stratum and the class U = I R_π(n_bg)), the dilute two-valley gas at onset is immiscible, so c = 1. What it could not exclude, (A_sol), is an accidental two-valley onset in N or T, far from K and U and at ε of order one (lattice-scale solitons), with non-binding interactions and J_z > −|J_⊥|. There the valley index would be conserved (the flip carries the incommensurate momentum 2δ), and a miscible gas would give a stable c = 2 on a codimension-1 locus, a counterexample to the d = 2 conjecture. Analytics do not reach this regime. This run is a direct search. A single convincing point is enough to refute; a clean null within the stated coverage is the other useful outcome.

## Tasks

1. Classical screen (cheap, local or Typhon). On the lattice classical spin chain for general bonds in N and T at ε of order one (no small-ε expansion), find uniform backgrounds that are stable and have an isolated soliton (a 2π twist, or the relevant texture) whose energy, as a function of its internal tilt or momentum coordinate, has two minima not related by any symmetry of the bond. Tune one parameter to make them degenerate, and compute the classical like-valley and unlike-valley pair interactions. Keep candidates whose like-valley interaction does not bind. Report coverage (how many bonds, which regions).
2. Quantum soliton band. For surviving candidates, compute the quantum one-soliton band directly, from ring ED (L up to about 20 to 24) or DMRG excited states in the sector adjacent to the uniform ground state, momentum-resolved. Confirm two degenerate minima at incommensurate momenta after tuning, and estimate the soliton masses and the two-body scattering data (like versus unlike) from two-soliton states, giving J_z and J_⊥ of C162's pseudospin chain at ε of order one, including the mass-imbalance correction that C162 left undetermined.
3. Direct test at the onset. If any candidate has J_z > −|J_⊥| (or the classical screen is inconclusive but the band is two-valley and non-binding), run open-chain DMRG and finite-entanglement scaling just past the onset (C144's `dmrg.py`, C153's `vumps.py` and `fes.py`, C158's `fesfit.py`, all validated), with c from entanglement. Also measure the structure factor, looking for two incommensurate peaks, and the valley-resolved densities. Fix the criteria before looking: a c = 2 candidate needs entanglement fits above 1.5 that do not fall from L = 128 to 256, truncation below 1e-7, two incommensurate peaks, and an FES slope above 1.5 at χ ≥ 256.
4. Scope honestly. State the soliton length, the pseudospin energy scale and the system size each estimate probes, and whether a miscible phase in the region searched would have been visible. If the miscible window is too thin to resolve anywhere, say so and give the bound.
5. Theorem-style summary, every claim tagged proved, derived, numerical, heuristic or assumed. Either an explicit c = 2 bond with evidence, or a null result with its coverage and the residual form of (A_sol).
