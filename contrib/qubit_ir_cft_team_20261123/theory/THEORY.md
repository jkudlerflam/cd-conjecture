## Dynamics, central charge and decisive measurements

**[Conditional speeds.]** In this symmetry-preserving Gaussian theory $\mathcal J H$ has eigenvalues $\pm v_1,\pm v_2$, with $v_i^2$ the eigenvalues of $H_u H_v$. **[Proved causal upper bound, derived here.]** In lattice units with $\hbar=1$ there is a loose microscopic speed bound for locally detectable propagation. The literal bond has eigenvalues $2,0,-1\pm3/16$ in its two parity blocks. Subtract $13/32$ times the identity from each bond to minimize its norm at $J=51/32$. The interaction picture for the on-site $Z$ field conjugates each bond by on-site unitaries and preserves its norm and range. For one-site probes at separation $r\ge1$, define $C_B(X,t)$ as the supremum of the evolved commutator norm divided by the norm of the initial operator on $X$. Differentiating the commutator and removing the norm-preserving homogeneous commutator gives the iterative bound

$$
 C_B(X,t)\le C_B(X,0)+2J\sum_{e\cap X\ne\emptyset}\int_0^{|t|} C_B(e,s)\,ds .
$$

The same differential argument applies to the time-dependent bonds in the interaction picture, using their uniform norm bound. Starting at a site, at most two bonds meet it; each subsequent bond has at most three overlapping choices including itself. A sequence reaching the other site has length $n\ge r$. Since $C_B(e,0)\le2\|B\|$ for overlapping support and vanishes otherwise, iteration gives

$$
 \|[A_x(t),B_y]\|\le \tfrac43\|A\|\|B\|\sum_{n\ge r}(6J|t|)^n/n!
 \le \tfrac43\|A\|\|B\|e^{-r+6eJ|t|}.
$$

Thus $v_{\rm LR}=153e/16\simeq25.99$ is an admissible causal bound. It bounds a ballistic signal with algebraic local weight and supplies no evidence for a mode. [Commutator iteration, Nachtergaele, Ogata and Sims, equations 2.5–2.12](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0603064)
