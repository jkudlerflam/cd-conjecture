## Verdict

C173's effective model is right, and the candidate has no hidden free, integrable or decoupled structure. But the candidate point itself, on J_x + J_y = 0, has a problem C173 could not see. There the two valleys bind: the two-body problem of (3.2) has an inter-valley bound state at K = π, 0.0195|t_2| below threshold at |w| = 1. So the leading competitor on the stratum is inter-valley pairing (c = 1), not only the easy-plane versus antiferromagnetic pseudospin question. And in the qubit chain the momentum-2π two-pair vertex, generated at order Δ⁴/V³, appears relevant on the stratum (dimension 4/K_ρ ≈ 1.1 to 1.25 with the ring estimate K_ρ ≈ 3.2 to 3.6), so it would remove one more mode there, below a scale far too small for any numerics at V/Δ = 40. Neither effect contradicts C173's unconverged c ≈ 2 readings at L ≤ 96. Both say the stratum is the wrong place for the candidate, and the openness argument survives only in the form "c = 2 at a nonzero valley offset".

## Key claims of this run

1. [proved, numerical] Exact Schrieffer-Wolff check. The des Cloizeaux effective Hamiltonian of the full Pauli chain (open L = 11, ring L = 12) confirms every coupling of (3.2) with relative errors ∝ (Δ/V)², and gives the ΔN = 2 vertices: −(2Δ Re w/V) a†_{b+2}a†_b and +(2Δ³/(3V²)) a†_{b+3}a†_b (spin language), each to 0.1% at V/Δ = 40 (sw_exact.out, sw_ring.out).
2. [proved] Selection rule. On J_x + J_y = 0 the symmetry U = I ∘ Π_j R_z(jπ + π/2) makes every even-separation pair creation odd, so the wΔ/V vertex vanishes there to all orders (measured below 1e-14 on the ring). U is the only extra symmetry within inversion × z-rotations (the other is inversion at D = 0).
3. [derived] Consistency with C153. The w = 0 band bottom μ_eff = −2 is C153's factorization line h_f = √(4J_z² − Δ²) to leading order, an exact check of t_2 and 3Δ²/V together.
4. [proved, numerical] Hidden-symmetry check (C163 §4.2): none. The in-plane exchange block has unequal singular values Δ/2 ± D, so the bond is in neither U(1) class, and the symmetric exchange has no zero eigenvalue, so it is not free. At D = 0 the bond is C153's staggered-charge model at g = b³(r − u) = 80.6, in the gapped polarized phase (the band is empty), and D breaks C153's U(1) at the leading order of the effective model (|w| = 2D/|t_2| = 1). The full qubit ring in its lowest band (L = 16): <r> = 0.509 ± 0.007 at V/Δ = 6 and 0.493 ± 0.008 at V/Δ = 12, against 0.407 ± 0.008 for the D = 0 control with C153's hidden Q (full_lowband_L16.out).
5. [numerical] Inter-valley binding on the stratum. At φ = π/2 the lowest two-body level at K = π lies below 2ε_min by 0.0017, 0.0195, 0.1226 for |w| = 0.5, 1, 2 (L = 400 and 800 identical, C173's twobody.py agrees). 6. The parity gap shows no resolvable pseudospin gap at L ≤ 36 (L·Δ_pb = 2.76 at L = 24, 1.91 at L = 36, against 5.4 and 4.0 for the one-valley control).
7. [derived] Resonant vertices on the stratum. A vertex is non-oscillating on J_x + J_y = 0 iff it has equal and even numbers of a and b creations and opposite density harmonics. So the infrared symmetry is U(1)_s × Z_4, and the two dangerous operators are the valley backscattering cos(2√2 φ_s) (dimension 2K_s, in (3.2), decides the pseudospin) and the two-pair vertex O_4 = (ψ_a†ψ_b†)² = cos(2√2 θ_c) (absent from (3.2)). Off the stratum both oscillate.
8. [derived, numerical input] The momentum-2π two-pair vertex. Lowest order: y_4 ≈ c_4 (16/9)(Δ/V)²|t_2| = O(Δ⁴/V³), from two separation-3 ΔN = 2 vertices (the direct ΔN = 4 term is at most relative (Δ/V)⁴, measured 1.9e-12 at V/Δ = 40). Dimension x_4 = 4/K_ρ ≈ 1.2: relevant. It pins the total phase, like C153's conversion vertex at w = 0, and removes one mode (c = 2 → 1, or paired c = 1 → 0). Locking length of a few thousand sites at V/Δ = 40, of order 100 at V/Δ = 8 [heuristic].
9. [derived, conditional] Openness. Off the stratum O_4 and the backscattering carry momentum and are cut off (commensurate-incommensurate), so a c = 2 phase at nonzero valley offset would be open in B_2. The candidate bond should be moved off J_x + J_y = 0.
10. [heuristic] With an inter-valley bound state, a valley offset acts as a Zeeman field on an attractive two-component gas, and the 1D analogue (attractive Gaudin–Yang) has a partially polarized c = 2 phase above the spin gap. That is a second route to c = 2 at nonzero offset, consistent with the reading persisting to φ = 1.54 and vanishing by 1.5. Untested.

R5 (registered after R3 showed an inter-valley bound state at K = π, before running manybody.py).
