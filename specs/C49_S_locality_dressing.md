# C49: Input S by quasi-local dressing and Lieb–Robinson bounds
Read `../../specs/_S_background.md` first. Route: construct a quasi-local unitary (Schrieffer–Wolff to optimal order, spectral flow or quasi-adiabatic continuation à la Hastings, Bravyi–Hastings–Michalakis stability) that block-diagonalizes the chain between the code sector and the massive sectors up to a remainder that is small on the scale of the logical level spacing, using Lieb–Robinson bounds and the cell gap. Chen–Lucas–Yin (2303.07386) show exact quasi-local block diagonalization is impossible in general; find what weaker statement (prethermal, low-energy-projected, or finite-volume with explicit error) suffices for c, and prove it for this chain.
