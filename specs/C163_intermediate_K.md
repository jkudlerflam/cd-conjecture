## Why

C159 Proposition C reduced every c > 1 candidate that could fill an open set or a hypersurface of the time-reversal strata to one with an attractive half-floating Luttinger sector. The two-boson candidate needs x₁ + x₂ > 2, and the Ising × boson candidate (σ at momentum π times a boson, coupled through σe^{2iφ} of dimension 1/8 + K) needs K > 15/8, so that the coupling is irrelevant. K = 15/8 is already Δ_AF = −0.669 for the XXZ parent, and K > 1 starts at the XX point. C160 and its referee C161 control only the K → ∞ corner next to the ferromagnetic Heisenberg point. The window that remains is the attractive liquids with K between 1 and the semiclassical range, far from the U(1) class, together with the neighbourhood of the twisted curve J = −R(φ) (h_F after a site-dependent rotation) at φ of order one. This is most of the remaining gap in the d = 2 programme.

## Tasks

1. Mechanism for a second sector. In an attractive liquid connected to a U(1) or zero-field parent, a second gapless sector (Ising or boson) has to come from a mode that is gapped in the parent and softens under the symmetry-allowed perturbations. Identify the candidate modes from the exact Bethe-ansatz spectrum of the parents in the attractive regime (XXZ plus DM, field, Δ ∈ (−1, 0), including string or bound-state branches and their momenta), and from the bosonization of the perturbations that break U(1) (Γ-type anisotropy, transverse fields, terms carrying lattice momentum). Decide whether any such mode can reach zero energy on a codimension ≤ 1 set while the liquid survives, at K > 15/8 for an Ising mode and with x₁ + x₂ > 2 for a second boson. A mode that softens only by destroying the liquid (a gap opening, a KT transition, a first-order jump) does not count.
2. The twisted curve. Near −R(φ) at φ of order one, the spiral-frame parent is XXZ near Δ = −1, and non-axial perturbations carry lattice momenta φ and 2φ. Derive the infrared there (C161 sketches emergent U(1) for irrational φ/2π and Z_m clock pinning at φ = 2π/m) and decide whether a second sector can coexist with the large-K boson.
