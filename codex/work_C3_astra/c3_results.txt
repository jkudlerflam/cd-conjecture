C3. Exact single-site Lin–McGreevy fixed points for qubits
Closed-book calculation, 21 October 2026. Natural logarithms throughout.

Status of the conclusions

The classical classification is proved. Nontrivial quantum fixed points are
proved to exist, including an XX example that has a pure TI infinite-chain
realization. A numerical classification finds eight classes, three with a
continuous parameter after quotienting by U tensor U, and six distinct values
of c_Delta. Every member of this catalogue fails four-site scale consistency.
No root with 0 < c_Delta <= 1 was found.

The quantum catalogue is a numerical classification, not a completeness theorem.
In particular, I have not proved that every solution has tau = I/2. The value
1.848934360211 is the supremum over the catalogue, attained by the tracial point.
It is the numerical prediction for the global supremum, not a proved sharp bound.
A rigorous bound for the complete solution set is 0 < c_Delta <= 7.395737440844.
Likewise, the absence of all quantum scale-consistent solutions is numerical,
whereas absence in the full-support classical case is proved.

The equations (**) are local consistency conditions. Their satisfaction alone
is not a proof of an infinite TI extension. Explicit pure TI extensions are
given below for the tracial three-site marginal and the untwisted XX example.
Infinite extensions and purity of the other catalogue entries are not established.

1. Classical theorem

Here classical means a common on-site basis throughout the chain. Diagonality
of sigma in two different bases at its two ends does not imply that sigma_12
and sigma_23 commute. The nilpotent example in section 2 illustrates this
important distinction. It is diagonal in an X basis at one end and a Y basis
at the other, but is not a stationary classical chain in one common basis.

Theorem. For strictly positive stationary probabilities p_xy, x,y in {0,1},
the only solution of (**) is p_xy = 1/4 and r_xyz = 1/8.

Proof. Write q for the common marginal and define

  A_y = sum_z p_yz^(4/3) q_z^(-1/3).

The exponential equation gives

  r_xyz = Z^(-1) p_xy^(4/3) p_yz^(4/3)
          q_x^(-1/3) q_y^(-1) q_z^(-1/3).

Taking its right marginal and imposing r_xy = p_xy gives

  (p_xy/q_x)^(1/3) = Z q_y/A_y.

The right side is independent of x. Hence p_xy = q_x b_y, and summing over x
forces b_y = q_y. This proves product structure instead of assuming it.
For p_xy = q_x q_y the three-site formula becomes

  r_xyz = q_x q_z q_y^(5/3) / sum_w q_w^(5/3).

Its marginal condition requires q_y^(2/3) to be independent of y. Positivity
then gives q_0 = q_1 = 1/2. Substitution verifies the solution.

Consequently lambda = (log 2)/2 and

  c_Delta = 3 log 2 / [2 h(1/4)] = 1.848934360211.

For the tracial state S_n = n log 2. Substituting K_n = n log 2 into the
operator combination gives K_Delta = 2 b eta log 2 for any a,b,c.
Thus the advertised single-site example is correct and its c is scale dependent.

2. Quantum catalogue and exact subfamilies

All accepted numerical roots have tau = I/2. Use Pauli matrices X,Y,Z, put

  L(J) = sum_{alpha,beta=1}^3 J_{alpha,beta} P_alpha tensor P_beta,
  sigma(J) = exp L(J) / Z_2,             Z_2 = Tr exp L(J),
  H_3(J) = L(J)_12 + L(J)_23,
  rho_3(J) = exp[(4/3) H_3(J)] / Z_3,   Z_3 = Tr exp[(4/3) H_3(J)].

Then

  lambda = 2 log Z_2 - (5/4) log 2 - (3/4) log Z_3.

These formulas specify the states themselves, not just their entropies. A
common local unitary acts by J -> R J R^T, R in SO(3). Let E_ab denote a
matrix unit, and R_phi a rotation in the xy plane about z. Define

  j_- = -0.4633984613117567,
  j_+ =  0.7305599241416642,
  j_0 =  0.7277678875729927,
  a   =  0.6910314999352538,
  b   =  0.2502781050763858,
  d   =  0.3751033007603815.

The catalogue, modulo common local unitaries, is

  J                                        dim quotient    lambda           c_Delta
  0                                             0        .346573590280    1.848934360211
  j_- R_phi, 0 <= phi <= pi                     1        .217034894085    1.157858776010
  j_+ R_phi, 0 <= phi <= pi                     1        .273981917602    1.461665273230
  j_0 diag(1,1,0) R_phi, 0 <= phi <= pi          1        .211592092571    1.128821991278

Before quotienting, each nontrivial entry has local manifold dimension three.
The tracial point is isolated. Jacobians in all 15 Gibbs coordinates, with
both three-site marginal equations and equal one-site marginals imposed, have
nullity three at all nontrivial representatives and zero at the tracial point.
The quotient dimensions also follow from the explicit rotational families.
At special rotation angles stabilizers change, as expected for a quotient.

The minimum eigenvalues of sigma and rho_3 at the representatives are

  Werner F         .0176215855047    .000674885514718

All are safely in the full-rank interior. Spectra are constant along each of

Exact Werner construction. For J = j I, write

  sigma = [I + t(XX+YY+ZZ)]/4,
  k = 4j/3,
  t = (exp(3k)-1)/(3 exp(3k)+1).

The three-site H = XX_12+YY_12+ZZ_12+XX_23+YY_23+ZZ_23 has eigenvalues
2, 0, -4, with multiplicities 4, 2, 2. Its output bond correlation is

  t_out = (2/3) [exp(2k)-exp(-4k)] /
                    [2 exp(2k)+1+exp(-4k)].

Set x = exp k. The equation t_out = t is

  -3 x^7 + 8 x^6 + 3 x^4 - 9 x^3 + 1 = 0
  = (x-1)(-3x^6+5x^5+5x^4+8x^3-x^2-x-1).

The second factor has exactly two positive roots. Its coefficient sequence
has two sign changes; its values at zero, one, and positive infinity have
signs minus, plus, minus. Descartes' rule and the intermediate value theorem
therefore give one root in (0,1) and one in (1,infinity). These define j_-
and j_+ above. The corresponding t are

  t_- = -.573684246896384,
  t_+ =  .309837885993719.

This is an existence and classification proof within the Werner ansatz.
For J = j R, a site-dependent rotation R^n turns every bond into j I.
The same construction works on three and four sites. It preserves spectra,
partial traces, and lambda, and proves the entire twisted Werner families.
Only conjugacy classes of R are identified by a common local unitary, leaving
one rotation angle after the required quotient.

Exact XX and nilpotent construction. Let r = tanh j. Both constructions obey

  r = (1/sqrt(2)) tanh[(4 sqrt(2)/3) j].                 (A)

For XX, L = j(XX+YY), sigma has correlations (r,r,-r^2), and the three-site
free-fermion spectrum gives this output nearest-neighbor correlation. More
explicitly, its one-particle log-density matrix is (8j/3) times the three-site
adjacency matrix. Its correlation matrix has diagonal 1/2, nearest-neighbor
entries tanh[(4 sqrt(2)/3)j]/(2 sqrt(2)), and zero distance-two entries.
The two-site reduced state is Gaussian with those entries, so fixing r also
fixes its ZZ correlation to -r^2. This checks every matrix element.

For the nilpotent example L = j X tensor Y. The two three-site bond operators
anticommute and each squares to I. Consequently

  rho_3 = [I + r(XYI + IXY)]/8,
  sigma = [I + r XY]/4,

provided (A) holds. Taking either marginal verifies the fixed point directly.

There is a unique positive nonzero j solving (A). For A = 4 sqrt(2)/3 > 1,
tanh(Aj)/tanh(j) decreases strictly from A to 1 on j > 0. Its logarithmic
derivative is 2A/sinh(2Aj) - 2/sinh(2j) < 0. Since 1 < sqrt(2) < A,
it crosses sqrt(2) once. Numerically

  j = j_0,                 r = .621697866912049,
  r^2 = .386508237722996.

The exact implicit formulas for the charges are

  lambda_nil = (log 2)/2 + 2 log cosh j
                         - (3/4) log cosh[(4 sqrt(2)/3)j],
  lambda_XX  = (log 2)/2 + 4 log cosh j
                         - (3/2) log cosh[(4 sqrt(2)/3)j].

In particular c_XX = 2 c_nil - c_tracial, an independent consistency check.
The XX rotations follow by site-dependent rotations about z. The reflection
entry is obtained using a period-two rotation by pi about x. These transformations
are consistent on every interval and preserve the marginal equations.

The two exotic entries are numerical roots. Their coefficients are specified
by the three marginal equations for J = a E_12 + b E_21 - d E_33. The code
solves those equations to machine precision. I do not give a separate analytic
existence or uniqueness proof for these numerical roots.

An analytic reduction for the unpolarized sector. Every sigma with zero Bloch
vectors has log sigma = constant + L(J). The three-spin Hamiltonian H=L_12+L_23
is invariant under time reversal, whose square on three spins is -1. Its
spectrum therefore has at most four distinct eigenvalues. Interpolate exp(4H/3)
by a polynomial a_0+a_1 H+a_2 H^2+a_3 H^3. The coefficient a_3 is a strictly
positive divided difference of the exponential, including the Hermite limits
when eigenvalues coincide.

The normalized partial traces of I,H,H^2 agree at the two ends. For H^3, direct
Pauli multiplication gives the difference of their correlation matrices as

  2 [J^2,J^T].

For example, the left output correlation matrix of H^3 is

  4 ||J||_F^2 J - 2 J J^T J + 2 J^2 J^T,

and the right expression replaces the last term by 2 J^T J^2. Therefore every
unpolarized fixed point satisfies [J^2,J^T]=0. This identity was also checked
numerically on a random J, with error 3.8e-14.

In three real dimensions the resulting matrices are either normal, or have an
orthogonal decomposition into a trace-zero 2 by 2 block and a one-dimensional
block. To see the reduction, J^2 is normal and commutes with J and J^T, so its
orthogonal real spectral blocks reduce J. A complex spectral plane gives a
normal block. On a real block where J^2 is scalar, a non-scalar 2 by 2 block has
trace zero. If J^2 is scalar on all three dimensions, write J=S+A with S symmetric
and A antisymmetric. Then SA+AS=0. In a basis diagonalizing S, A only couples
opposite eigenvalues; in dimension three an additional rotation reduces this
to one such plane and an orthogonal line. The nilpotent case is included.

A normal J is either symmetric and diagonalizable, or a rotation-scaling plane
plus an axial entry. The latter is gauge equivalent to a diagonal XXZ problem.
The nonnormal block can be rotated into [[0,a],[b,0]], leaving an axial entry d.
Thus diagonal and off-diagonal three-parameter searches supplement the full
nine-parameter search. This reduction does not establish tau=I/2 and does not
replace a proof that the remaining three-variable equations have no other roots.

3. Four-site scale consistency

Write A_i=log tau_i, B_i=log sigma_(i,i+1), and lambda=lambda_111. Substitution
of the single-site equation into the (1,1,2) equation determines a unique
normalized four-site candidate

  rho_4 = exp Q_4 / Z_4,
  Q_4 = (3/2)(B_1+B_3) + 2 B_2
        - (1/2)(A_1+A_4) - (3/2)(A_2+A_3),
  lambda_112 = -(2/3)(log Z_4 + 2 lambda).

No commutativity assumption is used. The (2,1,1) operator equation is then
automatic with the same eigenvalue. The (1,2,1) equation is also scalar, with

  lambda_121 = (4/3)lambda_112 - (8/9)lambda.

The binary entropies obey the matching identity

  h(1/9) = (4/3)h(1/3) - (8/9)h(1/4).

Hence the complete four-site problem reduces to the two three-site marginal
constraints and lambda_112 = lambda h(1/3)/h(1/4). This is the system tested.
It is not necessary to solve three independent logarithm equations numerically.

The following are Frobenius marginal errors and scalar mismatches. The left
and right marginal errors agree to numerical precision in this catalogue.

  class             ||Tr_4 rho4-rho3||_F    lambda112 - lambda h(1/3)/h(1/4)
  tracial             < 1e-15                  +.0698071481995
  Werner AF           .0106331153097           -.00266850608240
  Werner F            .00828841812561          +.0295003207749
  XX, both types      .00930443106118          -.00234076409070
  nilpotent           .00451173611238          +.0337331920544
  exotic, both        .0102506565135           +.0352334730263

Thus each nontrivial entry fails even the marginal constraints on the uniquely
determined four-site state. The tracial entry has the correct marginals but
fails the common-c condition. Twists are removed by consistent on-site rotations,
so these obstructions are constant along the displayed one-parameter families.

Classical corollary, proved. There are no full-support classical solutions of
the combined system. The unique single-site solution is tracial, whose four-site
eigenvalues are (2/3)log 2 and (4/9)log 2, whereas the common-c targets are
.392290972174 and .214989215983.

A direct 180-start solution of the combined quantum system found no accepted
full-rank root. Small residuals at small c came from approach to rank-deficient
c=0 boundaries. Among runs ending with c>.01, the smallest combined residual
was .00573129743426. This independent search supports, but does not prove,
absence beyond the catalogue.

4. Bounds and purity

Proposition. Every full-rank solution has 0 < c_Delta <= 6 log 2/h(1/4).

For the upper bound, S(B|A)+S(B|C) <= 2 log 2 and I(A:C|B) <= 2 log 2.
Their weighted sum gives lambda <= 2 log 2. For strict positivity, write
rho_3 = epsilon I/8 + (1-epsilon) rho', with epsilon=8 lambda_min(rho_3)>0.
The weak-monotonicity quantity W=S(B|A)+S(B|C) is concave and nonnegative.
Therefore W(rho_3) >= 2 epsilon log 2. Strong subadditivity then gives
lambda >= epsilon log 2/2 > 0. At the maximally mixed point take epsilon=1.
These are general bounds, not the numerical sharp bound from the catalogue.

The globally tracial infinite-chain state itself is mixed. Its three-site
marginal nevertheless has pure TI realizations. On a six-site ring, the product
of Bell pairs on (1,4),(2,5),(3,6) is invariant under a one-site translation and
has I/8 on every consecutive three-site block. The code checks this exactly up
to floating-point precision.

There is also an infinite-line example. Start with |+> at every site and apply
CZ gates between sites j and j+2. This is a pure TI graph state, obtained by a
finite-depth circuit. Its stabilizers are X_j Z_(j-2) Z_(j+2). For a finite
nonempty product of generators indexed by F, the sites min(F)-2 and max(F)+2
are in its support. Thus no nonidentity stabilizer is supported in an interval
of length at most four. Every such marginal is maximally mixed. This shows
that purity alone does not repair the scale inconsistency of the tracial
three-site data. This pure state is not globally tracial.

Theorem, explicit nontrivial pure realization. The untwisted XX fixed point
with c_Delta=1.128821991278 is the three-site marginal of a pure TI state on Z.

Proof. In Jordan-Wigner fermions, choose a translation-invariant Slater state
whose occupied set E in [-pi,pi] is the reflection-symmetric set determined by

  E intersect [0,pi] = [theta,pi/2] union [pi-theta,pi],
  theta = arcsin[(1-pi r/2)/2],
  r = tanh j_0.

Numerically theta=.01171990543757. Direct Fourier integration gives

  C_0 = |E|/(2pi) = 1/2,
  C_1 = (1-2 sin theta)/pi = r/2,
  C_2 = 0.

Occupation is 0 or 1 almost everywhere, so the quasifree state is pure. It is
the usual spin-chain ground-state construction under Jordan-Wigner. One may
choose the finite-range dispersion

  epsilon(k) = cos k [cos^2 k-cos^2 theta]
             = (1/4)cos(3k)+(3/4-cos^2 theta)cos k.

The occupied set is epsilon(k)<0. Nearest- and third-neighbor fermion hopping
map to a finite-range, parity-even spin Hamiltonian. The thermodynamic Fermi-sea
ground state gives the pure, number-conserving TI spin state. No assumption of
an arbitrary mixed-state purification is needed.

Its three-site correlation matrix has diagonal 1/2, nearest-neighbor entries
r/2, and distance-two entries zero. As computed in section 2, this is precisely
the correlation matrix of exp[(4j_0/3) sum_bonds(XX+YY)]/Z_3. Gaussian states are
determined by their correlation matrices, so their entire three-site density
matrices coincide. The numerical reconstruction error is 1.5e-15. This proves
a nontrivial exact single-site fixed point with a pure TI realization.

The continuous XX twists can also be implemented by site-dependent z rotations;
number conservation makes the resulting states TI. No purity claim is made
here for the Werner or exotic families, or for the nilpotent family.

5. Critical-chain comparisons

For the Ising chain use H=-sum_j(X_j X_(j+1)+Z_j). Define Jordan-Wigner Majoranas
u_j=(product_{l<j} Z_l)X_j and v_j=(product_{l<j} Z_l)Y_j. Its critical covariance is

  <i u_j v_k> = 2/[pi(2(j-k)-1)],
  <i u_j u_k> = <i v_j v_k> = 0 for distinct sites.

The scalar Fourier integral is (1/pi) integral_0^pi sin[(j-k-1/2)p] dp,
which gives the displayed coefficient. Wick's theorem reconstructs all three-site
Pauli coefficients. The code uses Pfaffians of the six-Majorana covariance and
checks mz=2/pi, xx=2/pi, yy=-2/(3pi), zz=16/(3pi^2).

For Heisenberg use the infinite-line singlet ground state. SU(2), reflection,
and time reversal fix its three-site density matrix to

  rho_3 = [I + g1 sum_alpha(P_alpha P_alpha I + I P_alpha P_alpha)
             + g2 sum_alpha P_alpha I P_alpha]/8,
  g1 = (1-4 log 2)/3 = -.590862907413260,
  g2 = 1/3-(16/3)log 2+3 zeta(3) = .242719079825741.

I use these known exact infinite-chain correlator identities, recalled closed
book; their Bethe-ansatz derivation is not supplied here. The reconstruction
from these identities is explicit and does not involve a numerical extrapolation.
An independent small-ring ED check at L=10,12,14,16,18 approaches these values.
At L=18 it gives g1=-.594277710150464 and g2=.246050736925471. A quadratic fit
in 1/L^2 to the last four sizes gives -.590860160865 and .242691793596.
This is a consistency check, not a precision derivation of g2; logarithmic
finite-size corrections are not controlled by this short extrapolation.

The Heisenberg rho_3 eigenvalues are

  (1+2g1+g2)/8, multiplicity 4,
  (1-3g2)/8,    multiplicity 2,
  (1-4g1+g2)/8, multiplicity 2.

For either chain, lambda here means the expectation S_Delta, not an assumed
operator eigenvalue. Define V=Tr rho_3 (K_Delta-lambda I)^2. The weighted vector
residual is sqrt(V)=||(K_Delta-lambda I)rho_3^(1/2)||_F.

  quantity                         critical Ising          Heisenberg
  S1                               .473946633734            .693147180560
  S2                               .593408511276            .953671626570
  S3                               .661408144672           1.096900783677
  S_Delta                          .0983276218808           .218233689682
  c_Delta                          .524567721696           1.164254226882
  sqrt(V)                          .0239902686391           .0329034949395
  ||K_Delta-lambda I||_op           .0859673780250           .181993993659
  ||K_Delta-lambda I||_F            .152050658655            .365020753011

Here F(sigma) is the normalized three-site exponential in (**). Both exact

In particular, closeness of c_Delta to a continuum central charge does not
establish the operator equation.

6. Numerical methods, counts, and limitations

All computations use NumPy/SciPy with one BLAS thread. The unrestricted solver
uses all 15 traceless Hermitian two-qubit Gibbs coordinates to maintain
positivity. Equal one-site marginals are imposed as three residuals. The two
three-site marginal equations each contribute their 15 Pauli coefficients.
Thus the full residual has 33 real components, and no reflection or spin
symmetry is imposed. During optimization tau is the mean of the two one-site
marginals; at accepted roots the additional equality residual makes this the
required common tau. Some unrestricted initial guesses do not themselves have
equal one-site marginals; this equality is solved for explicitly.

The 2400 starts cycle equally through generic random Gibbs states, mixtures
near random pure states, Bell-diagonal states, perturbed Werner states,
perturbed critical Ising two-site states, and perturbed Heisenberg two-site
states. The generic Gibbs scale ranges from .1 to 1.8. Near-pure admixtures
range from 1e-4 to .1. Full starting recipes and random seeds are in c3.py.
Least-squares stopping tolerances are 2e-11, with at most 350 function evaluations
and relative finite-difference step 1e-5.

Acceptance requires minimum sigma eigenvalue >1e-7 and full residual norm
<1e-7. The unrestricted accepted roots in fact have residuals <=8.87e-10 and
Bloch-vector norms <=1.79e-9. There are 1493 accepted roots, 391 boundary outcomes,
and 516 other failures or nonzero local minima. By c_Delta, the accepted counts are

  1.848934: 215; 1.157859: 762; 1.461665: 142;
  1.128822: 282; 1.488878: 38; 1.501909: 54.

A recurring polarized local minimum near the Ising input has c_Delta=.524509
and residual .00110529. It is rejected. This is a concrete reason not to label
all optimizer termination points as solutions.

Additional searches:

  Nine-coordinate unpolarized least squares: 2000 starts, 1825 accepted.
  Diagonal/off-diagonal canonical searches: 1200 starts, 1066 accepted.
  Independent Powell-hybrid root search: 1000 starts, 213 accepted after
    checking the full equations. These 213 were subsequently polished against
    the full overdetermined residual, giving maximum residual 1.82e-10.

The square root search uses the averaged marginal map only to obtain candidates.
It is not equivalent to (**). Every candidate is checked against both original
marginals and the one-site equality. In particular, 197 runs had small averaged
residual but failed the full residual at the stated thresholds. They are not
included in the catalogue.

Damped iteration used sigma_next=(1-alpha)sigma+alpha(rho12+rho23)/2, with
alpha=.15,.4,.7, 120 starts, at most 5000 steps, and stopping at map change
<1e-11 or minimum eigenvalue <1e-12. All 120 ran to the rank-deficient boundary,
so this unrestricted iteration did not discover interior roots. This is a
failure of this iteration as a search method, not evidence that interior roots
do not exist. It also explains the need for root solving.

Eighty symmetry-restricted, damped scalar iterations in the Werner AF, Werner F,
XX, and nilpotent ansatzes converged to the corresponding interior roots.
Their damping was .35, maximum iteration count 271, and maximum full residual
5.31e-13. These runs provide an actual fixed-point-iteration cross-check of the
analytic constructions. The exotic states were found by root solving.

The direct combined search used 180 starts and a residual consisting of the
33 single-site components, the 63 Pauli coefficients for each three-site
marginal of rho_4, and the scalar same-c condition. The three-site marginal
equations automatically enforce all shorter marginals. At its smallest
residuals, sigma approached rank deficiency and c approached zero.

Canonical representatives have full fixed-point residuals below 1.4e-15.
Ninety-six additional global rotations and nontrivial twists gave a maximum
residual 6.89e-15 and maximum change in lambda 5.58e-15. The scalar nature of
the four-site K combinations was independently checked, with spectral spreads
at most a few times 1e-12. Their scalar mismatches and marginal failures are
orders of magnitude larger than these numerical errors.

One initial canonical-search implementation lost positivity numerically when
reconstructing log sigma at very large Gibbs parameters. It was changed to
evaluate exp[(4/3)(L_12+L_23)] directly from the known J. The completed 1200-start
run uses that version. No rank-deficient result is promoted to a full-rank root.

The root searches are extensive but finite. They do not constitute an interval
proof, an exhaustive algebraic classification, or an exclusion of disconnected
polarized branches extremely close to the rank-deficient boundary. Thus the
sharp global supremum and the global quantum no-scale-consistency statement
remain conjectural at the level explicitly stated at the start of this report.
