# C54: Adversarial attack on input S
Read `../../specs/_S_background.md` first. Your job is the opposite of C48 to C53: try to show that input S fails for the wrapped C39 chain (Form W, d0 = 4464) or for the small-n demonstrators, or that it holds only in a form too weak to give c = n + 1. Look for: an effective coupling generated by the massive sectors at some order that is relevant or marginal and not forbidden by the exact U(1) or the margins (pair transfer, umklapp, length–payload resonances, couplings between moving zero-mode sectors); a breakdown of the coupling schedule as n grows (velocities of order 10^(-8.7 n) against corrections); a failure of the order of limits (fixed coupling, L to infinity, then T to zero); or a non-perturbative effect (instantons in the length fluid, defect proliferation, level crossings in the mobile sector). For every candidate, compute its size and scaling dimension explicitly from the C42/C44 bounds, and either show it is harmless or show it destroys the claimed phase. A precise failure is a valuable result; so is a precise list of every mechanism checked and why it is harmless.
