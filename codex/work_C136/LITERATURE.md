2026-11-07. [Proved, source audit] Rechecked [Kozlowski, arXiv:1508.05741v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1508.05741v2), Theorem 3.2, Proposition 3.4, Corollary 4.3, and the remark after Lemma 4.1. The paper assumes even lengths. Theorem 3.2 explicitly makes its leading constant and threshold uniform on compact density subsets of [0,1/2), at fixed anisotropy. Corollary 4.3 supplies the sub-half-filled energy correction. Endpoint expansions divide by the root density at the Fermi boundary. The remark identifies missing uniform energy control near half filling. No anisotropy-uniform crossover estimate is stated.
