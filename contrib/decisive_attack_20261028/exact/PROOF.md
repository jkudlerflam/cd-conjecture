# Circuit-dressed exact encodings: two locality obstructions

All statements below are finite-dimensional theorems except the optional
uniform-in-volume interpretation of an explicitly assumed interaction norm.
They do not assume a gap or a conformal limit. The proof builds on, and
restates the needed part of, C8 RUN8_RESULT section 10.

## 1. The separated-collar lemma

Partition a periodic chain into M >= 4 blocks. On block j let V_j be an
isometry from a logical space to the physical block, and put

    V = tensor_j V_j,  P_j = V_j V_j^*,  P = tensor_j P_j.

Suppose

    H = sum_j B_j + sum_j h_(j,j+1),   [H,P] = 0,

where the B_j act within blocks and the two terms incident on any one block
use disjoint left and right physical collars. A collar can contain multiple
sites. Let K_(j,j+1) be the connected part of V^* h_(j,j+1) V: subtract its
normalized partial traces and restore the scalar once, so that both partial
traces of K vanish.

**Lemma.** The logical operator algebras generated by the left and right
Schmidt factors of incident connected bonds commute. In particular,

    [K_(j-1,j), K_(j,j+1)] = 0.

**Proof.** Set Q_j=1-P_j. Invariance implies that the component of HV which
leaves block j outside its code and every other block inside its code is
zero. Contract logical block j+1 against any Hermitian linear functional
phi with phi(I)=0, and contract every other logical block except j by its
normalized trace. The B_j term and the bond incident on j from the left
vanish, because they are identities on j+1. All terms away from j vanish
before contraction because Q_j V_j=0. The result is

    Q_j A_phi V_j = 0,

where A_phi is the contraction of h_(j,j+1) against the neighboring code and
phi. It is Hermitian, supported on the right collar, and its compression is
the corresponding contraction of K, up to an irrelevant scalar. Hermiticity
also gives V_j^* A_phi Q_j=0. Thus A_phi preserves the code in both directions.
All right Schmidt factors arise this way, including linear combinations.
The same extraction on j-1 gives code-preserving left collar lifts.

The physical lifts commute because the collars are disjoint. Restriction to
a reducing subspace respects multiplication, so their logical compressions
commute. Expanding neighboring K in their Schmidt factors proves the last
claim. This extraction is essential: invariance of H alone does not assert
invariance of each physical term. QED.

## 2. Finite-depth dressing

Let H be a nearest-neighbour Hamiltonian on a chain of sites of arbitrary
fixed finite dimension. Let U be a depth-D circuit, where each gate acts on
an interval of at most r sites and gates in a layer have disjoint supports.
Set s=D(r-1). The support of U^* h_(i,i+1) U lies in

    [i-s, i+1+s].

This follows layer by layer: a layer can extend either endpoint of a support
by at most r-1 sites. No translation symmetry of the circuit is required.

Fix a block cut between sites 0 and 1. A conjugated term crossing that cut
can originate only at a bond with -s <= i <= s. The union of its possible
supports lies in [-2s,1+2s]. Hence the collar on either side has width

    w=2s+1.

For equal blocks of length b >= 2w, each conjugated term meets at most two
blocks, and the left and right collars within each block are disjoint.

**Theorem 1.** If b >= 4D(r-1)+2 and [H, U P U^*]=0, all adjacent connected
bonds of V^* U^* H U V commute.

**Proof.** Apply the separated-collar lemma to H'=U^*HU, grouping all terms
inside a block into B_j and all terms crossing a given cut into one h. The
hypothesized invariance is exactly [H',P]=0. QED.

This applies to invariance of the *whole* tensor-product code, dressed by
U. It does not apply merely because selected low-energy states are related
by a circuit, or because a finite-order effective Hamiltonian is accurate.

## 3. Incompatibility with the standard hopping target

On a logical space of dimension 2^n choose 2n Hermitian Clifford generators
Gamma_a, with {Gamma_a,Gamma_b}=2 delta_ab I, and parity P_log, with
P_log^2=I and {P_log,Gamma_a}=0. Define

    R_a=i Gamma_a P_log,
    K=J sum_(a=1)^m R_a tensor Gamma_a,   m=2n, J real and nonzero.

The left and right active logical factors satisfy

    [Gamma_a,R_b]=2i delta_ab P_log.

Consequently

    [K_12,K_23]=2i J^2 sum_a R_a tensor P_log tensor Gamma_a,
    ||K||=m |J|,   ||[K_12,K_23]||=2m J^2.                 (1)

For completeness, the norm assertions do not rely on a numerical check.
The m operators S_a=R_a tensor Gamma_a are commuting Hermitian involutions.
Every nonempty product of the S_a has zero trace, since the corresponding
nonempty Clifford monomial has zero trace on each tensor factor. Thus each
joint sign projector 2^-m product_a(I+sigma_a S_a) has trace one. All 2^m
sign assignments occur; the largest absolute eigenvalue of sum_a S_a is m.
The middle parity in the commutator is a unitary factor and does not change
this norm.

**Corollary 1.** An exact realization of this connected target under the
hypotheses of Theorem 1 must obey

    D(r-1) > (b-2)/4.

This is a circuit-depth requirement, not a nonexistence theorem for deep
circuits. For every fixed proposed n, a circuit with depth proportional to
the finite cell length remains possible in principle.

**Corollary 2 (target error).** Let K'_12 and K'_23 be adjacent connected
bonds under the hypotheses of Theorem 1. If both differ from K by operator
norm at most epsilon, then

    epsilon >= |J| (sqrt(m*m+m)-m).                         (2)

Indeed, [K'_12,K'_23]=0. Subtracting this commutator from (1) and using
||[A,B]||<=2||A||||B|| gives

    2m J^2 <= 4m |J| epsilon + 2 epsilon^2.

Solving this quadratic proves (2). The bound is on the *connected bond*
error. An estimate for a total Hamiltonian norm is not interchangeable with
it without further hypotheses. The factor in parentheses increases from
sqrt(6)-2 at n=1 toward 1/2 as n grows.

## 4. Exponentially local exact dressing must reach the hopping scale

Now let the transformed Hamiltonian have an interval interaction

    H'=sum_X Phi(X),
    M_mu=sup_x sum_(X contains x) exp(mu diam(X)) ||Phi(X)|| < infinity,

with mu>0. X is a specified supporting interval, and diam(X) is its number
of bonds. On a ring, supporting intervals are specified arcs. Take uniform
blocks of length b >= 2 and M >= 4 blocks. Assume exact invariance [H',P]=0
and exact compressed Hamiltonian

    V^* H' V = sum_j B_log,j + sum_j K_(j,j+1),

with K as in section 3. Internal logical terms B_log,j are arbitrary.
These are hypotheses, not conclusions about the repository's constructions.

**Theorem 2.** For R=floor(b/2),

    |J| <= (sqrt(3)+1) b M_mu exp(-mu R).                  (3)

**Proof.** Fix a block j and a generator label a. Contract block j+1 by
phi_a(X)=tr(Gamma_a X)/2^n and every other logical block except j by its
normalized trace. On terms intersecting block j this defines a Hermitian
physical lift A on block j. Terms disjoint from j give only scalar
identities on j and can be dropped. Exact invariance implies Q_j A V_j=0.
Its compression is J R_a plus a scalar, by the assumed logical Hamiltonian.

These contraction maps have operator norm, and completely bounded norm,
one: phi_a is trace against Gamma_a/2^n, whose trace norm is one, while the
other contractions are normalized traces through isometries.

Split A=A_short+A_long, according as diam(X)<R or diam(X)>=R. Since a term
which misses j+1 is annihilated by phi_a(I)=0, A_short is supported on the
right collar of j, of width at most R-1. The long part satisfies

    ||A_long|| <= sum_(X intersects block j, diam(X)>=R) ||Phi(X)||
               <= b M_mu exp(-mu R) = eta.                (4)

The last inequality assigns each term to at least one site of j, then
bounds the sum by the b pinned norms. Overcounting is harmless.

A contraction of block j-1 with psi_a(X)=tr(R_a X)/2^n gives B_short and
B_long with the same bound. B_short is supported on the left collar. These
collars are disjoint, since 2(R-1)<=b; hence [A_short,B_short]=0.

Scalars in the full compressions can be subtracted from the short lifts
without changing their supports, leakages, or commutators. After doing so,
write

    a_short=V_j^* A_short V_j=J R_a + delta_a,
    b_short=V_j^* B_short V_j=J Gamma_a + delta_b,
    ||delta_a||, ||delta_b|| <= eta.

Exact reduction of A and B, together with (4), gives

    ||Q_j A_short V_j||, ||Q_j B_short V_j|| <= eta.

For commuting Hermitian physical lifts A_short,B_short, inserting
I=P_j+Q_j between them proves

    ||[a_short,b_short]||
        <= 2 ||Q_j A_short V_j|| ||Q_j B_short V_j||
        <= 2 eta^2.                                      (5)

Meanwhile ||[J R_a,J Gamma_a]||=2J^2. The two linear error commutators cost
at most 4|J|eta, and the quadratic one at most 2eta^2. Combining with (5),

    2J^2 <= 4|J|eta + 4eta^2.

Thus eta >= (sqrt(3)-1)|J|/2, equivalently
|J| <= (sqrt(3)+1)eta. Inserting (4) proves (3). QED.

## 5. Implication and limitations

[research judgment] A finite-depth dressing whose depth stays small
compared with the cell size cannot produce the exactly invariant hopping
model needed to bypass the infrared problem. Exponentially local dressing
can do so only if its tails are large enough on the logical energy scale.
This is a reason to retain the perturbative, retarded-action approach.

Theorem 2 does not forbid a dressing with longer localization length, larger
interaction norm, or an exponentially small J. It does not rule out exact
encodings with nonproduct logical Hilbert spaces, or a different logical
Hamiltonian that has the same infrared CFT. It imposes no universal bound
on Virasoro central charge. No CFT identification is proved here.
