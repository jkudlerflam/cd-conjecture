## 1. Setting

A ring of M "cells", j in Z_M. Each cell carries a finite-dimensional Hilbert space K of dimension D, with a unitary grading Omega (Omega^2 = 1). The chain is a fermionic (Z_2-graded) tensor product: operators on different cells that are both odd anticommute. Equivalently, use a Jordan-Wigner representation; all Hamiltonian terms below are even, so they are local fermionic operators.

Inside each cell there is a code, an isometry G : F -> K, where F = C^{2^n} is the Fock space of n complex fermion modes (the "logical" fermions b_{j,a}, a = 1..n), graded by its parity P_F. The code is graded: Omega G = G P_F.

The Hamiltonian is

H = sum_j H_c(j) + lambda sum_j V_{j,j+1},

with:
- H_c even, H_c >= 0, ker H_c = ran G, and H_c >= Delta (1 - G G^dag) with Delta > 0. The excited spectrum of H_c is otherwise arbitrary (not quadratic in any fermions, possibly degenerate).
- V_{j,j+1} even, acting on cells j and j+1, ||V|| <= 1.
- First-order compression: (G^dag tensor G^dag) V (G tensor G) = h_1, an even operator on F tensor F. For definiteness assume h_1 is quadratic in the logical fermions (a hopping/pairing term), but say whether you need this.

Write W for the logical bandwidth scale, W = lambda ||h_1||, and assume W << Delta. The effective logical Hamiltonian obtained by Schrieffer-Wolff to order k is H_eff^{(k)} = lambda sum_j h_1(j,j+1) + sum_{m=2}^{k} lambda^m (Delta^{1-m}) X_m, where X_m is a translation-invariant sum of even operators supported on at most m consecutive cells.

## 2. Questions

A. Local Schrieffer-Wolff to finite order. State and prove a theorem: for each k there is an anti-Hermitian S_k = sum_j tau^j(s), s supported on at most k + 1 consecutive cells, ||s|| <= C lambda/Delta (explicit C), such that e^{S_k} H e^{-S_k} = sum_j H_c(j) + H_eff^{(k)} tensor-embedded on the code + (terms block diagonal with respect to P = tensor_j G G^dag acting on the excited sector) + R_k, with R_k = sum_j tau^j(r_k), r_k supported on at most k + 2 consecutive cells, ||r_k|| <= C_k lambda^{k+1} / Delta^k, and C_k <= C^k k!. Here "block diagonal" means block diagonal with respect to the product projections onto code and non-code of each cell, in the sense of the number of excited cells; be careful to say exactly which off-diagonal pieces survive in R_k. Prove that the maps preserve evenness and translation invariance. You may follow the standard linked-cluster construction; write it out.

B. What a per-cell remainder controls. Prove: (i) the ground-state energy per cell and the free energy per cell of H and of the block-diagonal part agree to within ||r_k|| uniformly in M and beta; (ii) Gibbs expectation values of local observables agree to within a stated function of ||r_k||, beta and the support, by a Bogoliubov/Duhamel argument; (iii) explain why per-cell bounds alone do NOT control level spacings of order 1/M at fixed lambda, by an explicit example, and why this matters if the logical chain is gapless.

C. Can the leakage be removed exactly? Argue whether there can exist a quasi-local unitary U (generated by a quasi-local anti-Hermitian operator with exponentially decaying tails, uniform in M) with U^dag H U exactly block diagonal with respect to the number of excited cells, when the logical chain H_eff is gapless with bandwidth W per cell and the cell excitations have energies Delta. Consider the resonant decay of one excited cell into about Delta/W logical excitations. Give the strongest correct statement you can (a proof of impossibility under a stated genericity assumption, or a reason why it can still exist for the low-energy part).

D. The main question: an imaginary-time representation. The aim is to show that the infrared problem of H is a weakly interacting Grassmann (fermionic functional integral) problem for the logical fermions alone, of the type treated by constructive fermionic renormalization group (multiscale analysis with Gram bounds on fermionic determinants), so that the gapped cell excitations never enter the infrared analysis. Concretely:

1. Show that by a cell-local even unitary (a product over cells) one can assume K = K_aux tensor F (graded tensor product) and G psi = |0_aux> tensor psi for a fixed even vector |0_aux>, so the logical fermions b_{j,a} are defined on every cell as a tensor factor. Check that this is possible with the grading, or say what extra dimension is needed.

2. Split H = H_Gauss + H_aux + H_int where H_Gauss = lambda sum_j h_1(j,j+1) acting on the F factors of all cells (whatever the aux state), H_aux = sum_j H_c(j), and H_int = the rest. Show that every term of H_int either changes the aux state of at least one cell away from |0_aux> or is nonzero only when some aux factor is not |0_aux>, and bound its norm.

3. Write Z = Tr e^{-beta H} and the logical Schwinger functions as a Dyson (Duhamel) expansion in H_int around H_Gauss + H_aux. Organize the aux-sector histories into space-time polymers (maximal connected space-time regions where some cell is not in |0_aux>, connected through H_int insertions), with the logical fermion operators inside each polymer kept as time-ordered operator insertions. Show that, after a fermionic coherent-state (Grassmann) representation of the logical trace with the Gaussian measure defined by H_Gauss, the partition function becomes a Grassmann integral int P(d psi) exp(V_eff[psi]) where V_eff is a sum over connected polymers of Grassmann polynomials, even, translation invariant in space and imaginary time.

4. Prove kernel bounds on V_eff of the form used in constructive fermionic RG: for each number 2m of Grassmann fields, the kernel's weighted L1 norm with weight exp(kappa_t |tau - tau'| + kappa_x |x - x'|) is bounded by (C lambda / Delta)^{something} times a per-site prefactor, with kappa_t of order Delta and kappa_x of order log(Delta/lambda). State the smallness conditions on lambda/Delta and W/Delta. Identify the hardest step. The danger is the combinatorics of many logical fermion insertions inside a large polymer (fermionic signs, and the number of time orderings); say how a Gram-type or determinant bound handles it, or why it fails.

5.
