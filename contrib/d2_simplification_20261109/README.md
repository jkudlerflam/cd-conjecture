# A one-field rational test family for the modified qubit cusp

AI assistance: Codex (OpenAI GPT-6 family), 2026-11-09. This is a
simplification and finite-ring comparison, not a counterexample or a
central-charge measurement. The microscopic site dimension is two and
every bond below is nearest neighbour and one-site translation invariant.

## Recommended simple bond to test

Fix all exchange coefficients and scan only the longitudinal field `h`:

\[
\begin{split}
h_{j,j+1}(h)={}&4Z_jZ_{j+1}
+\tfrac12(X_jX_{j+1}-Y_jY_{j+1})
-\tfrac1{512}(X_jX_{j+1}+Y_jY_{j+1})\\
&+\tfrac1{64}\left[X_jY_{j+1}-Y_jX_{j+1}
+9(X_jZ_{j+1}-Z_jX_{j+1})\right]
+\tfrac h2(Z_j+Z_{j+1}).
\end{split}
\]

In ordinary Pauli coefficients this is

| Coefficient | Exact value |
| --- | ---: |
| `Jx` | `255/512` |
| `Jy` | `-257/512` |
| `Jz` | `4` |
| `Dz`, multiplying `XY-YX` | `1/64` |
| `Dy`, multiplying `XZ-ZX` | `9/64` |
| Total longitudinal field | `h` |

All five exchange coefficients are rational numbers with denominators
that are powers of two. This removes the need to specify a fitted
phase, a square root in the transverse DM coefficient, or a separate
retuning parameter `q` when defining the test family. It does not prove
that exact valley balance is unnecessary at arbitrarily low density.

The starting field window suggested by small rings is approximately
`-7.948 < h < -7.946`. It must be retargeted using the actual density on
larger chains. The simple value `h=-8137/1024=-7.9462890625` is close to
the previously proposed C183 field, but it is not a certified critical
point. A fixed field should not be promoted merely because its
coefficients are convenient.

## What was compared

[numerical] The literal full qubit bonds were diagonalized on periodic
rings, without a blockade approximation or particle-number truncation.
The reference is C183's full-chain-retuned `V/Delta=16` bond:

`Jx=.4979983, Jy=-.5020017, Dz=.0152122, Dy=.13693064, Jz=4`.

The comparison also includes two ablations: cancelling only the
second-order real hopping generated by the transverse DM term, and
removing the `XX+YY` coefficient entirely.

At the far-below-vacuum field `h=-8.09375`, fourth-harmonic fits to
the full-ring excitation energies give the following valley-energy
splittings. These are band diagnostics at that field, not the interacting
finite-density valley energies.

| Bond | Signed splitting, in fitted `abs(t2)` units, L=14 |
| --- | ---: |
| C183 reference | approximately 0, below fit accuracy |
| Rational bond above | -0.04204 |
| Generated-hop cancellation only | +0.09100 |
| No `XX+YY` term | -0.53289 |

The harmonic-fit residual is about `0.0024 abs(t2)`. The rational
rounding therefore creates a modest, resolved imbalance. Removing
the real hopping correction entirely creates a much larger one and
is not a faithful simplification of the dilute two-valley candidate.

[numerical] The reference and rational bond track closely in the
small-ring density ladder. At `h=-7.9463`, their L=14 densities are
0.21359 and 0.21342. Their half-ring entropies are 1.03375 and 1.03035.
The rational bond's onset is shifted by roughly `5e-4` in field, so its
density must be targeted independently.

The strong size dependence is essential. Near `h=-7.9465` the rational
bond has density roughly 0.26 at L=12, 0.21192 at L=14, and 0.18135 at
L=16, corresponding to about three particles rather than a settled
fixed-density thermodynamic state. The L=14 and L=16 half-ring
entropies are 1.02945 and 1.01958. These sizes do not determine `c`,
and their finite-volume onset should not be called a thermodynamic
first-order transition.

## Why this is a useful simplification, with limits

[heuristic prioritization] At finite density a modest valley offset can
leave both modes populated and remove the equal-density backscattering
resonance. This is the partially polarized route discussed by C181.
The rational bond is therefore worth testing at measured densities
around 0.12 and 0.17, rather than demanding exact zero-density valley
degeneracy before every run. Whether those densities support two modes
must be measured. The no-`XX+YY` ablation is a useful one-valley control,
not the preferred candidate.

The physical field changes the effective matching. In particular,
neither a far-field valley tune nor the small fitted quartet-momentum
mismatch can be carried into the gas without remeasurement. The latter
is only about 0.001 to 0.002 radians here, and is not certified by the
finite-ring harmonic fit. The number-changing quartet and the mixed
charge-one interaction remain part of the infrared test.

Uniform `Y` is allowed by the surviving antiunitary symmetry. The
[independent symmetry audit](../d2_priority_audit_20261109/uniform_source_series.py)
finds a very small generated uniform source; checking only `X` cannot
prove its absence. A large `Y` expectation alone is not evidence of a
large source, a gap, or the loss of a two-mode liquid. No transverse
counterterm is silently inserted in the rational bond above.

## Exact simplification statements

[proved] A uniform spin rotation cannot remove the small real hopping
coefficient while keeping the same diagonal exchange and longitudinal
field form. If `S` is the symmetric exchange matrix and `B` the field,
the rotation invariant

\[
\operatorname{tr}S-\frac{B^T S B}{|B|^2}=J_x+J_y
\]

is nonzero for this family. The three exchange eigenvalues are distinct,
so a rotation that keeps `S` diagonal can only permute and reverse its
principal axes. Removing a DM component by rotating its vector trades
it for off-diagonal symmetric exchange or a tilted field; it does not
produce fewer couplings in the same form.

[proved, for the usual helical gauge] A rotation about `Z` by angle
`j theta` preserves the longitudinal field but makes the nonzero pairing
term acquire phase `(2j+1)theta`, and the nonzero transverse-DM term
acquire a charge-one phase depending on `j`. Requiring both to remain
one-site translation invariant leaves only the trivial continuous
gauge choice. Thus the familiar U(1)-chain gauge removal of a DM phase
does not remove these two DM components from this pairing model.
This statement does not claim to classify all possible dualities.
