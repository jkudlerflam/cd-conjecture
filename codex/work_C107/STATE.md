# C107 local state

2026-10-29. Completed investigation of compactness and semicontinuity for A=sum c_a v_a at fixed gauge-optimized M.

[Open] No universal finite F(d), general upper semicontinuity theorem, or physical unbounded-A family was obtained.

[Proved] Normalize in V/G, not the full bond space. G consists of scalar shifts and coboundaries. The quotient dimension is d^4-d^2 and its M-unit sphere is compact. The finite Casimir estimator is Lipschitz with constant 12L^2/pi in the quotient norm. Elementary almost additivity gives |E_L-Le|<=2M.

[Proved] Full-length limits have sector problems. Ferromagnetic XX, hopping t, has M=t and physical A=2t, but even Q_L tends to 2t and odd Q_L to t/2. Classical AF Ising has raw liminf minus infinity despite being gapped. These do not refute a vacuum-compatible Casimir definition.

[Proved] Uniform two-sided convergence fails near critical Ising, including on M=1. Field detuning L^-3 gives gapped points with A=0 and finite-L Q_L approaching the nonzero critical value.

[Proved] Critical XY in Pauli units H=-sum[(1+gamma)XX/2+(1-gamma)YY/2+Z] has M=sqrt(1+gamma^2), A=gamma, and Q_L=gamma+a_gamma/L^2+O(L^-4), with a_gamma=7pi^2(4gamma^2-3)/(240gamma). This disproves universal monotonicity and any universal decreasing correction Q_L+C M/L^2. It does not disprove more general corrected observables or physical upper semicontinuity.

[Proved logical obstruction] E_L(t)=-3Lt^2-t/(1+Lt^2), t in [0,1], has analytic and concave finite-L energies, order-L derivative bounds, controlled boundary errors, length concavity, and suitable open-chain almost additivity, but A_model(t)=6/(pi t) for t>0 and A_model(0)=0. It is not a spin-chain realization. It rules out an inference from these scalar energy properties alone.

[Proved] Global boundedness is equivalent to local boundedness at every point of the compact sphere, and to existence of a finite nonnegative upper semicontinuous majorant. The canonical upper envelope A^* may be infinite. An exact extension also needs agreement on the conformal subset. Finiteness of A^* is the missing physical theorem.

[Proved conditional] A velocity ceiling forces divergent total c if A diverges, but need not force velocities bounded away from zero. An integrable uniform envelope on N_h(u)=sum_(v_a>u)c_a would bound A by integrating in u. No envelope proved.

[Numerical] checks.py passes. Nine spin matrices, dimension at most 256, agree with the XY fermion sum to 1.95e-14. Larger L checks use only scalar quadrature and explicit sums. No heavy numerics or Typhon jobs.
