## Why here

C159 showed that on the time-reversal strata every c > 1 candidate that could fill an open set or a hypersurface needs an attractive (K > 1) half-floating Luttinger sector, and that such liquids live on the ferromagnetic side of the XY regime. The one uncontrolled window is the neighbourhood of the ferromagnetic Heisenberg point h_F = −(XX + YY + ZZ), easy-plane and DM-twisted, with Γ-type anisotropy and fields. That point is singular (z = 2 magnons, a degenerate multiplet, K → ∞ along XXZ as Δ → −1⁺, Bethe-string bound states below the two-magnon continuum), so no CFT argument applies there directly. A second gapless species could plausibly enter through (i) bound magnon pairs coexisting with single magnons, or (ii) a Bogoliubov magnon dispersion with two degenerate minima at ±k₀ once pair-creation terms (no U(1)) are present, giving a two-component dilute gas. In frustrated J1–J2 chains, two minima near saturation are known to produce multi-component and nematic liquids. Whether a nearest-neighbour one-site qubit bond can reproduce this is the question.
