The user requested a closed-book numerical attack on the c-d conjecture, entirely within this directory, with at most 75 minutes of local runtime. No pre-existing project files or external references were read. Parent project memory was not accessed or modified. This new local file records the session.

The finite campaign is finished. `REPORT.md` is the main result and `METHODS.md` contains the derivations and assumption flags. There are 3,526 retained measurement records in the merged catalogue, including 768 energy-only points. The grids and their actual sizes are listed in the report. The largest qutrit size is 14. The generic spin-3/2 sphere reaches 11 at selected points, and symmetry-reduced four-state calculations reach 12.

No verified counterexample was found. This is not an exhaustive exclusion and the thermodynamic phase catalogue remains incomplete. The four supplied CFT calibrations pass the chosen energy-fit envelope. Raw entropy slopes substantially overestimate c even at the saturating ULS anchors. Single-velocity energy estimates fail when velocities split.

The most useful new diagnostic is `sp4_two_velocity_test.json`. At theta=2pi/3 and pi, current-momentum states have Sp4 Casimir 3 (adjoint) and 2 (vector). Their velocities predict the ground-state Casimir coefficient to 0.67 and 0.24 percent under a five-plus-one Majorana ansatz with c_T=3. The naive energy charges at those points are 3.422 and 4.720. The continuum ansatz is supported, not proved. `sp4_towers.py` additionally checks Casimir variances to distinguish eigenvalues from expectations of mixed representations.

The strongest unresolved region is small positive Sp4 theta. At theta=pi/48, c_ent(L=12)=8.0286, with cut fits ranging from 6.3696 to 9.1669. Its adjacent gap has effective exponents 2.400 and 2.513 on 8->10->12. The first 0/pi crossing moves from theta=0.2351602 at L=6 to 0.1144165 at L=12. No Sp4_2 tower was identified. Several nonpolarized spin-3/2 sphere points also have large, unstable entropy slopes.

All U(1)-only ground energies were audited over magnetization. The adjacent-momentum velocity audits are in `u1_global_velocity.jsonl`, `refined_global_velocity.jsonl`, and `deformation_global_velocity.jsonl`. At (Delta,beta,D)=(0,1,2), L=14 has degenerate M=2 and M=6 ground states with entropy slopes 2.508 and 1.038. The unique-sector excess at (1,1,2), L=14 has only six nonzero-color sites and remains unresolved. Saturation at beta=1, D=4 has a quadratic mode; D>max(4,1+|Delta|) has a proved product-state gap.
