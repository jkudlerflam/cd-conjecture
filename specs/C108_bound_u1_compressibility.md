For a U(1) Luttinger sector, D = vK/pi (charge stiffness, bounded on the lattice by the proved budget D <= sigma_q^2 M) and the inverse compressibility is e''(n) = pi v/K, so v^2 = D e''(n) exactly, independent of K. Hence v <= sqrt(sigma_q^2 M e''(n)), and the U(1) case of A <= C M f(d) reduces to an upper bound on e''(n) in units of M. Investigate: (i) e(n) is convex in the density n and |e| <= O(M), and the chemical potential mu(n) = e'(n) should range within an interval of length O(M sigma_q) (prove this, for example by inserting or removing one charge with a bounded local operator); so the integral of e'' over the density window is O(M), giving an averaged bound; (ii) whether e''(n) can be pointwise large at fixed bond (near a Mott plateau edge, where v -> 0 but K stays finite, or elsewhere), and whether a large e'' forces a small D so that the product D e'' stays bounded (prove a pointwise bound on D e'' if possible, for example from a joint variational argument for the twist and the density shift); (iii) combine with the multi-sector stiffness matrix to bound sum_a v_a over all U(1) sectors coupled to conserved charges. A pointwise bound v_a <= C M g(d) for each such sector, together with a bound on their number by the number of independent on-site charges (at most d - 1), would give A <= C M (d - 1) g(d) for U(1) infrareds.
