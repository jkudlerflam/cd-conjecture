# C167 derivations: the assumption (A_W) on T and K_I

## Key step, for a referee

The harmonics that break the U(1)_m of the spiral parent come in two charges. The charge-2 part (in-plane exchange anisotropy) preserves the pi rotation about the spiral axis, and a bond whose charge-1 part vanishes is exactly a parity-class bond, XYZ + D_m (Lemma R0, proved and checked to 1e-13). In T the charge-1 part is the exchange block S_{m perp}, in K_I it is the in-plane field and nothing else. So the old expansion "small harmonics around the U(1) parent" (C163 Proposition P) can be replaced by "small charge-1 harmonic around a parity-class liquid with charge-2 of any size" (Proposition R). Every term generated by the charge-1 part has odd or even pi-parity according to its order, and in the half-floating boson it is a vertex e^{i(p vartheta + 2 n phi)} with p of that parity at momentum p alpha + n pi. Off the resonances alpha in pi Q such terms oscillate, and the zero-momentum ones only renormalize (K, v, alpha). So the infrared stays one boson, with KT-ordered and clock-locked phases, as long as the charge-1 part is small on the lattice scale. Inside the parity class itself c <= 1 is input A4 of the C159 theorem (C152). A second lemma (Lemma C) shows that the charge-1 harmonic couples to the boson's own e^{i vartheta} and destroys the liquid by a commensurate-incommensurate transition at an amplitude g_c ~ v (K theta_F)^{2 - 1/(4K)}, so a liquid can carry a charge-1 harmonic of lattice size only when the twist theta_F is of order one. What is left of (A_W) is the statement (A_W1) for charge-1 harmonics comparable to the exchange at theta_F of order one, with charge-2 arbitrary. The one window point whose open-chain c rose above 1 at L = 256 (hi_K_1, K_I, field fraction 1.02) was tested along its harmonic path. The staggered order there enters where the boson's own commensurate vertex reaches dimension 1/2, which is the single-boson KT route, not an independent Ising mode.

## 0. Conventions

As in C159 and C163. H = sum_j [ sigma_j^T J sigma_{j+1} + f . sigma_j ], Pauli matrices. Polar decomposition J = S O with O = R_m(theta) in SO(3) (tk.spiral_parent). Spiral frame sigma_j = R_m(-j theta) tau_j, in which the bond is tau_j^T R_m(j theta) S R_m(-j theta) tau_{j+1} + (R_m(j theta) f) . tau_j. Parent: S_0 = s_perp (1 - m m^T) + s_m m m^T, Delta = s_m/|s_perp| (antiferromagnetic convention). The charge-q parts of S and f about m pick up e^{i q j theta} in the spiral frame. Write

    S = S_0 + S_1 + S_2,   S_1 = P S M + M S P,   S_2 = P S P - s_perp P,   f = f_m + f_1,      (0.1)

with M = m m^T, P = 1 - M, f_m = M f, f_1 = P f. The harmonic fractions are h_1 = (|S_1| + |f_1|)/|S_0| and h_2 = |S_2|/|S_0| (Frobenius norms, charges.py). theta_F denotes the spiral-frame momentum of a charge-1 operator measured in the frame where the boson's e^{i vartheta} is uniform, that is theta_F = theta for s_perp < 0 and theta_F = theta + pi for s_perp > 0 (the staggered rotation about m). Bosonization: V_{p,n} = e^{i(p vartheta + 2 n phi)}, x_{p,n} = p^2/(4K) + n^2 K. In T and on the K_I branch connected to the parent, translation acts as phi -> phi + pi/2, vartheta -> vartheta + alpha (C159 Lemma A), and the commensurate vertex is V_c = e^{+-2 i phi} at momentum pi with x = K. Lambda = Z alpha + Z pi is the boson's charge lattice (alpha = theta_F up to renormalization).

The target (spec C167, from C163). (A_W): in an attractive half-floating liquid of T or K_I (K > 1) with harmonics comparable to the exchange, no lattice-scale excitation outside the charge lattice reaches zero energy while the liquid survives. (a) No independent staggered Ising mode at K > 15/8. (b) No composite band minimum off Lambda at K > 1.

## 1. One statement for both cases, and what the antiunitary forces

[derived] Both cases concern a lattice-scale branch b, not part of the boson's tower, whose band minimum P* moves to zero energy while the boson stays gapless. The branch is created from the ground state by some local operator O_b at momentum P*. The antiunitary A of the stratum (Theta in T, Theta R_pi(z) in K_I) commutes with translation and maps P to -P. Hence:
- if P* is in {0, pi} the branch can be A-self-conjugate and real. At P* = pi this is case (a), an Ising field sigma_I with translation acting as sigma_I -> -sigma_I. At P* = 0 it is C163 Lemma U (codimension 2);
- if P* is not in {0, pi}, the branch has an exact partner at -P*, degenerate with it (two valleys). If P* is in Lambda, C163 Lemma L locks it to the host. If P* is not in Lambda, this is case (b).

Lemma Θ2 (case b) [derived; inputs: momentum conservation, C159 Lemma A applied to the second boson, C159 table 4.1]. Let the two valleys +-P* (P* not in Lambda) carry occupation numbers N_+ and N_-. No microscopic charge is conserved in T or K_I, so N_+ + N_- is not conserved. Pair creation of (+P*, -P*) conserves momentum and is allowed, while every process that changes Q = N_+ - N_- by Delta Q != 0 carries momentum Delta Q P* plus an element of Lambda and oscillates. So Q is the only emergent charge, and the soft branch is a single complex field, A-odd in Q (A maps +P* to -P*). Two cases follow.

(b-sym) A unbroken. Then the Q density vanishes, the second boson (phi_2, vartheta_2) has A reversing phi_2, the translation shift of phi_2 is zero, and e^{2 i phi_2} sits at momentum zero with an A-even real part allowed. Its dimension is K_2, so the composite sector is gapless only if K_2 > 2 (the last row of C159 table 4.1). Its onset from the gapped side is the KT point K_2 = 2 of a relativistic O(2) field. Consequence: in such a c = 2 phase the composite order parameter O_b, a local operator at the incommensurate momenta +-P*, has quasi-long-range order with exponent eta_2 = 1/(2 K_2) < 1/4.

(b-brk) A broken spontaneously, with <Q> != 0 (one valley populated, the immiscible case of C162). The Q density is uniform and A-odd, like the host's m-density (2/pi) d_x phi_1. The coupling rho_Q d_x phi_1 is allowed, so <rho_Q> acts as a uniform field along m on the host, and the phase carries a spontaneous uniform magnetization along the spiral axis at zero field.

Either way, case (b) produces an open c = 2 phase (codimension 0), not a hypersurface. This was implicit in C159 Proposition C. What is new is the dichotomy and the two signatures, eta_2 < 1/4 at an incommensurate momentum outside Lambda, or a spontaneous m-magnetization.

Lemma O (case a, ordered side) [derived; inputs: C159 sections 4.1 and 4.2]. sigma_I at momentum pi couples to the A-matched combination of e^{+-2 i phi}. If sigma_I is A-odd (in T, Theta-odd), its lattice representatives include the staggered m-moment (-1)^j sigma^m_j, so the ordered side carries a staggered moment along m. If it is A-even, the ordered side is dimerized (or, in K_I, has a staggered in-plane moment). For 15/8 < K < 2 the ordered side is gapped (the boson is pinned by <sigma_I> V_c), for K > 2 it is floating + SSB. A diagnostic follows that separates case (a) from the boson's own ordering. Let x_pi be the smallest scaling dimension among operators at momentum pi with an overlap on the staggered moment (or the dimer operator). In the one-boson liquid x_pi = K and the liquid orders by KT when x_pi reaches 1/2 (V_c^2 becomes relevant). Next to an independent Ising surface x_pi drops to 1/8, the dimension of sigma_I, independently of K. Section 6 uses this test.

[derived, negative result for lead 3 of the spec] The antiunitary does not force sigma_I to mix with the liquid. The only coupling of dimension below 2 at the decoupled point is sigma_I V_c (dimension K + 1/8), and it is irrelevant exactly in the window K > 15/8. On rings of even length (Theta T_1)^2 = Theta^2 T_2 = +1 on the momentum-pi sector, so there is no Kramers pairing at pi. The LSM anomaly in T is carried by the boson, and the Ising factor with translation acting as its Z_2 is anomaly free. Lead 3 therefore gives nothing beyond C159.

## 2. Lemma R0: the charge decomposition and the parity class

Lemma R0 [proved]. (i) For every bond, removing S_1 and f_1 from (0.1) leaves a bond J_P = (S_0 + S_2) O, f_P = f_m that commutes with the global pi rotation Pi = prod_j R_pi(m)_j, and S_1, f_1 are exactly the parts odd under Pi. (ii) A bond of T with S_1 = 0 is a parity-cap-T bond, XYZ + D_m, B = 0 (the sheet det M > 0 of C159 Lemma 1). (iii) For a bond of K_I, S_1 = 0 identically, and the charge-1 harmonic is the in-plane field f_1 = B_perp alone. So K_I at B = 0 is again parity cap T.

Proof. (i) R_pi(m) = 2M - 1 commutes with R_m(theta), with M and with P, and R_pi P = P R_pi = -P, R_pi M = M R_pi = M. So R_pi S_0 R_pi = S_0, R_pi S_2 R_pi = (-P) S (-P) - s_perp P = S_2, and R_pi S_1 R_pi = -S_1 (one factor of P in each term), R_pi f_1 = -f_1, R_pi f_m = f_m. The bond transforms as J -> R_pi^T J R_pi under the global rotation. (ii) If S_1 = 0 then m is an eigenvector of S, J = S R_m(theta) is block diagonal (2 x 2 in the plane, s_m along m), and any real 2 x 2 block is a symmetric matrix plus D_m times the planar epsilon tensor, which a rotation about m diagonalizes into J_x XX + J_y YY + D_m (XY - YX). (iii) In C159 Lemma 1, J = diag + D_z A(e_z) on K_I has z as a common invariant axis of S and O (also when J_z < 0, where the polar factor flips sign), so S_1 = 0 and the spiral axis is z; f is in the plane. QED.

[numerical, charges.py] For 200 random (J, f) the bond with S_1 and f_1 removed is Pi-symmetric to 1.2e-13. XYZ + D_z bonds give S_1 = 0 exactly. The 16 attractive C159 points of C163 section 2.2 have charge-1 fractions 0.09 to 0.48 (all exchange, B = 0) and charge-2 fractions 0.03 to 0.54. hi_K_1 (K_I) has a field fraction 1.02 and a charge-2 fraction 0.77 at parent Delta = -0.154.

Dimensions. In T the parent (Delta, theta) plus the charge-2 amplitude span the 3-dimensional parity cap, and the complex charge-1 amplitude adds 2 (one phase is fixed by the rotation about m together with the charge-2 axis). In K_I the same holds with the field. So in both strata the charge-1 harmonic is a 2-dimensional complex amplitude on top of a 3-dimensional parity-class base.

## 3. Proposition R: all orders in the charge-1 harmonic around a parity-class liquid

Input. The base bond H_P (charge 0 and 2 only) lies in the parity class, whose phase diagram is C152's. Its chiral Luttinger liquid G1 is one compact boson (C152 result 3, two-Majorana picture, derived away from strong attraction and XZ; c <= 1 there is input A4 of the C159 theorem). In parity cap T both Theta and Pi are exact. Theta fixes the phi shift at pi/2 (C159 Lemma A), Pi acts as vartheta -> vartheta + pi and phi -> phi, and translation shifts vartheta by alpha (incommensurate away from theta in {0, pi}).

Proposition R [derived; inputs: C152 G1 at H_P, Lemma R0, momentum and Pi-parity bookkeeping, standard multiscale RG as in C163 section 2.1]. Let H = H_P + V_1 with V_1 the charge-1 part. Suppose H_P is in the interior of G1, and let delta_lat > 0 be the gap of H_P to every state outside the low-energy tower of its boson (Pi-even and Pi-odd). Then to every order in |V_1|/delta_lat the infrared of H is one compact boson with renormalized (K', v', alpha'), plus the commensurate vertices allowed at the resonances alpha' in pi Q. Its phases are the liquid (K' > 1/2), the KT-ordered period-2 states (K' < 1/2, Neel along m or dimerized, according to the sign of the umklapp), the clock-locked tongues, and their KT, Pokrovsky-Talapov and z = 2 boundaries. All have c <= 1. In particular neither case (a) nor case (b) of (A_W) occurs, for any size of the charge-2 harmonic for which H_P is in G1.

Derivation. Integrate out the states of H_P above a cutoff below delta_lat. The effective Hamiltonian on the boson's Fock space is P H_P P + P V_1 P + P V_1 Q (E - Q H Q)^{-1} Q V_1 P + ..., local because the integrated sector is gapped. (i) Pi-parity. V_1 is Pi-odd (Lemma R0), so the k-th order term has Pi-parity (-1)^k, and its projection onto the boson contains only V_{p,n} with p of the parity of k, times derivatives. (ii) Momentum. V_1 is translation invariant in the original frame, and so is every generated term. V_{p,n} carries momentum p alpha + n pi, so a generated operator survives at low energy only if p alpha + n pi = 0 mod 2 pi. For p = 0 this allows n even, that is the identity, derivative terms (which renormalize K, v and the twist alpha) and the umklapp cos 4 phi (Theta-even, dimension 4K). For p != 0 it singles out alpha in (pi/p) Z. (iii) Everything else oscillates and is irrelevant at the scale 1/|p alpha + n pi|. So off the resonances the infrared is the boson of H_P with renormalized parameters, and at the resonances it is the commensurate-incommensurate problem of the same boson with one clock vertex. No operator in this expansion acts outside the one-boson Hilbert space, so no second sector appears. The small denominators near resonances are not a breakdown of the expansion, because those states are kept in the effective theory and the clock problem is solved there. The expansion breaks down only when |V_1| reaches delta_lat. QED.

Remarks. (1) Proposition R at V_1 = 0 is C152. At small S_2 it is C163 Proposition P. It covers harmonics of the size of the exchange provided they are charge 2. (2) A renormalization of K' below 1/2 is allowed and gives period-2 order through the boson's own KT. The hi_K_1 path of section 6 shows this happening at large harmonics. (3) If H_P is gapped (charge-2 beyond the pairing threshold Delta_c(phi, K) of C152), a small V_1 keeps H gapped (c = 0). At the G1 boundaries of the parity class (Pokrovsky-Talapov edges, Ising-chiral junctions) the onset species is one (C152 result 4, C154 Lemma 3), and the Pi-odd perturbation acts on it as on any single species.

Consequence for (A_W) [derived, conditional on A4 for the base]. (A_W) holds wherever the charge-1 harmonic is small on the lattice scale, at every charge-2 amplitude. The part of (A_W) with large charge-2 and small charge-1 is therefore transferred to A4, which the C159 theorem already assumes, and is removed from (A_W).

## 4. Lemma C: how large a charge-1 harmonic a liquid can carry

Lemma C [derived; inputs: bosonization of the charge-1 operators, the commensurate-incommensurate transition of sine-Gordon with a twist (Japaridze and Nersesyan, Pokrovsky and Talapov)]. (i) The leading infrared content of the charge-1 harmonic is g cos(vartheta + theta_F x + chi) with g proportional to |h_1| and a nonuniversal nonzero coefficient. For the in-plane field of K_I this is the transverse field acting on sigma^+ ~ e^{i vartheta}[b + c (-1)^j cos 2 phi] (frame of theta_F). For the T exchange S_1, the product sigma^m_j sigma^+_{j+1} contains (-1)^j a cos 2 phi times (-1)^{j+1} c cos 2 phi e^{i vartheta}, whose operator product has a uniform e^{i vartheta} part, and nothing in Theta or Pi forbids it. (ii) Shifting vartheta' = vartheta + theta_F x turns the problem into sine-Gordon in vartheta' with a twist, whose soliton chemical potential is 2 v K theta~ per soliton (theta~ the distance of theta_F from 2 pi Z), against a soliton mass M proportional to v (g/v)^{1/(2 - 1/(4K))}. The liquid (a soliton lattice of vartheta', c = 1) survives for M < 2 v K theta~, that is for

    g < g_c(theta~) = C_0 v (K theta~)^{2 - 1/(4K)},      (4.1)

and beyond g_c the boson is pinned (a commensurate canted state, gapped, Theta-broken in T, A-preserving in K_I). For K -> infinity this is Dzyaloshinskii's g_c proportional to theta~^2. (iii) Near theta_F = pi the leading resonant vertex is e^{i vartheta} cos 2 phi, of dimension 1/(4K) + K, which is irrelevant for K > 1 + sqrt(3)/2 = 1.866. So in window (a) (K > 15/8) the charge-1 harmonic is first-order irrelevant near theta_F = pi.

Consequence [derived]. Formula (4.1) is valid while g_c is below the lattice scale. Where g_c(theta~) is small compared with the threshold of Proposition R, the liquid is destroyed by the charge-1 harmonic before that harmonic becomes large on the lattice scale, and every liquid in that range is covered by Proposition R. So the residual needs theta~ of order one. With v of order 1.7 |s_perp| (Bethe velocity at Delta = -0.75 in Pauli units, pi sin gamma / (2 gamma) times 4) and C_0 of order one, a charge-1 fraction of 0.3 survives only for theta~ above roughly 0.3 to 0.5 at K near 2. [heuristic for the numbers, since C_0 is not computed]

## 5. Where the method stops

The residual region has the charge-1 harmonic at the lattice scale, theta~ of order one, charge-2 arbitrary and the parent in the attractive window. In this region:
- there is no small parameter. Proposition R needs |h_1| < delta_lat, and C160 needs 1/K small, while here K is between 1 and a few;
- there is no extra symmetry. The bond is a generic point of the 5-dimensional stratum (Lemma R0 counts the charge-1 amplitude as 2 genuine dimensions);
- the C165 mechanism (staggered order and spiral as one SU(2)_1 multiplet near the hidden Heisenberg point) has no analogue. The attractive parent's nearest special point is h_F, where the staggered branch is at the top of the magnon band (4(1 + |Delta|) above the band bottom in Pauli units, C160), and the classical spiral to staggered-m switch e_s = e_N never occurs, since the collinear Neel-m product state has energy |s_m| per bond for any harmonics (its expectation is -m^T J m = -s_m) while the parent ground energy is negative [proved, elementary];
- the Perron-Frobenius route of lead 2 needs a sign-free product basis. In the spiral-axis basis the flip-flop amplitude carries the twist phase e^{i theta} and the charge-1 terms flip one spin with a sign that depends on the neighbour (sigma^x sigma^z type). So the bond is sign-free there only at theta in {0, pi} with h_1 = 0, which is inside the controlled set [derived for this basis; other local bases were not searched];
- a convexity interpolation from C163's controlled region (lead 2, second item) would need a bound on the lowest state at a momentum off Lambda as a function of the harmonic amplitude. The ground energy is concave in the amplitude (E_0 of an average of rotated Hamiltonians is at least the average of E_0, and rotations about m are isospectral), which bounds energies from above in every momentum sector. That controls neither the gap nor the existence of a separate branch [proved for the concavity, negative result for the method].

## 7. The residual

(A_W1) [assumed]. Take a half-floating liquid of T or K_I whose commensurate vertex has dimension x = K > 1, whose charge-1 harmonic is comparable to the exchange (|h_1| of order 0.3 |S_0| or more), and whose charge-1 twist theta~ is of order one. Then no lattice-scale branch outside Lambda reaches zero energy while the liquid survives. In case (a) (K > 15/8) the staggered dimension x_pi stays equal to K, with no Ising surface at which it drops to 1/8. In case (b) (K > 1) there is no open c = 2 phase of type b-sym (quasi-long-range order with eta_2 < 1/4 at +-P* outside Lambda) or b-brk (spontaneous uniform m-magnetization at zero field).

Why it is strictly narrower than (A_W). (A_W) covered all harmonics of the size of the exchange. Proposition R removes every bond whose charge-1 part is small, at any charge-2 amplitude (that part now rests on A4, already an input of the theorem). Lemma C removes small theta~ for any charge-1 amplitude, because the liquid dies first. Lemma Θ2 and Lemma O sharpen what a violation would look like into signatures that can be measured.

Where it bites. Parents with -1 < Delta < -0.67 (case a) or -1 < Delta < 0 (case b), charge-1 fraction above roughly 0.3, theta~ of order one (for the T exchange and the K_I field alike), any charge-2, away from h_F and gamma (C160, C163 Proposition G). In T this is the region of large Gamma-type exchange S_{m perp}. In K_I it is an in-plane field of the order of the exchange, below the field at which the liquid gives way (section 6.1 shows the K_I liquid surviving up to field fraction 1.0 at theta_F = 1.93, then KT order and polarization).

## 8. Consistency checks

- Lemma R0 against random bonds and against XYZ + D_z (1.2e-13, exact zero).
- Proposition R reduces to C152 at V_1 = 0 and to C163 Proposition P at small S_2. Its phase list (liquid, KT-ordered period-2, clock tongues) contains the Baxter and XXZ cases, and at theta in {0, pi} the resonant charge-1 vertices of C159 section 3.1.
- Lemma C reduces to Dzyaloshinskii's g_c proportional to theta^2 at K -> infinity, and its pinned side is the commensurate canted state, Theta-broken in T (allowed by LSM) and unique in K_I (allowed, no anomaly).
- Lemma Θ2 at P* -> pi merges the two valleys into one real field, which is case (a). At P* in Lambda it reduces to C163 Lemma L. The b-brk branch is C162's immiscible two-valley gas, now on a liquid background.
- Numerics: the small-lam x_pi of the hi_K_1 path reproduces the Bethe K = 1.06 of its parent. The charge-2-only bond at lam = 1 is a c = 1 liquid, the charge-2-plus-field bond orders only after x_pi reaches 1/2.
