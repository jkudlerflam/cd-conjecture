# C77: What limits the size of a counterexample
Your folder is `../work_C77/`. Read `../../specs/_simplest_background.md` first. Task: find the strongest physics-level statement about how small d can be in a counterexample. (i) For multi-species hard-core models (d = q + 1 states: q particle species and a hole, nearest-neighbour hopping, density interactions), show what the maximum total c is, accounting for spin-charge separation, Fermi-sea counting and the constraint that only one particle sits per site; compare with d − 1 = q. (ii) For general nearest-neighbour chains, examine counting arguments (number of independent conserved densities, Fermi points per band, Lieb–Robinson-limited velocities, entropy density s = (pi/6) sum c_a T / v_a ≤ log d) and say which, if any, gives an obstruction at small d without a crossover scale. (iii) Identify the smallest d at which no such obstruction is known, and propose a concrete candidate there.
