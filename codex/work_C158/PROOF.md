# C158 PROOF: derivations and the reasoning behind the numbers

Claims are tagged proved, derived, numerical, heuristic or assumed. Bond convention: h = sum J_ab s^a_j s^b_{j+1} + B.s_j, Pauli matrices. Bisector line: J = e_x e_z^T, B = -2 hp (1, 0, 1)/sqrt2, hp the field of arXiv:2209.06221 in units of K (h_c1 = 0.511 is |B| = 1.022).

## 1. Exact structure at XZ

1.1 [proved, check_alg.py] Put a_j = X_j Z_{j+1} and b_j = Z_j X_{j+1}. On each site j the pair (X_j, Z_j) appears in a_{j-1} = X_{j-1}Z_j, a_j = X_jZ_{j+1}, b_{j-1} = Z_{j-1}X_j and b_j = Z_jX_{j+1}. Comparing a_j with b_k: for k = j they overlap on two sites with an anticommuting pair on each, so they commute; for |j - k| = 1 they overlap on one site with equal Paulis (Z_{j+1} against Z_{j+1}, or X_j against X_j), so they commute; otherwise they are disjoint. Within one family, a_j and a_{j+1} share site j+1 with Z against X, so they anticommute, and a_j, a_k with |j - k| >= 2 are disjoint. So {a_j} and {b_j} are two mutually commuting copies of the Majorana-bilinear algebra (i gamma_j gamma_{j+1}), and H_XZ = sum a_j is the critical Majorana chain A, with B absent from H.

1.2 [proved] Corollaries used for validation.
- XZ + eps ZX = sum a_j + eps sum b_j: two decoupled critical Majorana chains with velocity ratio eps, c = 1/2 + 1/2 = 1, e_0 = -(2/pi)(1 + eps).
- XZ + eps (-1)^j ZX: the sign pattern is removed by gamma_j -> s_j gamma_j with s_j s_{j+1} = (-1)^j, so it is the previous model (not a regulator).
- XZ + eps ZX on even bonds only: the b_{2j} commute pairwise and with H_A, so the ground state is (A ground state) x (b_{2j} = -1 for all j), with a B gap 2 eps and c = 1/2 exactly, e_0 = -2/pi - eps/2. Every bipartition through an odd bond cuts one b-pair and adds log 2 to S, which the numerics show (S spread 0.346 = log 2 to 1e-3).

1.3 [proved, check_alg.py] X_j anticommutes with exactly one a (a_{j-1}) and one b (b_j); Z_j with exactly a_j and b_{j-1}; Y_j with a_{j-1}, a_j, b_{j-1}, b_j. An operator that anticommutes with exactly one bilinear of a Majorana chain is a half-infinite Majorana string (an Ising order or disorder operator); one that anticommutes with two neighbouring bilinears is a single Majorana. Hence Y_j = (Majorana of A)(Majorana of B) up to an operator commuting with both algebras (the hybridization of C144), while X_j and Z_j are products of one string of A and one string of B. [heuristic consequence] The bisector field couples the sectors through operators of scaling dimension 1/8 in the critical A chain, a strongly relevant coupling. This is why the second-order "B hopping" picture of C144 (smooth kernel, B scale of order delta^2) does not apply on this line, and why the low-energy theory has to be found numerically.

1.4 [proved] Symmetry of the bisector line. Let H_d be the Hadamard (X <-> Z, Y -> -Y) on every site and R the reflection j -> -j. R maps X_j Z_{j+1} to X_{-j} Z_{-j-1} = Z_k X_{k+1} with k = -j-1, and H_d maps this back to X_k Z_{k+1}; H_d fixes X + Z. So U = H_d R is an exact symmetry (the one used by the coordinator; it is C156's "pi rotation about a + b times reflection" in the Kitaev frame). A field component along x - z is odd under U.

## 2. Estimators and why the naive ones fail near XZ

2.1 Finite-entanglement scaling [assumed, standard]. For a uniform MPS optimized at bond dimension chi in a CFT, S = (c/6) log xi + const with xi the correlation length of the MPS (Calabrese-Cardy for the half-infinite cut, Tagliacozzo et al. 2008, Pollmann et al. 2009), and xi ~ chi^kappa with kappa = 6/(c (sqrt(12/c) + 1)). Rams, Czarnik and Cincio (PRX 8, 041033 (2018)) replace 1/xi by the gap delta = eps_2 - eps_1 of the transfer-matrix spectrum, which approaches the same scaling with smaller corrections in a single-sector CFT. fesfit.py computes all three, plus the slope against xi_ic, the correlation length of the leading incommensurate mode.

2.2 Two sectors with different velocities [derived, heuristic level]. Take two decoupled CFTs (c_1, v_1) and (c_2, v_2), and an MPS that is a product of sector MPSs with chi = chi_1 chi_2 (the optimum need not be a product, but this captures the counting). The variational energy excess is sum_s a_s v_s xi_s^{-2} with xi_s ~ chi_s^{kappa_s}. Minimizing at fixed log chi = log chi_1 + log chi_2 gives v_1 xi_1^{-2} kappa_1 = v_2 xi_2^{-2} kappa_2, so xi_2/xi_1 tends to a constant, of order sqrt(v_2/v_1). Then S = (c_1/6) log xi_1 + (c_2/6) log xi_2 + const, and with xi = max(xi_1, xi_2) the slope dS/dlog xi tends to (c_1 + c_2)/6. The asymptotic estimate is correct. Before the asymptotic regime (chi_2 of order 1), chi_2 can only grow in integer steps, so S grows in log 2-sized steps unrelated to xi_max, and pairwise slopes oscillate around the true value. xzeps eps = 0.1 shows this with pairs between 0.83 and 1.41 around c = 1 [numerical]; I take +-0.25 as the systematic scatter of single pairs in two-velocity systems at chi <= 56.

2.3 The crossover artifact [numerical, with mechanism]. Near XZ the leading transfer eigenvalue can belong to a gapped sector whose xi saturates (here a k = pi mode, xi_pi of about 9 at hp = 0.3 and about 20 at hp = 0.2), while the entropy of the slow critical sector keeps growing below it. Then 6 dS/dlog xi_max is S growth divided by almost nothing and reads 2 to 25, with sign changes, at hp = 0.2 and 0.3 for chi <= 24 to 40. The 1/delta estimator fails in the same window because the levels of the two sectors cross. Neither is evidence for c > 1. The correct reading uses only bond dimensions where the leading mode belongs to the growing sector (xi_ic > xi_pi), and checks that the gapped mode has saturated. This is the main adversarial point against any small-chi c > 1 reading near XZ, and it applies equally to open-chain profile fits at L <= 64, which sit inside the same crossover (C156: S(L/2) = 1.046, 1.039 at L = 32, 48 for hp = 0.3).

## 3. The bisector line

3.1 [numerical] hp = 0.45 (|B| = 0.9). From chi = 12 the leading transfer eigenvalues are a complex pair at arg = +-0.332 pi per two-site cell; xi grows as chi^1.333, and kappa = 1.333 inverts to c = 1.014. S against log xi has slope 6 x (0.168): c_pair = 0.951, 1.005, 1.018, 0.997 for chi = 16 -> 24 -> 32 -> 40 -> 48, least squares 1.008 to 1.009, 1/delta 0.90 to 1.06. The state keeps the U = H_d R symmetry (sym_HR = 1.000000 at chi = 12, tested). Result: c = 1.01 +- 0.03.

3.2 [numerical] Scaling dimensions at hp = 0.45 (chi = 48). eps_k/eps_1 = x_k/x_1 at the largest chi: 1 (two levels, +-Q, Q = 0.332 pi per cell), 2.05 (k = 0), 4.28 (+-Q), 5.19 (k = 0), 5.54 (+-2Q). [heuristic] The pattern 1 : 2 : 4 with momenta Q, 0, 2Q is what a single compact boson with an incommensurate vertex operator gives (x_{2Q} = 4 x_Q for the doubled charge, and a k = 0 operator at 2 x_Q; for a free Dirac fermion, K = 1, the ratio 2 is the current, x = 1, over the 2k_F operator, x = 1/2). The 5.54 against 4 is the expected slow convergence of higher levels at chi = 48. This supports c = 1 as one boson (equivalently the two Majoranas A and B combined into one Dirac fermion, as C156 guessed), and argues against a c = 3/2 Ising + boson structure, which would show an additional real k = 0 or k = pi mode with x_Ising = 1/8 below the boson levels.

3.3 [numerical] hp = 0.3 (|B| = 0.6). For chi <= 16 the leading mode is a k = pi pair with xi_pi = 7.0 to 7.8 while S grows by 0.18; the naive slopes read 14, 6.6. At chi = 24 an incommensurate pair (arg +-0.60 pi per cell) overtakes it. Afterwards xi_pi saturates (8.4, 8.7, 8.9, 9.0) and xi_ic grows (9.6, 12.9, 17.3, 21.3). c_pair = 1.065, 1.026, 1.131 for chi = 24 -> 32 -> 40 -> 48, least squares 1.066, kappa 1.236 -> 1.145. Spectrum at chi = 48: 1 (+-Q, Q = 0.596 pi), 2.09 (k = 0), 2.36 (k = pi, two levels, the gapped sector, ratio still rising with chi), 3.44 (+-0.4 pi). The same 1 : 2 structure as at hp = 0.45 in the critical sector. Result: c = 1.07 +- 0.05 from the three pairs, with the +-0.25 single-pair systematic of section 2.2; consistent with 1 and not clearly above it. The picture is one gapped sector (k = pi, xi about 9 sites) plus one critical incommensurate c = 1 sector.

3.4 [numerical] hp = 0.2 (|B| = 0.4). Up to chi = 40 the k = pi mode (xi_pi = 14 to 20) leads and the incommensurate pair (arg 0.75 pi) is still below it (xi_ic = 3.4 to 7.9, growing). Naive estimates are 4 to 6 (artifact of section 2.3), the xi_ic slope 1.42 (still contaminated, since xi_pi is itself creeping). Undetermined locally; the Typhon job runs it to chi = 384.

3.5 [numerical] hp = 0.6 (control, soliton phase). S = 0.114 at chi = 12 and xi = 1.8, sym_HR = 0.616: gapped and U spontaneously broken, as Sorensen et al. report (twofold-degenerate staggered-chirality phase).

3.6 [heuristic] Why the critical sector moves to the incommensurate pair as hp decreases. At small hp, A is nearly the critical Ising/Majorana chain, but the relevant string coupling of section 1.3 gives it a k = pi mass whose correlation length grows as hp decreases (xi_pi about 9 at hp = 0.3, about 20 at hp = 0.2), while the emergent incommensurate liquid has a small velocity. The crossover length is the larger of xi_pi and the length at which the slow liquid's entanglement overtakes, which grows quickly as hp -> 0. That is consistent with C156's emergent scale of order 1e-3 K at hp = 0.2 to 0.35 and with finite chains up to L = 48 not yet showing growth of S(L/2).

## 4. YX and ZY at XZ

Result: c = 1.00 +- 0.05 at eps = 0.2 and 0.3, in agreement with C154 (SU(2)_1 after the period-3 rotation; the cyclic point (1,1,1) is the Heisenberg chain). ZY is the mirror image of YX (C154, proved by a reflection); the six-site ZY run is in the Typhon job. eps = 0.05 and 0.1, where C154 sees a dimerization tendency, are in the Typhon job.

## 5. Ashkin-Teller plane and the tuned hyperplane

[numerical] At beta = 0.3 every alpha sampled between 0.17 and 0.23 is gapped at chi <= 48 (S saturated to 1e-4, xi <= 32). The phase with a log 2 bond-entropy alternation (B pairs, alpha <= 0.196) and a phase without it (alpha >= 0.2) meet near alpha = 0.197 to 0.2, close to C144's c = 1/2 line (alpha/beta = 0.64 to 0.68). Energies are not concave in alpha there, so some runs sit in metastable branches, and the c = 1/2 line itself was not resolved by these local runs. Nothing here is near c = 1, let alone above it. The tuned hyperplane T (beta = 0.3, alpha = (2/pi) beta) with YX, ZY, XX + ZZ, B_y and bisector-field admixtures is only in the Typhon job.

## 6. What would change the conclusion

A counterexample would need, at some point, c_pair and least-squares slopes that stay above about 1.25 over a range of chi in which (i) the leading transfer mode is stable, (ii) every gapped mode has saturated, and (iii) the Pollmann-Moore exponent agrees (kappa near 1.05 for c = 3/2). No point satisfies (i) and (ii) with c above 1.13. The open risks are hp <= 0.2, where the crossover was not passed locally, and the tuned hyperplane, which was not sampled locally.
