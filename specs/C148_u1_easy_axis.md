## Setting

The U(1) class is h = J_⊥(SˣSˣ + SʸSʸ) + D(SˣSʸ − SʸSˣ) + J_z SᶻSᶻ − (B/2)(Sᶻ⊗1 + 1⊗Sᶻ). With J = √(J_⊥² + D²) and Δ = J_z/J, C129 reduces it to the XXZ ring at flux Lπ in each magnetization sector, with the field cancelling in matched sectors (2L, 2n) versus (L, n), a twist cost at most Jπ²/(4L), and M ≥ M_0, where M_0 = J(1 + |Δ|)/4 for |Δ| > 1. The target L(E_{2L} − 2E_L) ≤ C M is now proved (or reduced) for −1 ≤ Δ ≤ 1 and is trivial for Δ ≤ −1. The side Δ > 1 is not covered by any theorem. Already available there from C129: the dilute-sector bound for n³ ≤ L (uniform in Δ ≥ −1), and the exact saturation region B ≥ J(Δ + 1) where the defect is zero.

The physics: at zero field the chain is a gapped Néel antiferromagnet, twofold degenerate on even rings. Between the critical fields h_c1(Δ) (the spinon gap, where magnetization onsets with a square-root, a z = 2 band onset) and h_c2 = J(1 + Δ) (saturation) the chain is a gapless Luttinger liquid with c = 1 and velocity at most of order J, while M grows like JΔ/4. So at large Δ the ratio J/M is small and the bound should get easier. The hard regions are Δ just above 1 (continuity with Heisenberg) and fields near h_c1 (dilute spinons).
