## The reported d=10 family is now available

The [markerless construction](../markerless_d9_d10/README.md), dated
2026-11-12 and published on 2026-11-18, is the source the collaborator
apparently meant. Its author says it remained in a local checkout until
publication. This supersedes the earlier unsuccessful GitHub source
searches; it does not upgrade a conditional claim into a theorem.

**[Explicit bond; proved invariance in the contribution.]** There are
nine material states: four oriented data letters, one walker, two active
tokens and two blind express tokens. No marker letter is needed. One
uncoloured vacancy makes the mobile model d=10. A common head-motion
rule preserves the legal material sector exactly, removing finite-K
leakage; a penalty can still raise competing illegal sectors. The
microscopic state saving is real and also simplifies this part of the
construction.

**[Computer-assisted finite band certificates; conditional infrared
interpretation.]** The logical model has 2n simple Fermi points with
separated pair sums certified at exactly n=9,10,11,12,14,16,20. Under
the stated matching, sector-selection and infrared inputs, the frozen
sector has c=n and the mobile chain has c=n+1. Thus the n=20 mobile
member predicts c=21 at d=10. No central charge is measured here, no
all-n band theorem is proved, and no microscopic unbounded-c theorem
follows from these seven members.

**[Referee correction and remaining gap.]** An independent AI referee
found that the first mobile bond's genuine on-site energies caused the
cell pattern to lose to a headless competitor. The corrected bond is
J times the entire frozen bond embedded in material-material contacts,
plus vacancy hopping and an occupancy chemical potential. One-cell
checks support the correction. The referee still leaves wrong-length
competitors, several cells, and tokens/ports with vacancies unchecked;
J/t is not quantified against the selector and band margins. The
two-cell port remainder is inherited with hypotheses checked but its
constant not re-derived. These are concrete follow-up targets.

For the user's simplicity preference, marker removal and uncoloured
vacancies are genuine improvements, but the certified coefficients remain
extremely unequal: one n=9 schedule uses g=2^-163 and lambda=2^-359,
with D=1000 in units where the head hopping is one. There is no
moderate-ratio realization yet. This markerless d=9/d=10 family now
takes priority over further dimension-specific d=20/22 work.

## Single-rail nine states: three bands are possible, but this seed is unstable

The [three-flavour study](../nine_state_three_flavours_20261118/README.md)
implements and verifies all five controls as literal nearest-neighbour

onsite matrix outside the available physical control space.

**[Proved finite band algebra.]** An exact rational calculation gives six
simple Fermi roots and positive velocities at a three-flavour point, with
a small independent-control box. Thus the band-synthesis step succeeds.
The certificate's control halfwidth is only 10^-9, and the parameters and
small velocities do not establish a simple or broadly robust model.

**[Numerically matched coefficients; proved property of the leading RG
equations; conditional physical interpretation.]** The full generated
quartic has three resonant pair-transfer channels. Their coupled leading
flow lies in an invariant growth region: all transfer magnitudes grow,
and their forward-scattering dimensions become more relevant. The
smallest initial negative-D margin is about 7.45124 g^2. This includes
coupling through the third channel, which the two-flavour test lacked.

The tested three-mode Gaussian state is therefore unstable in the stated
weak-coupling analysis. **There is no stable c=3 result.** The eventual
microscopic phase and central charge were not determined. Neither this
point nor the bounded exploration rules out stable points elsewhere in
the nine-state family. The earlier two-flavour mechanism is not refuted,
but it does not extend automatically. Arbitrary flavours, finite-K
control, nonzero-port ground-sector selection and matching remain open.

## Eleven states: the actual loop lifts resonances in an analytic family

uses the same eleven states, phases and nearest-neighbour rules. Restoring

**[Proved in the contribution; not independently reviewed here.]** The
loop produces a linear displacement of opposite-Fermi-point pair sums
when the finite express-rail delay is retained. The strict symmetric
Gram limit would incorrectly erase that first-order effect.

All pair-sum margins are proved positive for a new analytic family with
n=1,2,3,4. For every finite n>=2, the two extreme pair-centre responses
are distinct and separated from the remaining responses. The general
problem is reduced to explicit rational moment coefficients: proving
L_j!=0 for 1<=j<=n-3 would complete this sufficient all-n argument.
The needed general nonvanishing is still open. A vanishing leading
coefficient would require higher-order analysis, not prove an exact
resonance by itself.

This does not replace or extend the older unmodified n=11 and n=13
finite certificates; it is a different analytic family on the same
alphabet. Finite-g, finite-K and higher-port errors are budgeted against
the new small splitting scale. The finite-K error includes an absolute
zero-port payload splitting and cannot be multiplied by lambda^2.

The state count improves on the larger all-n architecture, but the
coefficient hierarchy remains severe. The small diagnostic already has

No arbitrary-n band theorem, microscopic CFT, or unbounded-c result

## Qubits: an allowed locking mechanism and a corrected relevance test

The [V4 locking audit](../v4_locking_operators_20261118/README.md) derives
the exact microscopic symmetry actions for the four-term qubit candidate.

**[Exact microscopic algebra; conditional Gaussian analysis.]** Symmetry
allows a quartet term, which changes the emergent particle number by four,
relative-density backscattering, and mixed locking terms. An open c=2
region is compatible with the abstract theory, but the actual symmetries
do not by themselves protect it or place V4 inside it. Mixed terms can
be relevant even when the two primitive terms are individually irrelevant.

**[Correction.]** C180's covariance formula with drift used the wrong
commutator metric for spatial bosonic fields. An explicit free example
has quartet dimension two, whereas that formula gives 2.5. The corrected
formula agrees with the existing C191 chiral implementation and an
independent Fourier-oscillator check. The zero-drift formula and the
quartet selection rule survive. The old drift-adjusted values should
not be used to certify stability.

The most informative future observable is the connected momentum-pi
inter-valley pair-correlation exponent eta_pair. Within the stated
Gaussian regime,

```text
x_quartet = 2 eta_pair.
```

Thus eta_pair<1 makes a nonzero quartet perturbation relevant;
eta_pair>1 makes this particular perturbation irrelevant; equality
requires the marginal flow. This one test would not settle relative
or mixed locking. The existing short-distance, anchor-dependent data
do not give a defensible exponent or quartet-amplitude bound. No new
central charge was measured, and the old 2.5669 entropy slope remains
provisional. No DMRG was restarted.
