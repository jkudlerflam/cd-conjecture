# Running coupling versus detuning: a quantitative conditional test

Status: **proved differential-inequality comparison under the stated
field-theory hypotheses**; numerically calibrated on a test flow.
No coefficients or dimensions of the p153c medium are fitted here.

## 1. Fix the normalization before comparing lengths

Assume a stable two-component compact Gaussian regime with fields
`Phi=(phi_A,phi_B,theta_A,theta_B)` and logarithmic difference covariance
G. With the spatial chiral metric `eta=[[0,I],[I,0]]`, a local vertex is

\[
V_{n,m}=:e^{i(n\cdot\theta-2m\cdot\phi)}:_{a_0},\quad
\ell=(-2m_A,-2m_B,n_A,n_B),\quad x={\ell^T G\ell\over4}.        \tag{1}
\]

The Gaussian Hamiltonian must be positive and its unequal signed speeds
and density-current mixing must be measured in the same convention. The
correct G is described in the companion
[response calculation](../qubit_finite_density_modes_20261118/RESPONSE.md).
Replacing it with two symmetric-line K values is not valid off symmetry.

Choose a fixed positive reference velocity u (physical sites per physical
time) for the RG coordinates. All velocity ratios and their running must
be covered by the comparison constants below; u is not silently set to t.
For the quartet define

\[
H_4=\int dx\,[\lambda_4 e^{iq_4x}V_{(2,2),0}+\mathrm{h.c.}],
\quad
S_4={y_4\over2\pi a_0^2}\int dx\,d(u\tau)
 \cos(2\theta_A+2\theta_B+q_4x+\alpha),                         \tag{2}
\]
\[
\boxed{|y_4|={4\pi a_0^2|\lambda_4|\over u},\qquad
q_4=\operatorname{wrap}[2(k_A+k_B)].}                           \tag{3}
\]

In effective units set v=u/t, t=1/80. Then
`|y4|=4 pi a0^2 (|lambda4|/t)/v`. Normal ordering is at a0; changing a0
requires rematching lambda, fields and covariance, not simply reinserting
the same coefficient in (3). The trial momentum-patch width 1/8 in the
finite-ring calculation does not determine a0 or u.

For one fixed dimension, linearized growth gives
`y(l)=y(0) exp[(2-x)l]` and the detuning scale
`l_delta=log[1/(a0 |q|)]` when `a0 |q|<1`. The usual comparison of
`a0 |y|^(-1/(2-x))` with `1/|q|` ignores both dimension running and
couplings to other operators. It is therefore only a mnemonic.

## 2. The asymmetric operator set cannot be reduced to one cosine

The full local bosonic lattice has integer n,m with
`n_A+n_B` even. Its mismatch is

\[
q_{n,m}=\operatorname{wrap}[n\cdot k+2\pi m\cdot\rho].          \tag{4}
\]

Neither valley exchange nor the symmetric-line Sigma projection survives
Re(w) nonzero. The separation-two source is already included in the
computed quartet. At matching it also supplies pair and density-assisted
channels whose amplitudes must be tracked independently.

| Channel | n | m | Mismatch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Same-valley pair | (2,0), (0,2) | 0 | 2kA, 2kB |
| Inter-valley pair | (1,1) | 0 | kA+kB |
| Quartet | (2,2) | 0 | 2(kA+kB) |
| Valley conversion | (-1,1) | 0 | kB-kA |
| Relative backscattering | 0 | (1,-1) | 2pi(rhoA-rhoB) |
| Mixed quartet/backscattering | (2r,2r) | (s,-s) | 2r(kA+kB)+2pi s(rhoA-rhoB) |
| Other allowed harmonics | any parity-even n | any integer m | equation (4) |

Sums and differences of these charges are generated by operator products.
Detuning each elementary pair does not eliminate its resonant products.
Likewise a nonzero quartet mismatch does not exclude density locking or
a mixed resonance. Unequal raw peak weights do not measure rhoA-rhoB.

If a positive lower eigenvalue bound `G(l)>=g_min I` holds throughout a
weak-flow interval, any operator with x<=2 satisfies
`|n|^2+4|m|^2<=8/g_min`. This provides a finite relevant search radius
once the covariance and its errors are known. An excluded irrelevant
tail still needs a summable amplitude/OPE bound; a low-charge truncation
alone is not a certificate.

For an uncertainty box in the **matched** k and rho,

\[
\Delta q\le\sum_\nu |n_\nu|\Delta k_\nu+
                  2\pi\sum_\nu |m_\nu|\Delta\rho_\nu,
\quad q_{\rm low}=\max(0,|\operatorname{wrap}q|-\Delta q).      \tag{5}
\]

This lower bound must remain valid after the zero-mode/equilibrium shifts
allowed in the weak flow. If it can cross zero, an imposed bare-band
detuning is not a protected cutoff. Non-null relevant vertices also stay
in the test: relevance is not automatically ordinary sine-Gordon pinning.

## 3. Coupled comparison with dimension running

Consider a specified set of active near-resonances, together with a
bounded omitted tail and all generated channels that can remain active.
Let Y be an amplitude norm, for example a weighted sum of absolute
dimensionless cosine couplings with quartet weight one. The weights and
norm must be fixed when determining the constants C,D. Suppose, while
Y<=rho, all of the following are established:

1. The initial dimensions obey `x_i(0)>=x_min` with uncertainty included.
2. The upper right derivative of Y and each dimension satisfy

   \[
   D^+Y\le(2-\min_i x_i)Y+C Y^2,\qquad
   |dx_i/dl|\le D Y^2,\quad C,D\ge0.                          \tag{6}
   \]

   C includes mode mixing, OPE coefficients, velocity anisotropy and the
   controlled tail. D includes the covariance and normalization drift.
   These are bounds, not an assumed universal one-loop normalization.
   Extra forcing by unbounded omitted couplings invalidates (6).
3. Every relevant active mismatch has a maintained lower bound
   `q_min>0`. Keep all such channels in the comparison until the latest
   possible cutoff `l*=max(0,log[1/(a0 q_min)])`. Dropping a channel
   earlier only improves this conservative comparison, provided its
   products were included before it was dropped.

Bootstrap Y<=rho on [0,l*]. Then
`x_i(l)>=x_min-D rho^2 l*`. Define

\[
A=\max(0,2-x_{\min}+D\rho^2l_*).                             \tag{7}
\]

Comparison with `z'=A z+C z^2`, z(0)=Y0, gives

\[
z(l)={Y_0e^{Al}\over1-(CY_0/A)(e^{Al}-1)}.
\]

The explicit sufficient initial budget is therefore

\[
\boxed{Y_0\le Y_*:={\rho\over e^{Al_*}
                  +(C\rho/A)(e^{Al_*}-1)}.}                  \tag{8}
\]

For A=0 use `Y*=rho/(1+C rho l*)`. Equation (8), preferably with a
strict numerical margin, closes the bootstrap and keeps every included
coupling below rho until detuning. It includes the marginal case and a
worst-case dimension drift. `flow.py` checks the closed form and integrates
a saturating coupled example `Y'=(2-x)Y+C Y^2`, `x'=-D Y^2` to verify
the comparison. It does not simulate the unknown p153c RG equations.

**Scope:** (8) rules out reaching this chosen weak-coupling threshold
before detuning under (6). It is not, alone, a proof of a microscopic
stable phase. Resonant products, first-order transitions, loss of two
populated modes, and Gaussian positivity must be excluded separately.
Failure of (8) is **undecided**, not proof of locking. Reaching strong
coupling in a guessed flow is also not an identified infrared CFT.

If a channel has q_low=0, do not give it a finite l*. One must instead
control its full coupled resonant flow. For example, an isolated residual
set with all initial dimensions >=2+delta is harmless under (6) if
`Y0<min(delta/(4C),delta/(2 sqrt(D)))`, with absent C,D constraints
omitted when zero: a bootstrap gives `Y(l)<=Y0 exp(-delta l/4)` and
`D integral Y^2 dl <=delta/2`. For a residual set fed by detuned channels,
their generated initial budget and drift must be included first. No such
resonant-sector estimate has been established for p153c.

These inequalities show exactly how accurate and small the missing
matched amplitude would have to be in that convention. M4, e_L, v,

The rows are not evidence for or against stability. In particular the
finite bound cannot be inserted with M4=1 and e_L=0 just because that
would produce a number.

## 5. Verdict for the inputs actually determined

The available bare minima give q4=-0.0108673493, whereas the strongest
saved L=512 finite-window carrier diagnostic gives q4 about +0.0166.
The companion [interface](../qubit_finite_density_modes_20261118/interface.json)
explicitly marks the dressed thermodynamic mismatch and its uncertainty,
G, velocities, a0 and normalizations as unknown. Formal regression errors
are not matching error bounds. Consequently no positive q_min is certified.

Our new finite source-squared response and its bound supply neither a
uniform B_match nor the remaining inputs to (9). The defensible conclusion
is **undecided**: no stable two-boson region, instability, locking length,
gap, or Virasoro c is established by these data. The new progress is an
actually evaluated connected term, an amplitude bound, a refuted composite
shortcut, and a coupled quantitative inequality for the missing inputs.
