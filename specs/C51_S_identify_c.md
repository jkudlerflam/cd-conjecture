# C51: From constructive RG to the central charge
Read `../../specs/_S_background.md` first. Route: assuming the conclusions of C50 (a rigorously controlled multi-channel Luttinger liquid), prove a statement that identifies the total Virasoro central charge as n + 1 in the sense of the c–d conjecture: for example the low-temperature free-energy asymptotics f(β) = e₀ − (π/6) Σ_a v_a⁻¹ β⁻² + o(β⁻²), the finite-size spectrum organized into conformal towers with the correct multiplicities, or interval entanglement asymptotics. Treat the moving zero-mode sectors at irrational density and the several velocities carefully, and say which definition of c the proof delivers.
