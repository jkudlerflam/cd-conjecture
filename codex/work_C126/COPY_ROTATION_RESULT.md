# C126 supplemental result

2026-11-06. This supplement consolidates independent copy-rotation and trial-orbit checks with the concurrent main write-up. Derivations are in [COPY_ROTATION_PROOF.md](COPY_ROTATION_PROOF.md).

[Proved] Correctly signed copy rotations give, for the standard parity-resolved spin realization of a nearest-neighbor even quadratic fermion chain,

    E_(NL)-N E_L <= pi^2 M (N-1/N)/(6L).

The signed copy cycle has R_N^N=(-1)^(N-1). If the original ring ground parity is p and its fermionic boundary sign is eta=-p, the unfolded sign is eta^N(-1)^(N-1)=-p^N. It is therefore the physical boundary condition of the trial's conserved total parity. This removes the extra boundary-matching assumption for this class. Pairing requires SO(N), not U(N). The trial may contain Jordan-Wigner strings in the original tensor-copy coordinates.

[Proved] The resulting finite-size constants are C=pi^2/4 for doubling and K=pi^2/6 for the Casimir lower bound. The derived A<=pi M is weaker than the inherited sharp A<=2M. C119's 10pi/3 is an all-temperature ratio coefficient; the new comparison improves its zero-temperature consequence only. For a fixed CAR boundary convention unrelated to physical parity, odd N gives the Casimir statement without changing the spin structure.

[Proved] An on-site low-energy symmetry does not follow from permutation invariance. On every even Heisenberg ring L>=4, the two-rung correlation eigenspace with eigenvalue r=4E_L/(3L) is su(2)^direct-sum N. A continuous on-site ground-state stabilizer cannot permute its simple ideals. The cyclic copy permutation does. Thus no exact ground-preserving path exists for any finite N.

[Proved] There is a stronger obstruction to arbitrary, possibly spatially varying rung unitaries in an ordered example. For H=-J sum ZZ, L>=3, and a symmetric cat ground state in each of N copies,

    inf_(U=tensor_x U_x) [<U Omega|Htw|U Omega>-N E_L]=JN,

where each U_x is any unitary on the entire 2^N-dimensional rung. The actual defect is zero. The minimum cost per copy remains J as N grows. All lengths are compatible. The proof is an exact reduction to doubly stochastic matrices and a Hamming-distance bound, not an optimization over a chosen cyclic logarithm.

[Proved limitation] A different choice of ordered vacuum, or a coherent sum of rung-unitary products, can avoid that obstruction. For example the inherited C114 coherent seam state has cost 4J/(L+1) for the same cats. The theorem does not disprove finite C(d), and does not exclude an approximate or spatially nonlocal construction in general.

[Proved] For real ground vectors the two-copy fractional-swap family has the exact cost

    T=sum_x [k_plus sin^2(theta_x+theta_(x+1))
             +k_minus sin^2(theta_x-theta_(x+1))],
    theta_L=theta_0+pi/2.

Every profile costs at least 2 min(k_plus,k_minus) when both coefficients are positive. For Heisenberg, k_plus=a+4a^2/3, k_minus=a-4a^2/3, and 3/8<=a=-E_L/L<=1/2. Consequently every fractional-swap profile has T>=1/3 on even L>=4. Its ratio to the true critical defect diverges at least linearly with L.
