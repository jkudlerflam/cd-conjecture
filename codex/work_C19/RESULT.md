# C19. Uniform spectral filters for the fermion target

## 1. Verdict and scope

[proved] There is a uniform family of finite matrix cells with two qubit
ports, n logical fermion modes, an exactly degenerate 2^n-dimensional
unperturbed code, and exact product fermion parity. Its second-order
exchange is independent flavour hopping with arbitrary hopping phases
and chemical potentials, plus a scalar. Only n+1 port-accessible
excitation energies are needed. The construction uses Jacobi polynomial
coefficients instead of an arbitrary matrix whitening or an arbitrary
unitary completion. All coefficients follow the same finite sums as n
changes. An explicit reflection completion has dimension

    D_ref = 16(n+2) 2^n.                                      (1)

This is the dimension of an entire cell, not a microscopic-site alphabet.

[proved] This removes the unstructured dependence on N=2^n in the
finite-dimensional part of route A. It does not provide the requested
translation-invariant nearest-neighbor program. In particular, an
arithmetic formula or a Python loop is not a local Hamiltonian controller.
No value of d0, and no fixed-d unbounded-c model, is established here.

[proved, restricted obstruction] A graded qubit-port construction with
only k accessible positive energies needs

    2n <= 4(k-1).                                             (2)

Repeating an identical gadget without adding spectral poles cannot
produce arbitrarily many independent flavours. Our k=n+1 construction
obeys this bound. It is not a no-go theorem for uniform cells.

[proved in C18, applied here] The exact frustration-free version of the
single-static-Bell-check interface cannot implement these filters.
Positive Bell checks and positive port-idle terms that annihilate every
history code vector give scalar source resolvents. C18 discovered and
is developing this obstruction during this run. Section 9 explains its
application without claiming its controller work as a C19 result.

[numerical] Two- and three-cell exact diagonalization verifies the
second-order matrices at the sizes in Section 10. Third-order
non-Gaussian terms are present. Thus the statement that only couplings
are renormalized is proved through second order, not at finite coupling
to all orders.

[assumed] The incommensurate target has the usual n-boson infrared
interpretation and weak-coupling stability described in ir_control.
No thermodynamic central charge was measured in this run. The mobile
cell extension and its extra boson remain conditional on a spatial core.

[proved, task accounting] Tasks 1 and 2 are completed at the abstract
finite-cell level, including chemical potentials generated by exchange.
Their spatial-program requirement and task 3 remain open. There is no
proof that a general uniform gadget is impossible, so the premise of
task 4 is not met. An exact BCO adaptation and alphabet count have not
been supplied or silently assumed.

## 2. The target and its grading

[proved] Let gamma_1,...,gamma_2n be Hermitian Majoranas on n qubits,

    gamma_(2a-1) = Z_1 ... Z_(a-1) X_a,
    gamma_(2a)   = Z_1 ... Z_(a-1) Y_a,
    P = product_a Z_a,
    eta_u = i gamma_u P.

Both gamma and eta satisfy the Clifford relations. They are odd under
P. For consecutive cells, the global Jordan-Wigner Majoranas are
Gamma_(j,u)=(product_(l<j) P_l) gamma_(j,u), and

    i Gamma_(j,u) Gamma_(j+1,v) = eta_(j,u) gamma_(j+1,v).       (3)

Use c_(j,a)=(Gamma_(j,2a-1)+i Gamma_(j,2a))/2. The target is

    H_* = sum_(j,a) [-t_a (exp(i theta_a)c†_(j,a)c_(j+1,a)
                          + exp(-i theta_a)c†_(j+1,a)c_(j,a))
                     + mu_a (c†_(j,a)c_(j,a)-1/2)].           (4)

In an ordinary tensor product of cell codes its hopping bond is

    K_hop = sum_(a,u,v=1,2) J_a[u,v]
                 eta_(2a-2+u) tensor gamma_(2a-2+v),

    J_a = -(t_a/2) [[sin theta_a, cos theta_a],
                    [-cos theta_a, sin theta_a]].            (5)

The on-cell field is

    F = -sum_a (mu_a/2) Z_a.                                 (6)

[proved] To check the sign in (5), expand

    c†_j c_(j+1)
      = [-i eta_1 gamma_1 + eta_1 gamma_2
         -eta_2 gamma_1 -i eta_2 gamma_2]/4.

Multiplication by exp(i theta) and addition of its Hermitian conjugate
gives (5), including the minus sign in (4). This uses the common
cell parity, not separate Jordan-Wigner strings for each flavour.

[proved] A particularly elementary uniform choice uses alpha=exp(-1),

    p_(a,L) = pi alpha^(2a),
    p_(a,R) = pi alpha^(2a-1),
    k_a = (p_(a,R)-p_(a,L))/2,
    theta_a = -(p_(a,R)+p_(a,L))/2,
    t_a = 1+alpha^(2a),
    mu_a = 2t_a cos(k_a).                                    (7)

The dispersion is -2t_a cos(p+theta_a)+mu_a. Its two simple roots
are the stated p's, with nonzero opposite velocities. Using the standard mathematical input that e is transcendental, alpha is
transcendental, and no nonzero integer combination of these distinct powers
of alpha equals an integer. Thus the p's have no nontrivial signed
momentum resonance modulo 2pi. This statement includes sums as well as
differences and avoids the zero-band-center pairing problem.

[proved] The hopping strengths are distinct. The chemical potentials
are also distinct. For completeness set x=alpha^(2a-1) and
c=pi(1-alpha)/2. Then mu_a/2=(1+alpha x)cos(cx). The inequalities
cos y>=1-y^2/2 and cos y<=1 imply

    (mu_a-mu_(a+1))/2
      >= x[alpha(1-alpha^2)-c^2 alpha(1+alpha^2)/2] > 0.

The final constant is positive, as follows for example from
0.367<alpha<0.368 and pi<22/7. Each k_a lies strictly between zero
and pi, so each band is partially filled.

[heuristic] This geometric design is deliberately inefficient. Its
slow velocities and resonance margins shrink rapidly with n. It is
sufficient for existence at fixed n and permits the same power update
at each iteration. The established Sidon design in ir_control gives
better margins and could replace (7). Its constants are inherited,
not rederived here. A finite relevant/marginal resonance list has a
strict positive margin at each fixed n; approximate arithmetic need
only preserve that finite margin.

## 3. Orthogonal filters without matrix whitening

[proved] Put k=n+1 and E_e=e for e=1,...,k. Define

    C_ef = 1/(e+f),       p_e=1/k.

For j=0,...,n-1 set

    Q_j(x) = sum_(l=0)^j binom(j+2,l) binom(j+1,j-l)
                            x^l (x-1)^(j-l),
    h_j = (j+1)/[(2j+4)(j+3)],
    f_j(t) = x(1-x) Q_j(x)/sqrt(h_j),  x=exp(-t).

Expand

    f_j(t) = sum_(e=1)^k X_(e,j+1) exp(-e t).                 (8)

These are finite sums of integers divided by one square root. There
is no N-dimensional linear algebra in their definition. Their two
identities are

    sum_e X_ea = 0,
    X^T C X = I_n.                                          (9)

[proved] Here are the orthogonality and normalization steps. With
w(x)=x(1-x)^2, the binomial polynomial obeys Rodrigues' formula

    Q_j(x) = (-1)^j/[j! w(x)]
             (d/dx)^j [x^(j+1)(1-x)^(j+2)].

Its leading coefficient is binom(2j+3,j). Integrating j times by
parts, with no endpoint term because of the displayed vanishing
powers, gives zero against every polynomial of degree less than j.
For Q_j itself the same operation gives

    integral_0^1 w(x) Q_j(x)^2 dx
      = binom(2j+3,j) integral_0^1 x^(j+1)(1-x)^(j+2) dx
      = binom(2j+3,j) (j+1)!(j+2)!/(2j+4)!
      = h_j.

Changing variables x=exp(-t) gives

    integral_0^infinity f_j(t) f_l(t) dt = delta_jl.

Expanding the exponentials gives the second equation in (9). The
first is f_j(0)=0. Thus signs and cancellations in the filters are
part of the construction, not neglected cross-flavour errors.

[proved, small cases] For n=1 the single column is
sqrt(12)(1,-1). For n=2 the two columns are

    sqrt(12) (1,-1,0),       sqrt(12) (-2,7,-5).

These examples expose both the growing coefficient sizes and the
consecutive-energy rule. The exact rational checks test the unnormalized
Gram matrix, avoiding cancellation in floating point.

## 4. Positive weights and exact parity

[proved] Take qubit ports p_L,p_R, partners s_L,s_R, and a flag register
with basis 0,1,...,k. The code is

    G psi = |Omega>_(p_L,s_L) |Omega>_(p_R,s_R)
               |flag=0> psi,
    |Omega>=(|00>+|11>)/sqrt(2).                              (10)

Use the port Pauli basis F_X=X,F_Y=Y,F_Z=Z. The six source isometries
S_(L,a)=F_a^L G and S_(R,a)=F_a^R G are mutually orthogonal and
orthogonal to G. Every traceless single-port compression is zero.

[proved] In syndrome order X,Y,Z introduce

    A_1=(|X><Z|+|Z><X|)/sqrt(2),
    A_2=i(|X><Z|-|Z><X|)/sqrt(2).

They have trace zero and tr(A_u A_v)=delta_uv. They are odd under
T=diag(-1,-1,+1), the Bell-pair grading Z_p Z_s. Notice that A_2
is imaginary. Its transpose must be retained in the left weights.

Define the dimension-3N matrices

    D_e^R = sum_(a,u) X_ea A_u tensor eta_(2a-2+u),
    D_e^L = -sum_(a,u,v) X_ea A_u^T tensor
                                  J_a[u,v] gamma_(2a-2+v).    (11)

At first ignore chemical fields and set

    M_e^R = p_e I + rho D_e^R,
    M_e^L = p_e I + rho D_e^L.                               (12)

They sum to identity by (9).

[proved] These large matrices have a small spectral description.
On the X,Z syndrome block each D has the form

    [[0,B],[B†,0]],       B=(U+iV)/sqrt(2),
    U^2=V^2=q I,        {U,V}=0.

The Y block is zero. Therefore B^2=0, {B,B†}=2q I, and

    D^3=r^2 D,          r=sqrt(2q).

For the two sides,

    r_e^R = sqrt(2 sum_a X_ea^2),
    r_e^L = sqrt(sum_a X_ea^2 t_a^2/2).                      (13)

Equation (5) makes the two rows for each flavour orthogonal with equal
norm, which is the reason the left formula also holds. All t_a<2 in
(7), so r_e^L<=r_e^R.

[proved] Let r_max=max_e r_e^R. Without fields, rho=1/(4kr_max)
makes every weight at least 3/(4k). This exact bound replaces the
large operator-norm computation used in a generic C9 synthesis.

[proved] The physical grading is

    Q = Z_pL Z_sL Z_pR Z_sR P,                              (14)

with the flag even. Each term in (11) is a product of an odd syndrome
matrix and an odd logical operator, hence is even. In the code QG=GP.
This is a product of single-register involutions, including Z on each
of the n spatially labeled logical qubits. The finite matrix Hamiltonian
below respects it exactly. A homogeneous NN realization still has to
preserve this grading in every transition rule.

## 5. Chemical potentials from the exchange itself

[proved] Chemical potentials need no separate splitting of the
unperturbed code. Use the unused Y syndrome on the left. Put

    d=(1,-1,0,...,0),
    g=p^T C d = 1/[2(k+2)],
    sigma=-rho^2/g,

    M_e^L = p_e I + rho D_e^L
                 + sigma d_e |Y><Y| tensor F,               (15)

and keep M_e^R as in (12). The equality for g follows by telescoping
sum_(f=1)^k [1/(f+1)-1/(f+2)]/k. All weights still sum to identity.

Choose

    rho = min(1/(4kr_max), sqrt(g/(4k ||F||)))                (16)

when F is nonzero, and omit the second bound when it is zero. The X,Z
block is bounded below by 3/(4k), as is the Y block because
|sigma| ||F||<=1/(4k). Thus all weights are strictly positive. This
also proves that the square roots used next exist. The small examples
retain the first value of rho.

[proved] The Y block in (15) is diagonal in the occupation basis.
Its square root is obtained by accumulating the scalar
sum_a mu_a(z_a-1/2) and taking one square root. On the X,Z block,
with p=1/k and r from (13),

    sqrt(pI+rho D) = sqrt(p) I + a D + b D^2,
    a = [sqrt(p+rho r)-sqrt(p-rho r)]/(2r),
    b = [sqrt(p+rho r)+sqrt(p-rho r)-2sqrt(p)]/(2r^2).          (17)

This is interpolation at eigenvalues -r,0,r. If r=0 use sqrt(p)I.
It does not require a square root of a 2^n by 2^n arbitrary matrix.

## 6. An explicit reflection Hamiltonian

[proved] Let S denote the 6N-column matrix of all flag-zero sources.
For flag e let S_e be the identical single-syndrome embedding at that
flag, and set

    M_e = M_e^L direct_sum M_e^R,
    W = sum_(e=1)^k S_e sqrt(M_e).

Then W†W=I and S†W=0. Define

    R = I-SS†-WW†+SW†+WS†.                                  (18)

It exchanges S with W and is identity on their joint orthogonal
complement. Consequently R=R†, R^2=I, and RG=G. All these statements
follow by multiplying the displayed isometries and using S†W=0.
No unspecified completion of a unitary is needed.

[proved] Let H_diag vanish on G, be e on every state of flag e>=1,
and be k+1 on the flag-zero complement of G. Define

    H_cell = Delta R H_diag R.                              (19)

Its complete kernel is G, its gap is Delta, and

    S† P_e S = M_e,          e=1,...,k.                      (20)

Indeed R S=W and the flag-e projection of W is S_e sqrt(M_e).
The extra energy k+1 has zero weight on S. This proves the entire
boundary spectral measure, not only the spectrum of H_cell.

[proved] The factorized space in (10), including flags, has dimension
16(k+1)N, giving (1). Every term in (18),(19) commutes with Q because
S and W intertwine the same source grading and H_diag is even.
The port bond

    v=sum_(a=X,Y,Z) F_a^R tensor F_a^L                       (21)

commutes with Q tensor Q. Thus the whole matrix-cell chain has exact
logical fermion parity at every coupling, including all higher orders.

[proved] This Hamiltonian is not spatially local within a cell. The
reflection contains source projectors, flags, and Majorana strings.
Its tensor factorization supplies n logical mode registers, but does
not turn (19) into nearest-neighbor transition rules.

### An additive alternative for the eventual compiler

[proved, not separately numerically tested] The reflection above uses
both ports in one matrix expression. This is avoidable. Give each end
its own flag register and construct the same reflection separately for
its 3N syndrome source, with weights M_e^L or M_e^R. Let h_L and h_R
act on their own port, partner, flag, and the shared n-mode register.
Each is positive and satisfies

    h_s >= Delta Q_s,
    Q_s = I-|Omega,flag=0><Omega,flag=0|_(p_s,s_s,flag_s)
             tensor I_logical.

The projectors Q_L,Q_R commute, and each h_s commutes with the other
end's Q. Therefore h_L+h_R has precisely the common Bell/flag code,
gap Delta, and invariant sectors with only the left or only the right
end excited. In those sectors its source measures are the required
M_e^L and M_e^R. Noncommutation of h_L,h_R on the shared logical
register inside the doubly excited sector does not change these facts.
The two sides together have dimension

    D_sep = 16(n+2)^2 2^n.

Every term h_s touches only one port. The second-order result below
therefore applies to this additive alternative too. It is a useful
compiler target because no energy term needs both distant ports.
It is still nonlocal within each end's workspace, and it does not evade
the exact FF static-Bell-check obstruction in Section 9.

## 7. Second-order bond, all chain terms, and scales

[proved] The full unperturbed chain is sum_j H_cell,j and the
perturbation is lambda sum_j v_(j,j+1). First order vanishes. Inserting
(20) gives the bond coefficient

    H_eff,bond^(2) = -(lambda^2/Delta)
          sum_(e,f,a,b) C_ef M_e^R[a,b] tensor M_f^L[a,b].     (22)

There is no complex conjugate on the second syndrome entry. In (11),

    sum_(a,b) (A_u)_ab (A_v^T)_ab = tr(A_u A_v)=delta_uv.

Terms linear in D vanish by tr A_u=0. Orthogonality (9) kills every
cross-flavour term in the quadratic contribution. The minus sign in
D^L cancels the leading minus sign in (22). This gives +rho^2 K_hop.

[proved] The Y field has zero contraction with either A_u, since their
only entries connect X and Z. Only the scalar p_e I on the right
contracts with it. Its contribution is

    -sigma (p^T C d) I tensor F = rho^2 I tensor F.

There is no field-field term because the right weights contain no
field. Therefore the complete answer is

    H_eff,bond^(2) = (lambda^2/Delta)
       [a0 I + rho^2 (K_hop + I tensor F)],
    a0=-3 p^T C p.                                         (23)

On a ring every cell receives its chemical potential once. On an open
chain (23) puts fields on cells 2,...,M. The pure-exchange ED uses this
open boundary convention. An order-lambda^2 field on the first cell
would give the conventional uniform-field open chain, without changing
the bulk claim. Earlier diagnostic scripts use separate fields on all
cells and are labeled as that variant.

[proved] Distinct bonds have no second-order cross term. A cell at an
outer endpoint of their union is acted on once. Its last projection
contains G†F_aG=0, since the unperturbed resolvent cannot turn an excited
eigenstate of that untouched outer cell into a code state. Disjoint
bonds have the same property. Hence there is no three-cell interaction
at order lambda^2. The entire second-order chain is the sum of (23).

[proved, consistency checks] The result is Hermitian. It is even under
the product parity. The factor lambda^2/Delta has energy units. Turning
all t's and mu's off leaves a scalar. The original resolvent quadratic
form is negative semidefinite, so a0 dominates the positive eigenvalues
of the remaining term; the strict weight bounds ensure this without
an independent assumption. The one-flavour hopping sign agrees with
direct Jordan-Wigner multiplication in the numerical checks.

[proved, finite-cluster estimate] For each fixed n and number of cells,
ordinary degenerate perturbation theory gives a local expansion with
remainder starting at O(lambda^3/Delta^2). The logical scale is
J=lambda^2 rho^2/Delta. A conservative sufficient hierarchy is

    |lambda|/Delta << rho^2/C_n,

where C_n includes the actual finite-cluster constants and port norms.
The condition |lambda|<<Delta alone need not make the error small
relative to the much smaller logical exchange. An extensive operator
norm estimate is not a uniform thermodynamic statement.

[numerical] The cubic coefficient has components beyond scalars and
quadratic Majorana terms, as reported in Section 10. They cannot all
be described as renormalizations of t and mu. Their compatibility with
an n-boson phase is the weak-coupling nonresonant RG assumption from
ir_control. C13's mobile-cell analysis requires additional length-mode
mismatches and does not itself implement this matrix cell.

## 8. A pole-count obstruction and what uniformity means here

[proved] Assume a cell has exact parity, its port grading is Z, its
traceless port compressions vanish, and its source spectral measure has
support on k distinct positive energies. Give X,Y parity -1 and Z
parity +1. A weight entry M_e[a,b] is odd on the logical space only
when a,b have different parity. There are exactly four such ordered
slots, XZ,ZX,YZ,ZY.

For each slot, sum_e M_e[a,b]=0, because S_a†S_b=0. Its span over e
therefore has dimension at most k-1. The entire space of odd logical
operators available on one side of (22) has dimension at most 4(k-1).
The odd-odd part of (5) has operator Schmidt rank 2n, because every
J_a has determinant t_a^2/4>0 and all gamma and eta operators are
linearly independent. This proves (2), over the complex field as well
as for the Hermitian interaction.

[proved, scope] This rules out a repeated constant-pole slice with
fixed k. It does not rule out a fixed microscopic alphabet whose
longer cells have more spectral poles. Our construction needs n+1
accessible poles and is within a factor two of this necessary count.

[proved, algebraic uniformity] A fixed classical algorithm generates
(8),(11),(15),(17),(18) from n and occupation bits. It uses the following
bounded loop bodies, with their ranges obtained from n.

    generate alpha powers and one flavour's t,theta,mu
    generate Q_j by the binomial sum, then multiply by x(1-x)
    accumulate row norms and select rho from (16)
    for each energy flag, build its two Clifford linear forms
    apply the quadratic square-root formula on X,Z
    accumulate occupation energy and its scalar square root on Y
    assemble W and the reflection (18)

The coefficient arrays have O(n^2) entries. Integer binomials may have
many bits, and numerical precision has to grow as the target scales
shrink. The algorithm has no n-dependent list of arbitrary N by N
matrices. The numerical code uses dense matrices only to verify its
finite instances.

[heuristic] Reversible arithmetic, Gaussian mode rotations, and flag
control could implement this restricted algorithm. The Hamiltonian
still needs a rule table that initializes its workspace, computes
coefficients to sufficient precision, applies only parity-compatible
operations, and rejects every malformed program sector. Such a table
has not been constructed here. In particular, “the same loop body”
at the formula level does not prove a 20-to-30-letter implementation.

## 9. The local-program obstruction and alphabet accounting

[proved in C18, inherited] C18's live work shows why the cheapest exact
FF interface fails. In a legal history, suppose every positive static
Bell check and every positive port-idle energy term annihilates the
code at its clock time. For a traceless port operator F, the own
Bell check then maps F|code> to itself, while the other checks and
port-idle energies annihilate it. Port-idle propagation carries these
source histories with exactly the same work evolution as the code.
Their Hamiltonian reduces to a scalar clock problem tensored with the
logical identity. Thus G†F_a exp(-t H)F_b G is scalar in the logical
register. This cannot equal (11) or (15) for nonzero hopping or fields.

[proved, consequence for this construction] The reflection cell has
nonconstant logical spectral weights, so it is outside that restricted
interface. Initialized ancillas do not fix the stated FF obstruction.
One must relax exact annihilation of every positive event, or allow
richer port-touching terms such as syndrome-conditioned work energies.
An approximate non-FF energy program must keep code splitting below J
and preserve the product grading exactly. A small simulator error
without such a scale comparison does not suffice.

[assumed, conditional accounting from C8/C18] C18's two-idle-port
wrapper has separate two-dimensional port letters and bulk clock
letters carrying a work space of dimension w. Its controller skeleton
counts are

| Clock | Merged selector skeleton | Separate C7 walker on the bulk |
| :-- | --: | --: |
| Nine-letter binary clock | 9w+4 | 27w+4 |
| Fifteen-letter full-sweep clock | 15w+4 | 45w+4 |

The plus four counts the two distinct qubit port letters. The factor
three in the last column is C7's L,X,R track on bulk letters only.
These are arithmetic counts for that specified skeleton, not d0 for
the present task. A data qubit alone gives w>=2, but coefficient
arithmetic, flags, work-state types, and any event mechanism also have
to be counted. The binary clock is not by itself a general sweeper.
Neither C18's boundary wrapper nor the uniform formulas determine w.

[proved, unresolved count] No finite complete rule alphabet has been
verified for this cell. Consequently d0 with either selector is
undetermined. The approximately 130 BCO estimate in minimal_d0 is not
promoted to an exact count. No BCO transition table or marker-bounded
adaptation was verified here. Since a general uniform gadget has not
been proved impossible, replacing this gap by an asserted BCO fallback
would not meet task 4 either.

[heuristic, simulator specification] A candidate compiler can now be
tested against a concrete target rather than an arbitrary N by N
matrix. It should preserve (14), implement (19) with the n+1 accessible
spectral weights (12),(15), and control the boundary resolvent error
relative to rho^2. A sufficient conservative requirement is code
splitting much smaller than J, first-order port error times |lambda|
much smaller than J, and local second-order error much smaller than J.
The source vectors and their resolvent are required, not just the
low spectrum. All locality and legality claims must hold on the full
cell Hilbert space.

## 10. Numerical checks and reproduction

[numerical] All code uses Python, NumPy, SciPy, and the Python standard
library. It writes only inside this folder. Dense local matrices are
constructed independently of the predicted second-order answer. Local
spectral decompositions then evaluate the physical source resolvent.
Full-chain low eigenpairs come from sparse Hermitian diagonalization,
followed by QR and Rayleigh-Ritz orthonormalization. The canonical
logical Hamiltonian is recovered from the polar factor of the overlap
with the unperturbed code. This compares matrices in a fixed logical
basis, not only sorted eigenvalues.

[proved, exact sector reduction used in the reflection ED] The unused
outer Bell pair at each end of an open reflection-cell chain remains
in Omega. The cell Hamiltonian and the used port operators preserve
this subspace. Each end therefore has dimension 4(n+2)2^n rather than
16(n+2)2^n. The middle cells retain their full dimension. This invariant
sector contains the whole unperturbed code. Every omitted sector has
unperturbed energy at least Delta. At the tested lambda, the bound
2|lambda| ||sum v||<Delta separates it from the continuing code band;
the much smaller explicit fields in the earlier variant do not spoil
that inequality. This is an exact sector restriction, not a truncated
resolvent or an approximate cell simulation.

[numerical, final exchange-only version] The direct finite-cell tests
are as follows. Both t and mu in these rows come from the port exchange.
