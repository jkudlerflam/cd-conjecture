## Verdict in one paragraph

No c > 1 was found or derived anywhere. The YX and ZY directions at XZ, the most dangerous region named in the spec, are not symmetry-free: they lie on a new 2-dimensional twisted-symmetry stratum that is exactly a period-3 XYZ chain, and there the slow sector is the SU(2)_1 Heisenberg liquid (c = 1) or, very close to XZ, its spontaneously dimerized neighbour (c = 0). For genuinely symmetry-free bonds the codimension count does not exclude c > 1: Ising x floating boson (codimension 1) and two floating bosons (codimension 0) are kinematically allowed, as C68 already found. What decides is dynamics, and every route to a second independent soft sector was closed in the controlled regions and at the onset of every uniform polarized phase, but not in general. The outcome is a conditional derivation of c <= 1 on all of B_2 whose single non-standard input is that no critical component of B_2 is an island disconnected from the controlled set (assumption (H), equivalently a one-species statement (A6)).

## Task 1: codimension and RG count

- [proved] Site-dependent symmetries (PROOF 1, Theorem 1). For distinct singular values of J, writing J = S O (polar), every on-site symmetry, uniform or site-dependent, is Q_j = O^{-j} Q_0 O^{j} with all Q_j in the principal sign group of S. The only site-dependent symmetry not accompanied by a uniform unitary one is a period-3 twisted Klein group, which exists exactly on the cyclic stratum h = alpha XZ + beta YX + gamma ZY with B = 0 (2-dimensional). Together with C67/C68 this gives the complete list of symmetric strata with dimensions: N (8), T (5), K (5), parity (4), U(1) (3), staggered U(1) (2), cyclic (2), XYZ (2).
- [proved, standard] Without symmetry no LSM-type statement survives: translation alone has no anomaly (H^3(Z, U(1)) = 0) and imposes only momentum selection. With B = 0 (stratum T, and the cyclic stratum) Ogata-Tasaki forbids a unique gapped state.
- [derived] Codimension k(C, g_T) = number of relevant spin-zero operators invariant under the infrared image g_T of translation (PROOF 4.1, table 4.2). Incommensurate translation acts through a torus and forbids every charged operator. Minimal counts in N: Ising 1 (period-2 order) or 2 (uniform order, liquid-gas endpoint); floating boson 0; Ising x floating boson 1; SU(2)_2 1 (only at a special radius of Ising x boson; 10 > 8 without a torus action, so impossible otherwise); two floating bosons 0; Ising^2 x boson 3; TCI x Ising >= 3; Z5 parafermion >= 2. So the count cannot exclude c > 1; it isolates P1 (Ising x floating boson) and P3 (two floating bosons) as the only candidates occupying hypersurfaces or open sets. Loci with k >= 3 would have to sit near strata where symmetry removes operators; for SU(2)_k with odd k that is a curve in the cyclic stratum, and a 20-point grid of that stratum (L = 12, 18) finds only the SU(2)_1 region (c_S 0.98 to 1.07) and a ferromagnet-like ordered region near the XZ-FM edge, no third phase (PROOF 3.5; coarse grid, denser one queued).

## Task 2: reduction to symmetric strata

- [proved] The premise's list (U(1), parity, staggered charge, singular) is incomplete: T, K and the cyclic stratum must be added (Theorem 1). T and K are not solved classes.
- [proved algebra, derived physics] Spiral frame (PROOF 2). Every bond is exactly a U(1)-class parent (XXZ + DM along the rotation axis m of O, field along m) plus harmonics of U(1)_m charge +-1, +-2 that carry lattice momenta q(theta + pi) + 2 n k_F; the straight path lam in [0, 1] from parent to bond stays in B_2. For small harmonics the harmonics are irrelevant off a union of resonance hypersurfaces and lock commensurate gapped phases on them, so near the U(1) class every gapless point is a c = 1 floating liquid (emergent U(1) x U(1) from translation), a KT or z = 2 boundary, or a period-2 Ising surface descending from parent Neel order, and is connected to the U(1) class along the path. C68's single floating anchor becomes a statement about every gapless parent.
- [derived] Emergent symmetry at every conformal point: the infrared image of translation (torus for floating liquids, Z2 for period-2 Ising surfaces, full SU(2) x SU(2) on the cyclic stratum's critical region). Ising critical endpoints carry the unprotected Ising Z2 of the fixed point; near the parity stratum their 6-dimensional set contains the parity-class Ising surfaces.
- [heuristic] The strong statement (every symmetry-free critical component connects to a symmetric stratum) is neither derived nor refuted: the path leaves the perturbative regime at lam of order one (harmonic fraction 0.61 at C68's floating point 1282), and nothing forbids a critical locus closing up inside N. Numerical check of the path at 1282: a local pilot (L = 32, 64) gives a smooth window-averaged entropy along lam at L = 32 and c_mid = 0.99 at the parent, but an unexplained 0.51 at lam = 0.25, and was cut short by machine load; the full path (L = 32, 64, 128) is in Typhon job 17594765. The connection of 1282 to the U(1) class is therefore not yet demonstrated.

## Task 3: the dangerous regions

YX and ZY at XZ.
- [proved] They lie on the cyclic stratum. The rotation sigma_j = P^{-j} tau_j maps alpha XZ + beta YX + gamma ZY exactly to a period-3 XYZ chain with bond couplings cycling through (alpha, beta, gamma) (checked to 3e-14). XZ + eps YX becomes XX + eps YY, YY + eps ZZ, eps XX + ZZ; ZY is its mirror; (1,1,1) is the antiferromagnetic Heisenberg chain. Symmetry: twisted Klein plus translation generate A_4 acting by its irreducible 3-dimensional representation, plus Kramers Theta. C144's period-3 commensuration is the twist P on rings with L not divisible by 3, and its exact triplets are A_4 multiplets.
- [derived] Schur's lemma for A_4 forbids every relevant SU(2)_1 operator (tr g by translation, the Neel vector by A_4) and forces the marginal current-current coupling to be isotropic. So in the SU(2)_1 basin the fixed point has full emergent SU(2) x SU(2) and c = 1 (marginally irrelevant sign), or the chain dimerizes with an exponentially small gap (marginally relevant sign). Nothing else, in particular no c > 1.
- [numerical] Ring ED to L = 24 (momentum sectors, L = 24 on Typhon): every point from eps = 0.05 to Heisenberg shows the SU(2)_1 tower in identical quantum numbers. Casimir c with velocity from the current level, at (18, 24): 1.08 (eps = 0.2), 1.045 (0.3), 1.021 (0.5), 1.010 (1,1,0), 1.004 (Heisenberg calibration). Singlet/triplet ratio: above 1 and slowly decreasing for eps >= 0.2 (marginally irrelevant, c = 1); below 1 and falling at eps = 0.05 (0.918, 0.787 at L = 18, 24) and crossing 1 near eps = 0.07 at L = 18 and near 0.1 at L = 24 (marginally relevant: dimerized, c = 0). The excess of c_cas at small eps (1.79 at eps = 0.05) is the gapped-but-slow A Majorana and falls with L. The emergent velocity is linear, v = 5.7 eps at small eps.
- Verdict on YX/ZY: c = 1 (SU(2)_1) for eps above eps_c, with eps_c between about 0.1 and 0.15 from L <= 24 level crossings (heuristic extrapolation; safe range 0.07 to 0.2); c = 0 (dimerized, A gapped) for 0 < eps < eps_c; c = 1 at eps_c. In all cases c_tot <= 1, which completes (EM-XZ) and (NC) of C143/C144 in the YX/ZY directions.

Ising surfaces meeting c = 1 regions (PROOF 5).
- [derived] Each kinematic c > 1 candidate requires a specific neighbourhood: P1 needs a "floating + spontaneous period-2 order" phase; P2 a first-order surface inside a liquid ending at the c = 3/2 point, with an order field independent of the boson; P3 an open c = 2 region entered by a second onset inside a floating liquid. All require two independent soft sectors.
- [derived, conditional on a one-boson infrared] In a single floating boson every momentum-pi lattice operator has zero projection onto the gapless sector (all vertex momenta are incommensurate), so spontaneous period-2 order needs a separate gapped species; within the controlled regions the order field is a vertex of the same boson, which is locked whenever the order is long ranged. Hence Ising surfaces end on Pokrovsky-Talapov surfaces at Lifshitz-type (z > 1) junctions, matching contrib/d2_multicritical and d2_floating_ising numerically.
- [proved algebra, derived physics] Lemma 3 (one soft species at onset): at the instability of any uniform polarized phase the linear-spin-wave band is omega(k) = a_s sin k + sqrt(f(cos k)) with f quadratic, so the soft set is a single incommensurate momentum, a pair +-k_0 bound by pairing into one boson (only when the DM component along the moment vanishes), {0, pi}, or a flat band. Two independent incommensurate modes never soften together. Checked against exact L = 12 spectra at strong field (error falls as 1/|B|).

Coordinator cases. (a) An isolated c > 1 gap closing all of whose deformations lead to one gapped phase is excluded on the free locus [proved], near it [derived], in the U(1) class and its lam-neighbourhood, at onsets of polarized phases (Lemma 3), on the cyclic stratum and near singular strata; elsewhere only direct numerical location of gap closings (C155) can decide, and this run does not exclude it. (b) A c = 2 floating phase is kinematically stable but needs two independent incommensurate soft modes; these are excluded in all controlled regions and at every polarized-phase onset, and the pair and second-minimum mechanisms failed numerically (d2_two_fluid). A nearest-neighbour qubit chain supporting two independent incommensurate modes is therefore not excluded in general, but no mechanism for it survives the tests.

## Task 4: calibration and the conditional derivation of c <= 1 on B_2

Calibration: Heisenberg (1,1,1) gives c_cas = 1.004 and v within 0.8 percent of the exact 2 pi with the same estimator used on the YX line; the (1,1,0) open-chain DMRG at L = 48 gives parity fits 1.08 / 0.70 (wide 1.10 / 0.68), an alternating SU(2)_1 profile. Lemma 3 is checked against exact spectra.

Conditional theorem [derived] (PROOF 7). Assume (A1) every bond is gapped, conformal with finitely many unitary sectors, or singular; (A2) the weak c-theorem of C141 near conformal bonds; (A3) c <= 1 on the U(1) class, free locus and zero-field XYZ [established]; (A4) c <= 1 on the parity and staggered-charge classes [C152, C153, pending]; (A5) emergent c <= 1 near every singular point [C141 to C144, now including YX/ZY]; (A5') c <= 1 on the cyclic stratum [this run, derived plus numerical]; and (H) every connected critical component of B_2 has a closure point in the controlled set of (A3) to (A5') or in the lam-neighbourhood of the U(1) class. Then every conformal bond of B_2 has c <= 1. The proof uses the closure lemma: c is constant on a critical component and c at a conformal frontier point is at least c of the component, so upper bounds propagate inward from the controlled set. (H) fails exactly for a c > 1 island (cases (a), (b), or a "floating + SSB" region far from controlled regions), and can be replaced by the one-species statement (A6): outside the controlled set a conformal point has a single boson or at most two Majoranas.
