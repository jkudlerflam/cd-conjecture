# Larger correlation-length test — 2026-11-10, before chi64 completes

At chi16 and24 the unconstrained infinite MPS has substantial transverse
one-point order. At chi48 this order is below 4e-4, while the leading two
transfer correlation lengths are nearly equal (84.53 and 82.19 sites).
The fit spanning chi16,24,48 gives about 2.13 and has visible curvature;
it is not a central-charge assignment. A change of finite-chi variational
branch invalidates treating those low-chi points as a single asymptotic fit.

After the real-gauge chi64 state completes, extend that branch to chi96.
Compare entropy, the four largest transfer lengths, transverse order,
energy and stationarity. This tests further growth versus saturation and
provides three points on the nearly symmetric branch if chi64 and96 remain
there. A chi128 extension will be considered only after these completed
data show what is unresolved. No coefficient or phase is assumed.
