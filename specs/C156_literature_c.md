# C156: Literature check, nearest-neighbour qubit chains with c > 1

Your folder is `../work_C156/`. Read `specs/_qubit_c_context.md` first.

## Goal

Find out whether any translation-invariant, nearest-neighbour, spin-1/2 (qubit) chain with a one-site unit cell is known, or claimed, to have a critical point or phase with total central charge c > 1, and what general results bound c for such chains.

## Tasks

1. Search systematically: spin-1/2 chains with symmetric off-diagonal Γ terms and Kitaev–Γ chains (uniform, one-site unit cell, for example the work of Yang, Nocera, Affleck and collaborators), XYZ in arbitrary fields, chains with Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya and Kaplan–Shekhtman–Entin-Wohlman–Aharony terms, interacting Kitaev and Majorana chains, compass and 90-degree compass chains, Zhang–Song staggered-field work, and any numerical phase-diagram study reporting c ≥ 3/2, c = 2, SU(2)₂ or Ising × boson in a spin-1/2 chain. For each candidate, check carefully whether the model really is nearest-neighbour with a one-site unit cell (many need a two-site cell, bond alternation, or three-site terms, which do not count).
2. Collect rigorous or exact results that constrain c for these chains: exact solutions, rigorous RG (Benfatto, Falco, Mastropietro, Giuliani, Porta on interacting fermion chains and universality of c), gap theorems (Bravyi–Gosset–Liu–Wong), and any general statements on the number of gapless modes for one fermion per site.
3. Write a short annotated bibliography in RESULT.md: each entry with the model, its exact Hamiltonian in the σ^T J σ + B·σ form when it fits, the reported c and method, and whether it bears on the conjecture. Flag immediately at the top of RESULT.md any credible nearest-neighbour one-site qubit chain with c > 1.
