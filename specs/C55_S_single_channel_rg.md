# C55: The single-channel retarded RG theorem
Read `../../specs/_S2_background.md` first. Target estimate (a) in its simplest case. Take the nearest-neighbour ten-letter demonstrator with the anisotropy made small and tunable (C53's diagonal counterterm, or the collaborator's Jp/Jz tuning in `demonstrator/SINGLE_CHANNEL_RG.md`), off half filling. After massive matching (C47, C48 or the collaborator's finite-cell theorem), its logical action is a single-channel lattice fermion action: XXZ plus a retarded correction with exponentially decaying kernels of all degrees and norm O(lambda). Prove that this action is a Luttinger liquid, in the sense of Benfatto–Mastropietro (arXiv:0907.2837): convergent multiscale expansion, vanishing beta function, a Fermi-point counterterm fixed by a continuity or Brouwer argument, and the density and pairing exponents K and 1/K. Start from the "remaining theorem" stated in the collaborator's note and prove it, or prove as much of it as you can and state the exact remaining lemma. The key new point relative to the published proof is the retarded, arbitrary-degree initial action; show how BM's ultraviolet and first infrared steps absorb it, using C50's finite-scale theorem and the collaborator's UV initialization. If this succeeds, it is the first rigorous Luttinger theorem for a chain whose logical model arises through ports.
