# C33: Do the 23-letter chain's bands cross for every n?

Read `../../specs/_routeA_background.md`, `../../STATUS.md`, and `../../contrib/routeA_spatial/README.md` with its code (ycell.py, logical_chain.py, hermpart.py) first. C32 (`../work_C32/`) works on the same chain's mobility and scales; read its PROGRESS.md and take a different angle.

**Task.** The 23-letter computation-free chain has an exactly degenerate 2^n code, exact U(1) and parity, zero first order, and a second-order hopping matrix T whose Hermitian part is positive definite (checked at n = 2, 3, 4; n = 4 needs a larger express energy D_x) so that all n bands cross a small common field with distinct centres. In the limit of large express energy T tends to a phase times a positive Gram matrix. An explicit counterexample to c ≤ d − 1 at d = 23 needs this to hold up to n of about 23. (1) Prove, at finite parameters with an explicit schedule in n (D_x(n), couplings), that the Hermitian part of T stays positive definite and the band centres stay distinct with Sidon-compatible margins, or find the n at which it fails and why. (2) If it fails, modify the cell within a small fixed alphabet so that it provably holds for all n. (3) Compute T exactly for n up to as large as you can (use the structure; do not build 2^n spaces), and report the margins and band scales as functions of n.

Rules: theoretical-physics standard, finite-dimensional statements proved.
