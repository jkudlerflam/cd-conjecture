# C57: The T² coefficient from constructive RG
Read `../../specs/_S2_background.md` first. Target estimate (b). C51 section 7 shows that a scale-by-scale bound on the subtracted scalar RG contributions, |delta_h(T)| <= C q_h² eta_h min(1, (T/q_h)^p) with p > 2 and eta_h -> 0, implies f(T) = e0 - (pi/12) sum (1/u_R + 1/u_L) T² + o(T²). Prove this bound in the Benfatto–Mastropietro framework for the interacting single-channel lattice model (the XXZ chain at small anisotropy off half filling, then with the retarded correction of C55), by running the multiscale expansion at finite temperature and comparing scale by scale with the dressed Gaussian reference. Determine whether existing work (for example on the specific heat or the free energy of interacting one-dimensional fermions by the Benfatto–Mastropietro school) already gives it, and state precisely which published estimate is used. Then say what changes in the multichannel case. A rigorous T² coefficient for interacting XXZ at small anisotropy, identifying the thermal c = 1, would be valuable on its own.
