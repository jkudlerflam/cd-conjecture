## Steps to attack, at minimum

1. The blow-up (PROOF §1). Is the continuum functional right for every direction u of the perturbation, including directions where the leading anisotropy or DM vanishes and the relevant scales change (the pure-DM wedge with ħ_eff = 2ε, directions where only the field or only a traceless anisotropy enters)? Does the claimed ħ_eff hold uniformly, so that "punctured neighbourhood" is justified, or are there directions where the coherence length stays O(1) or the semiclassical parameter does not go to zero?
2. The semiclassical correspondence (SC). Can quantum effects absent from the classical texture analysis create a second gapless sector near h_F? Examples to consider are order by disorder, the spin Berry phase or topological term for s = 1/2 at the scale of a soliton period, quantum melting of a soliton lattice into something other than one boson, and soliton bound states.
3. M1 (Mather's graph theorem plus Poincaré–Bendixson). Check that the energy is a Tonelli Lagrangian on S² in the needed sense, including the DM term as a magnetic term. Check that the textures relevant to the ground state are supports of minimizing measures (Mather set, not merely Aubry or Mañé), and that the Poincaré–Bendixson step applies to the projected Lipschitz vector field on a compact invariant subset of S². Also check how coexisting ergodic minimizers are treated.
4. M2. The claim is that a minimizing periodic texture has no Hessian zero mode at nonzero Bloch momentum (real positive Floquet multipliers). The DM term breaks reversibility of the chain coordinate, and the linearization on S² is a two-component, gyroscopically coupled problem, not a scalar Hill equation. Decide whether the bottom of the Jacobi spectrum can sit at nonzero Bloch momentum, and if the claim needs a reduction to a scalar normal equation, whether that reduction is valid off the energy surface.
5. M3. Check the solid-angle pairing argument and the claim that the alternative is not a minimizer.
6. Lemma 3′ (magnon minima at k = 0 or π). Check that the BdG form used is the general one for a one-site cell around a canted moment, and what happens with DM along the moment.
7. The two-magnon and McGuire-collapse argument, and the continuum-to-lattice step (persistence of hyperbolic invariant circles).

Use small local checks (seconds) where they settle a point. For example, compute the Jacobi or Hessian Bloch spectrum on an explicit chiral soliton lattice with a DM component and a generic anisotropy, and test whether its minimum ever sits at nonzero Bloch momentum.
