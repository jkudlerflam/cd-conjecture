# Results on qubit stability and small dimension constructions

Date: 2026-11-18. OpenAI Codex, GPT-6 family. Synthesis of the four
completed GPT-6 Astra Max chats in the [stability round](STABILITY_ROUND_20261118.md).
All four assigned turns were observed completed and idle. The proofs and
calculations below have internal checks but no independent human review.
This summary checks their stated scope and does not constitute a new
independent verification of every derivation.

The largest advance is in the modified frozen d=9 family: an exact
ground-sector comparison now survives nonzero coupling between cells on
compatible rings, and the interacting expansion has local error bounds
independent of chain length. The qubit work computes a connected response
at finite density and exposes missing measurements in the detuning test.
The original mobile d=10 model gains a uniform susceptibility theorem for
one channel. No new microscopic Virasoro c or unbounded-c CFT theorem is
established by these four chats.

## Nine states gain local interaction bounds and ground sector selection

The [uniform matching contribution](../markerless_uniform_matching_20261118/README.md)
advances the modified all-n nine-state bond beyond its leading quadratic
bands. A connected expansion through fourth order in the intercell port
amplitude lambda retains the first quartic interaction, connected
three-cell terms, and the distortion generated by the L1 readout. In
particular, that readout generates attractive density interactions; it
cannot be treated as an undistorted scalar field.

**[Proved finite-order bound in the contribution.]** An exponentially
weighted local interaction norm bounds the full residual by O(lambda^5),
with constants independent of the number of cells. Only its projection
onto the logical subspace improves to O(lambda^6). This is a finite-order
local transformation, not an exact separation of the full logical band
from all auxiliary excitations.

**[Proved sector comparison in the contribution.]** For the stated clock
and coupling assumptions, set gamma=E_(n+1) and B=sqrt(2)|lambda|. On rings
whose length is divisible by n+1, gamma>5B places the microscopic ground
space entirely in the translated sectors tiled by the selected cells.
Every competing sector with N_W wrong cells lies at least
N_W(gamma-5B) above the exact selected ground energy. The comparison
includes altered head counts, zero-data cells and headless cells; K=0
is sufficient. See the [complete hypotheses and proof](../markerless_uniform_matching_20261118/DEFECTS.md).

The estimates can be made small compared with the smallest physical mode
velocity and the leading momentum separations, with nonzero coefficients
fixed as system size grows. The decisive remaining gap is an all-scale
interacting matching/RG argument controlling the residual auxiliary
coupling, forward scattering and dressed momentum cutoffs. The separate
issue of incompatible frozen ring lengths also remains.

This round improves control, not simplicity: it adds no physical states
or controls, but the coefficient hierarchy remains severe. The deliberately
conservative n=2 diagnostic has a largest-to-smallest microscopic
coefficient ratio of about 10^289. This is a sufficient example, not a
lower bound on necessary hierarchy. See [scales and scope](../markerless_uniform_matching_20261118/SCALES_AND_SCOPE.md).

## The qubit locking calculation now uses a finite density medium

The two qubit chats use the same fixed phase-1.53 Hamiltonian, with full
on-site field h_z=-19.97589125. Its thermodynamic c=2 status remains open.

The [quartet contribution](../qubit_detuned_quartet_20261118/README.md)
computes a connected four-single-leg response in the leading interacting
blockade model, including both asymmetric pair sources and interacting
intermediate states. Its finite-ring source-expansion coefficients are

| Ring length | Reference particle number | g4,L/t |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | 3 | -0.000309319086 |
| 24 | 5 | -0.001233862897 |

Here t=1/80 and the precise definition of g4,L is a collective Legendre
coefficient at second order in the formal pair-source parameter. These
values are not a thermodynamic continuum cosine coupling. A separate
real-space implementation agrees with the momentum-resolvent calculation
at L=16 to 8.5e-15 relative error. The calculation also demonstrates why
amputating a composite pair response would not identify the desired
four-leg vertex.

A new sufficient running-versus-detuning inequality includes changing
scaling dimensions and mixing with other operators. Its use requires
matched initial couplings, velocities, covariance and a maintained lower
bound on the dressed momentum mismatch. None is supplied by assuming an
order-one continuum coefficient or by extrapolating these two small rings.

## Saved qubit data do not yet determine the needed dressed response

The [finite-density modes contribution](../qubit_finite_density_modes_20261118/README.md)
finds a finite-window carrier shift Q+pi about +0.00830 at L=512, whereas
the bare band minima give -0.00543367. Independent long-pair records also
give a positive finite-window shift. The sign difference shows that bare
momenta cannot simply be substituted for the interacting ones; length
drift and filtering uncertainty still prevent a thermodynamic cutoff
certificate. It neither establishes nor refutes detuning protection.

An explicit perturbative dressing identity shows that raw spin particle
counts contain virtual-pair contributions. Raw density and fluctuations
therefore cannot directly be read as the conserved charges of two bosons.

The usual leading uniform parameter probes have response rank at most
three: one combination is an overall rescaling of H, which leaves its
ground state unchanged. The [response protocol](../qubit_finite_density_modes_20261118/RESPONSE.md)
derives the normalized four-channel kernel needed to recover the full
Gaussian theory, including unequal chiral velocities and density-current
mixing. A distance-two current supplies a fourth channel in a calibrated
free example; its rank and normalization in the actual interacting medium
still need measurement. Saved equal-time correlators do not provide that
missing static response. No target covariance or velocity was inferred
from a control model.

## Ten states gain a uniform response theorem for one channel

For the original corrected mobile d=10 model at half occupancy, the
[thermodynamic selector contribution](../markerless_thermodynamic_selector_20261118/README.md)
proves, for every even material-site count N,

    0 <= t gamma_N(0) <= 3/4,
    lim_(N->infinity) t gamma_N(0)
      = (24-pi^2)/(18pi^3) = 0.025318163345... .

This is the canonical uniform contact-response channel. It is distinct
from the limit of small nonzero momenta. Other momenta now have explicit
determinant spectral formulas; the convenient same-grid two-particle-hole
truncation fails away from zero momentum.

For the actual defects, the unchanged background supplies a favorable
cross term. A sufficient static target can be weakened to local response
and the ten background harmonics, rather than a supremum over every
momentum. A second-cumulant estimate at nonzero J retains clock dynamics
and bounds its curvature without assuming a gap for the vacancy fluid.
Within its stated acoustic calculation the first single-clock logarithm
is J^5 log(t/J), so this term does not force divergent curvature.

The needed uniform bounds on the remaining channels and higher connected
terms are still missing. There is no certified thermodynamic J/t allowance
or complete mobile selector theorem. This original mobile clock remains
distinct from the modified frozen all-n clock and its L1 readout.

## A separate simple qubit candidate was added by the collaborator

The new [chirality-Ising data](../d2_xy_yx_chirality_imps_20261118/README.md)
concern

    H = sum [(9/16) XY + (7/16) YX + ZZ] - h sum Z.

Near h=1.7466, finite-entanglement fits give c=1.53 to 1.59 and an order
exponent about 0.13, consistent with a proposed boson times Ising theory
with c=3/2. This is a different point from the phase-1.53 candidate.
With the field counted once per site as displayed, it has four literal
Pauli monomials and a coefficient ratio about four. It is particularly
relevant to the user's simplicity preference.

The result is numerical: an independent reproduction, the Ising velocity
and an independent exponent measurement remain absent; a weak first-order
transition beyond the observed lengths is not excluded. Nearby apparent
c about 2 also remains unexplained. These data were a separate collaborator
submission, not a new calculation by the four stability chats.
