# A uniform local normal form with every generated operator retained

2026-11-18. **[Proved finite-order theorem; no independent review.]**
This upgrades finite-cluster error estimates to an explicitly defined
interaction norm independent of the number of selected cells. It is not
an exact thermodynamic block diagonalization or an infrared CFT theorem.

The local elimination method is the standard local Schrieffer-Wolff method;
see [Bravyi, DiVincenzo and Loss, §4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/1105.0675v1#S4.SS4).
The proof below supplies a direct majorant for this bond, its weighted
lambda^2 readout, and an exponentially weighted interaction norm. The
linked coefficients, field distortion and defect application are worked
out separately here. No convergence of the infinite-order expansion is
imported from that paper.

## 1. Norm and local gap

For a specified decomposition into cell intervals Phi={Phi_X}, set

    ||Phi||_kappa=sup_x sum_(X contains x) exp(kappa |X|)||Phi_X||,
    kappa>=0.                                       (1)

The norm on the right is the **full many-body operator norm** on X, including
all occupations and auxiliary states. |X| counts cells. On a finite ring
use connected cyclic intervals, including wrapping ones. The decomposition
is the connected-support decomposition generated by nested commutators;
we never replace (1) by the norm of the extensive sum or by one-particle
matrix elements. Additive constants can be separated explicitly.

For the physical model in COEFFICIENTS.md,

    H_0=sum_x h_x, h_x>=delta Q_x, delta=1/2,
    ||H_0||_0<=h=D+8,
    ||V||_0<=v=2sqrt(2), ||F||_0<=f=n|chi|.          (2)

H_0 includes finite g. The conservative bound D+8 follows from the
single-head hopping row sums and a=3-e_v<=4+q^2; it is independent of
cell count and of the number of logical bit states. All parameters and n
are fixed throughout the thermodynamic limit.

If interactions A and B have maximum support sizes r_A,r_B, then

    ||[A,B]||_0 <=2(r_A+r_B)||A||_0||B||_0.          (3)

For example, for terms with x in X, summing the overlapping Y terms costs
at most |X|||B||_0; then include the terms with x in Y. Disjoint terms
commute. This elementary bound is the only combinatorial estimate needed.

## 2. Local inverse and recursion

For an interval X let P_X=product_(x in X) P_x, Q_X=1-P_X and
R_X=Q_X(H_(0,X))^(-1)Q_X. Define

    L_X(O)=R_X O P_X-P_X O R_X,
    D_X(O)=P_X O P_X+Q_X O Q_X.

For Hermitian O, L_X(O) is anti-Hermitian,

    ||L_X(O)||<=||O||/delta,
    [L_X(O),H_0]=-(Q_X O P_X+P_X O Q_X).             (4)

No energy difference between two excited states is inverted. Thus resonances
inside the auxiliary spectrum do not invalidate (4).

Starting with H^(0)(s)=H_0+sV+s^2F, perform m successive transformations.
At step r let O_r be the coefficient of s^r in the current formal series,
retaining its local decomposition, set S_r=sum_X L_X((O_r)_X), and use

    H^(r)(s)=exp(s^r ad S_r)H^(r-1)(s).             (5)

Its degree-r coefficient becomes sum_X D_X((O_r)_X). Lower coefficients
are unchanged. A degree-k term has support size at most k+1, because every
new commutator is connected and degrees add. Every coefficient through
degree m is locally block diagonal and preserves the global code P.

For real s, the finite product of exponentials in (5) is an actual unitary
on every finite ring or open chain. We only truncate the transformed
Hamiltonian, not this finite product of unitaries.

## 3. Explicit, terminating constants

Here is one conservative arithmetic prescription, implemented in bounds.py.
Set c_0=h, c_1=v, c_2=f, and c_k=0 for 3<=k<=m. For r=1,...,m:

    s_r=c_r/delta,
    c_k(new)=c_k(old), k<=r,
    c_k(new)=sum_(l=0)^(floor(k/r))
                c_(k-lr)(old) [2(k+2)s_r]^l/l!, k>r.   (6)

Then ||S_r||_0<=s_r and c_k bounds the relevant coefficient norm. For
k=r the cancellation in (4) is used, since pinching D_X is contractive.
For k>r, iterated (3) gives the factor in (6); its largest support sum is
at most k+2. No term is suppressed because it happens to be nonquadratic.

Let R=m+1, W_kappa=exp(kappa R), and choose

    r_0=min(1, min_(1<=r<=m,s_r>0)
                       [1/(4 R W_kappa m s_r)]^(1/r)).              (7)

Put B_0=h+r_0 v+r_0^2 f. For |s|<=r_0, the exact transformed interaction
has norm at most 4 W_kappa B_0. To see this, let
A=sum_r |s|^r s_r. An ordered chain of l commutators, each of range at most
R, has unweighted bound (2R)^l(l+1)! times its input norm. Its support
weight costs at most W_kappa^(l+1). Summing the factorial denominators
from the m exponentials gives A^l/l!, and hence

    ||H^(m)(s)||_kappa
      <= W_kappa B_0 sum_(l>=0)(l+1)(2R W_kappa A)^l
      = W_kappa B_0/[1-2R W_kappa A]^2
      <=4 W_kappa B_0.                              (8)

The series is uniformly convergent in this norm on a slightly smaller
disk; the boundary estimate follows by continuity or a limit from inside.
Cauchy's bound applied to this interaction-valued series proves, for
|lambda|<=r_0/2,

    U H(lambda) U^dagger=H_0+sum_(r=1)^m lambda^r D_r+R_(m+1),
    [D_r,P]=0,
    ||R_(m+1)||_kappa <= C_(m+1)|lambda|^(m+1),
    C_(m+1)=8 W_kappa B_0/r_0^(m+1).                (9)

This is the claimed **volume-independent full local remainder bound**.
Constants depend on n,D,g,q and the chosen readout/loop, but never on the
number of cells. There is no claim that C_(m+1) stays bounded as m grows.

Take m=4 for the current calculation. Multiplication by i on each charged
head sends V to -V, leaves H_0,F invariant and acts as identity on P.
The local inverse respects this grading, so the projected transformed
Hamiltonian is even in lambda. Consequently

    P U H U^dagger P=lambda^2 K_2+lambda^4 K_4+P R_5 P,
    ||P R_5 P||_kappa<=8 W_kappa B_0 |lambda/r_0|^6.  (10)

The **unprojected** remainder in (9) is still of order lambda^5. Its
coupling to auxiliary states has not been removed by projecting it away.

One can also control the ground energy of the truncated normal form.
The local block-diagonal stability lemma in
[Bravyi, DiVincenzo and Loss, §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1105.0675v1#S4.SS2)
applies to the product code and our local gap. Its sufficient condition is

    sum_(r=1)^m 2^(r+3) c_r |lambda|^r < delta.     (11a)

Under (11a), the truncated Hamiltonian has a ground state in P. For example
take lambda below

    lambda_bd=min(1,min_(r,c_r>0)
                     [delta/(m 2^(r+4)c_r)]^(1/r)).

Then (9) bounds the difference between the exact selected-sector ground
energy per cell and the truncated code ground energy per cell by
C_(m+1)|lambda|^(m+1), uniformly in cell count. This is an energy estimate,
not a statement about long-distance correlators. It is not used in the
finite-defect comparison, for which an extensive energy error is inadequate.

## 4. Frame, connectedness and the computed coefficients

The local transformation can differ from the global positive-overlap
frame by a unitary within the code. Through the orders calculated here
this does not change K_2 or K_4. Indeed S_1 P=R V P, and
P S_1=P S_1 Q; the inverse local transformation has overlap

    P U^dagger P=I+(lambda^2/2)P S_1^2 P+O(lambda^3).

There is no P S_2 P term. Its displayed correction is Hermitian, so its
polar code rotation starts at order lambda^3. Since the code Hamiltonian
starts at order lambda^2, a possible frame difference starts only at order
lambda^5; the grading removes odd projected orders. Thus the explicit
Hermitian fourth-order expression in COEFFICIENTS.md is also the fourth
coefficient of this local normal form. At higher orders the frame must be
tracked rather than assumed identical.

Both the local recursion and the normalized wave operator are additive
on disconnected subsystems. For the latter, the intermediate-normalization
wave operator and its metric factorize, which proves additivity of its
Hermitian representative. Taking mixed edge coefficients then eliminates
disconnected support. This proves connectedness here without discarding
the folded normalization terms.

For bulk clusters, the improved bounds (11)-(12) of COEFFICIENTS.md give

    ||K_4||_kappa <= C_4,
    C_4=2 e^(2kappa)(B_2+B_mix)+3 e^(3kappa) B_3+e^kappa B_F.       (11)

There are two edges and three length-three intervals containing a fixed
cell. This includes the full finite-g operator, all generated many-body
pieces, the mixed readout term and the one-cell field-square term.
Small wrapping rings can instead use the general coefficients (6).
They do not affect a thermodynamic bulk estimate.

The complete order-lambda^2 correction to the scalar-plus-quadratic
reference obeys, directly from (5) of COEFFICIENTS.md,

    ||I_2||_kappa
       <= e^(2kappa) zeta lambda^2 g^4/[16(1-g^2/4)].              (12)

No density, exchange or correlated hopping term is omitted from (11)-(12).

## 5. Converting a local operator bound to a fermionic bound

On an interval of r cells there are N=nr logical modes. Expand an even
operator in normal-ordered products c_I^dagger c_J with a fixed CAR order.
Möbius inversion over occupied spectator modes bounds any coefficient
by 2^N||O||; there are at most 4^N coefficients. Thus its coefficient
one-norm is at most 8^N||O||. Jordan-Wigner strings stay within the same
cell interval because the operator is even. This bound is deliberately
coarse but includes every particle sector.

Resolving every leg into the 2n chiral branches costs at most an additional
(2n)^(2N) in this coefficient bound. Hence the choice

    kappa_CAR=1+n log[8(2n)^2]                       (13)

controls the full coefficient one-norm with an extra exp(r) summability
weight. Polynomial moments of the support, needed for momentum derivatives,
can be paid from that extra weight. This is an explicit full-interaction
normalization; it is not a one-particle proxy.

The cost of this unoptimized conversion is large. At the saved n=2
diagnostic parameters the unweighted majorant radius is about 3.76e-4,
whereas (13) gives about 1.26e-26. The corresponding bounds are sufficient
estimates, not observed breakdown scales. This calculation does not
improve the microscopic coefficient hierarchy.

## 6. Why this is not global Kato elimination

At lambda=0, the entire product code is degenerate and separated by delta.
At nonzero lambda its logical bandwidth is extensive, of order M times
the local logical energy scale on M cells. States containing one auxiliary
excitation also have extensive logical bandwidth. There is no available
volume-independent spectral gap separating the *whole* dressed code
manifold from those states. Even a decoupled logical-plus-auxiliary toy
model has overlapping energy intervals once its extensive logical width
exceeds the auxiliary cost. The usual global norm condition also fails:
||lambda V|| grows with M.

Equations (4)-(9) avoid that problem by inverting only positive local
excitation energies above a product vacuum. They prove a finite-order
normal form with a small local residual, not exact invariance of a global
low-energy band at finite lambda. A small residual could affect arbitrarily
long-distance observables through an allowed relevant or marginal process.
An exact low-energy matching/RG argument is still required; neither an
extensive ground-energy error estimate nor a prethermal interpretation
would establish a compact unitary CFT.
