The specific features to handle: (1) the flavours have velocities and bandwidths separated by large factors, so their conformal regimes set in at very different scales; show the RG from the lattice through each scale down to the infrared, including the stability of the already-gapless modes while slower ones are still being formed; (2) the connected local effective action after eliminating the massive cell excitations, with locality and the size of the generated terms controlled at each order; (3) the generated non-Gaussian terms (third-order pairing is forbidden by U(1); the τ² and λ³ terms of `../work_C31/`; four-fermion forward and exchange terms) and why they renormalize couplings only, given the margins. State every assumption, and say whether a careful theoretical physicist should accept c = n at each fixed n.
