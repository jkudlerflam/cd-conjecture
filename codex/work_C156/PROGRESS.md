- Observation (proved, checked numerically): a chain with alternating bonds is in B_2 whenever J_{j+1} = P J_j P^T and B_{j+1} = P B_j for a fixed P in SO(3) (a screw symmetry); the gauge R_j = P^j gives the uniform bond J_0 P. check_kg.py confirms that the Kitaev-Gamma chain equals the uniform chain J' = [[0,-K,0],[0,0,-G],[-G,0,0]] (spectra agree to 1e-15 at L = 6). check_equiv.py confirms that the AFM Kitaev spin chain in a [110] field equals K sum S^x_j S^y_{j+1} - h sum (S^x+S^y)/sqrt2 (L = 8).
- The Kitaev chain in a [110] field (Sorensen, Gordon, Riddell, Wang, Kee, arXiv:2209.06221) has a reported critical line 0 < h < 0.511 K with no central charge given. This is in B_2, so running ED (ed.py, kit110.py) to measure c.
