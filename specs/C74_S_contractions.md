# C74: Projector chains through Gaussian contractions (Lemma N)
Read `../../specs/_S2_background.md`, then `../work_C61/`, `../work_C64/`, `../work_C70/` and `../work_C72/` in full. C72 proved a moment-first interpolation with bounded counters and a depth induction for chains of nested Taylor projectors on one fixed kernel, but not with intervening Gaussian contractions or with gains assigned to distinct scale cuts. Task: extend C72's depth induction through the Gaussian contractions of the Gallavotti–Nicolò tree (determinant expansion with Brydges–Battle–Federbush bounds), assigning each gain to its scale cut, and so prove C60 (17) and the corrected Lemma N for the single-channel retarded action; or identify the first contraction pattern where the counters cannot be kept bounded. Rules of `_S_background.md` apply: tag claims; no probabilities in any file; Python only in your folder; no network commands (browsing tool allowed for primary sources); plain prose without em-dashes; PROGRESS.md, RESULT.md, PROOF.md.
