## 1. The lattice model

A ring of M sites, j in Z_M. On each site there are n complex fermion modes c_{j,a}, a = 1..n, with canonical anticommutation relations. The reference Hamiltonian is

H_* = sum_j sum_a [ -t_a ( e^{-i theta_a} c^dag_{j,a} c_{j+1,a} + h.c. ) - mu_a c^dag_{j,a} c_{j,a} ],

with t_a > 0. Its single-particle dispersion is e_a(k) = -2 t_a cos(k - theta_a) - mu_a. Assume |mu_a| < 2 t_a and write cos k_a = -mu_a/(2 t_a), k_a in (0, pi). Flavour a has two Fermi points p_{a,+} = theta_a + k_a (right mover, velocity v_a = 2 t_a sin k_a > 0) and p_{a,-} = theta_a - k_a (left mover, velocity -v_a). Index the 2n Fermi points by i = (a, s), s = +/-, and write p_i.

The perturbations allowed are those that the physical construction produces. They are

V = sum_j tau^j(w),

where tau is translation by one site, and w is a Hermitian operator that is a polynomial in the c's and c^dag's, even under total fermion parity (every monomial has an even number of fermion operators), with exponentially decaying tails: w = sum_{r>=0} w_r with w_r supported on sites {0,...,r} and ||w_r|| <= eps e^{-kappa r}. Crucially, NO particle-number conservation is assumed: pairing terms such as c_{j,a} c_{j+1,b} and quartic terms that change particle number by 2 or 4 are allowed. No spatial inversion, time reversal or flavour symmetry is assumed. The only symmetries are translation by one site and total fermion parity (exactly), and Hermiticity.

A second, separate question (part F) concerns the case where fermion parity is NOT exact. There, the chain is the Jordan-Wigner transform of a spin chain with on-site dimension 2^n, and the perturbation may contain terms odd under the total parity. Such terms are non-local in the fermions (they carry a Jordan-Wigner string).

## 2. Continuum input you may use

Near each Fermi point write c_{j,a} ~ e^{i p_{a,+} j} psi_{a,+}(x) + e^{i p_{a,-} j} psi_{a,-}(x) + (higher harmonics). Bosonize each chiral fermion psi_i with a chiral boson, so that a local lattice operator becomes a sum of chiral vertex operators with integer charge vectors Q in Z^{2n} (Q_i = number of psi_i minus number of psi_i^dag, schematically), times polynomials in derivatives of the bosons. At the free point the scaling dimension of the lightest operator with charge Q is Delta_0(Q) = (1/2) sum_i Q_i^2, and its conformal spin is (1/2) sum_i s_i Q_i^2 (sign s_i = +/- for the chirality of Fermi point i). Its lattice momentum is P(Q) = sum_i Q_i p_i mod 2 pi. Fermion parity of the operator is sum_i Q_i mod 2. The Q = 0 sector contains the chiral currents J_i (dimension 1) and products of currents and derivatives.

Small momentum-zero quadratic forms in the currents (forward scattering, sum_{i,l} G_{il} J_i J_l with G a real symmetric 2n x 2n matrix, plus velocity changes) are exactly marginal and move the theory along a manifold of Gaussian (multi-component Luttinger) theories, in which a vertex with charge Q has dimension Delta_G(Q) = (1/2) Q^T Mmat(G) Q with Mmat(G) = 1 + O(||G||) positive definite. You may derive the first-order form of Mmat(G) if you need it.
