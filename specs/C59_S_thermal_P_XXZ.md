# C59: Estimate P for interacting XXZ (the T² coefficient)
Read `../../specs/_S2_background.md` first, then `../work_C57/RESULT.md`, `PROOF.md` and `SOURCE_AUDIT.md` in full, and `../work_C58/RESULT.md` (which shows that (a) and (b) are now the only remaining inputs). C57 reduced estimate (b) to uniform one-step derivative bounds and a zero-temperature defect in a four-history bootstrap, verified the first-order coefficient T²/(12t²) (independently rechecked numerically), and found two leads: Giuliani–Mastropietro arXiv:1211.3030 prove the universal finite-size correction (c = 1/2) for non-solvable Ising models via an RG plus a partition-function inequality, and list the c = 1 interacting case with interaction-dependent exponents as open; a December 2024 Rutgers short talk by B. Renzi (with Giuliani, Mastropietro, Santini) announces a fermionic RG proof of the universal leading thermal free-energy correction for weakly interacting quantum chains. Tasks: (i) use your browsing tool to locate any paper by these authors (arXiv, 2024 to 2026) and, if found, state its theorem exactly and check whether it covers XXZ off half filling at small anisotropy, and then the matched retarded action; (ii) independently of (i), prove the hypotheses of C57's bootstrap for the XXZ chain at small anisotropy off half filling, adapting the Giuliani–Mastropietro scale-by-scale comparison (their (3.84) to (3.91)) from the free Majorana reference to the dressed Luttinger reference with its running marginal coupling and vanishing beta function; (iii) state the final theorem, f(T) = e0 − (pi/6v) T² + o(T²) with v the dressed velocity, or the exact remaining lemma.
