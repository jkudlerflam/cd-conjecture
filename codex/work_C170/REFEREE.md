# Referee report on C165 PROOF.md §1.2, §2 and §4

Each claim is marked correct, gap (fixable) or wrong, with the corrected statement where needed. Re-derivations are in PROOF.md (sections R1 to R5), scripts and outputs in this folder. I wrote all code independently and read only C165's family definition and its JSON outputs.

## Summary

The conclusion C165 draws for weak harmonics, no staggered Ising transition inside the liquid at spin 1/2 and c ≤ 1 near the switch, survives. At the hidden Heisenberg point it is in fact stronger than claimed, because the selection rule behind it is exact within the SU(2)_1 basin. Three statements are wrong. (1) DM harmonics do contribute to the classical N⁴ coefficient, and they do so as strongly as Γ terms (more strongly at the pitches used). (2) The quantum shift of the Néel onset at hz = 0 is O(harmonics²), not O(1). (3) The "gapped Néel up to hz = 2.2" plateau-edge reading at Dz = 2 is a metastable VUMPS state. One gap matters for scope. On the spin-flop line away from Δ = 1 the infrared is not SU(2)_1, so the "one SU(2)_1 primary, dimension ≥ 8" argument does not cover it. The conclusion holds there by a different argument (PROOF R4.2), which I supply.

## §1.2 Frames and the parent

1. Site rotations map the parent to the antiferromagnetic spin-1/2 XXZ chain with Δ = Dz/|J_⊥| in a longitudinal field, for every a, exactly on open chains and with a boundary twist on rings. **Correct** (proved). Re-derived, and checked numerically: spectra agree to 5.5e-14 (L = 8, frame.py).
2. Under the maps, the σ-frame spiral becomes in-plane μ-Néel and the staggered σ^z stays staggered μ^z. **Correct.**
3. The switch is Δ = 1 at hz = 0 and the spin-flop line at hz ≠ 0. **Correct classically. Gap as a quantum statement.** The quantum counterpart of the classical spin-flop field hz_sf = 2√(Δ² − 1) is the Néel plateau edge hz_c1(Δ), which is much lower (0.022 against 1.50 at Δ = 1.25, 0.78 against 3.46 at Δ = 2, PROOF R4.2), and it is a Pokrovsky–Talapov transition, not an SU(2)_1 point. Corrected statement: "classically the switch is the Heisenberg point or the spin-flop line. Quantum mechanically it is the KT point Δ_eff = 1, h_eff = 0, or the Néel plateau edge h_eff = hz_c1(Δ_eff)."
4. ν-frame momentum bookkeeping (charge 2 at 2q, charge-1 bond terms at q + π, transverse fields at q, hz at π). **Correct**, for the classical ν frame. Remark: the ν frame is antiunitary, so it is not the frame for the quantum argument. In the μ frame, charge-1 bond terms and transverse fields share the modulation q + π and hz is uniform. The exact statement is that the full bond commutes with T_μ exp(−i(a − π) S^z) (checked to 4e-15, frame.py).

## §2 Classical Landau theory

5. Proposition 1, rigid-cone linearity in cos²θ for every bond and q ∉ {0, π}. **Correct** (proved). Re-derived. It holds at q = π/2 too.
6. The second-order relaxation formula (2.2), δE = −|P_L B_k|²/(8(1 − cos k)). **Correct.** Re-derived (PROOF R3.1).
7. Charge-2 coefficient c_2 = g² cot²q/4, convex. **Correct.** Reproduced to all digits by exact linear response (lr2.py). With both charge-2 entries it is c_2 = (g² + g_xy²) cot²q/4.
8. Γ coefficient c_1 = G²(1 − cos q)²/(2(1 + cos q)), concave, and the linear part (2.4). **Correct.** Reproduced exactly at Dz = 1.
9. "Charge 1, antisymmetric (DM_x, DM_y): L·B = 0 because L^T A L = 0 for antisymmetric A. No quartic term." **Wrong.** In the ν frame the bond matrix is Rot_j^T A Rot_{j+1}, which is not antisymmetric. The DM energy on the staggered cone has a sinθ cosθ component at momentum q + π. Exact second-order result (PROOF R3.3): pure DM gives a concave u² coefficient −D² sin²(q/2). At q = 0.817 this is 5.3 times the Γ value at equal amplitude. Γ_xz interferes with D_x (and Γ_yz with D_y), so the charge-1 terms do not add separately. Corrected statement:

       N⁴ coefficient = c_2 − c_1,   c_2 = (g² + g_xy²) cot²q/4,
       c_1 = sin²(q/2) |tan(q/2) Γ_c − i D_c|²,   Γ_c = Γ_xz − iΓ_yz,   D_c = D_y + iD_x.

   Every charge-2 term is convex, every charge-1 bond term (symmetric or DM) is concave, fields are linear. Checks: eight random harmonic vectors match this formula to all digits, and C165's formula gets the sign of the window wrong in two of them (check_c1.py). In a direct nonlinear minimization, g = 0.2 has a window 1.000 < Dz < 1.019. Adding D_x = 0.12 shrinks it to 1.004 < Dz < 1.019, as predicted, and D_x = 0.25 removes it (first-order jump between Dz = 1.0200 and 1.0225), where C165's formula predicts the full window (clmin.py, cl_g02*.out).
10. "Harmonics of different charge carry different momenta and do not interfere at second order." **Correct**, but incomplete. Harmonics of the same charge do interfere (see 9).
11. "The terms that allow coexistence are the charge-2 harmonics." **Correct**, and sharper after the correction: they are the only convex terms at second order.
12. Window width 2(c_2 − c_1), with u linear in the detuning. **Correct** once c_1 includes DM. On C165's line Q2 (D_x = 0.03) the DM correction is about 1 percent of c_2, so its classical numerics stand. For the random symmetry-free harmonics of family A3 (DM entries comparable to the others) the classical prediction of which points have a window changes, though the quantum screen itself does not depend on it.
13. §2.4, the window is a few percent of the exchange in this family. Not re-derived (numerical and heuristic, not load-bearing).

## §4 Quantum mechanism

14. At the switch (hz = 0, Δ = 1) the infrared is SU(2)_1, and the staggered σ^z and the two σ-frame spiral components are N^a = tr(gσ^a), one primary of dimension 1/2. **Correct** (derived). Scope: this holds at h_eff = hz + O(harmonics²) = 0, not at hz = 0, because harmonics generate a uniform field (item 18).
15. The quadratic (spin-2) part of the Landau potential is the marginal XXZ anisotropy. **Correct.**
16. A diagonal spin-4 operator has dimension ≥ 8 in SU(2)_1 (h ≥ m², j_L + j_R ≥ 4). **Correct.** Re-derived. Wording: only the spin-4 harmonic of the quartic separates coexistence from a first-order switch. The spin-2 and spin-0 parts of u² renormalize the anisotropy and the constant.
17. "To all orders in weak harmonics only charge-zero products at total momentum zero survive." **Correct, and stronger than stated.** The full bond commutes with T_σ = T_μ e^{−i(a − π)S^z} (exact, checked numerically). In the SU(2)_1 basin an operator with momentum P and charge Q is allowed only if P − Qα ∈ 2πZ, with α = a − π + O(harmonics²). N^z and dimerization (Q = 0, P = π) have T_σ eigenvalue −1 for every α, so they are forbidden nonperturbatively, as long as the infrared stays in the SU(2)_1 basin. Charged relevant or marginal operators need a ∈ {0, π}. Corrected scope: "outside resonance tongues around a ∈ {0, π}" (tongue widths of order harmonic^{2/3} for the dimension-1/2 N^± and of order harmonic for the transverse-field currents), rather than "on hypersurfaces".
18. The spec's question, whether any harmonic generates a relevant operator at momentum 0 or π that splits the multiplet. **Answer:** nothing at momentum π, at any order. At momentum 0 the uniform field J^z + J̄^z (dimension 1, charge 0) is generated at second order, by products of a transverse field with a charge-1 bond term. Numerical check: ring L = 12, hx = G = 0.15 gives m = 1.6e-3, scaling as amplitude² (heff.py). It splits the triplet, but it is the tuning parameter of the parent and moves the bond onto the spin-flop/plateau-edge part of the switch set. It does not create an independent order. C165 should state that its SU(2)_1 point is h_eff = 0.
19. "In the liquid at hz ≠ 0 the staggered moment has no projection on the boson, so a staggered order needs a second sector, which perturbation theory in the harmonics does not create." **Gap (fixable).** The first half is correct (the vertex momenta π + 2nk_F are incommensurate). The second half is asserted, and near the plateau edge with Δ − 1 of order one, where SU(2)_1 does not apply, it needs a mechanism. The fix (PROOF R4.2) is derived. Off resonance the effective chain is a U(1)-symmetric spin-1/2 XXZ chain with weak extra couplings. Coexistence there needs an S^z = 1 two-spinon bound state below threshold. The parent has none, and its spinons scatter with S(0) = −1 and no zero-energy resonance, so a weak attraction cannot bind them in 1D.
20. "The classical window is O(g²) wide, and the quantum shift of the Néel onset is O(1)." **Wrong at hz = 0.** For the parent, the classical switch and the quantum KT point coincide at Dz = 1, so with harmonics both move by O(g²). At hz ≠ 0 there is an O(1) separation, but it comes from the field: at hz = 0.05 the classical spin-flop is at Δ = 1.0003 and the parent's quantum Néel onset at Δ = 1.321. Corrected statement: "at hz = 0 both sit at Dz = 1 + O(g²) and the relative position is not decided at this order. At hz ≠ 0 the classical window lies inside the parent's magnetized liquid, below the plateau edge." The conclusion of §4.2 does not depend on this sentence.
21. §4.3, numerical, line Q2 at χ ≤ 40. **Consistent, not confirming.** Near a KT point the Néel gap is exponentially small, so χ ≤ 40 cannot place the onset ("near Dz = 1.2"). The parent's PT edge at hz = 0.05 is Dz = 1.32, so the points at Dz = 1.25 and 1.3 read as gapped Néel lie on the parent's liquid side. Harmonic shifts of O(0.1) could account for it, but the reading is not decisive. No effect on c ≤ 1.
22. §4.3, "Plateau edge (Dz = 2.0, same harmonics): gapped Néel with N = 0.73, ξ = 4 up to hz = 2.2." **Wrong.** The parent's plateau ends at hz_c1 = 0.78. At hz = 2.2 the VUMPS energy (−2.4781) lies 0.1 above two independent estimates of the ground-state energy: the parent ring at L = 20 gives −2.571, and open-chain ED of the full bond gives −2.575, against an estimator error of about 0.02 (pfull.py, plateau2.py). The VUMPS states are metastable Néel states. Corrected statement: "with these harmonics the plateau edge at Dz = 2 is near hz ≈ 0.8, the parent's value up to O(harmonics²). Above it the ground state is magnetized." Consequence for job 17601089, family A2: the fields sample mostly the magnetized side (Dz = 1.6 with hz from 0.3 against an edge at 0.27, Dz = 2.0 with hz from 0.6 against 0.78). A2 points reading gapped Néel must be checked against a magnetized seed by energy before use.
23. §4.4 and §4.5. Not refereed in depth, as they lie outside the three sections named. One consistency check: "K = 1/2 at the hidden Heisenberg point, K near 1 at the plateau edge" agrees with R4.2 (PT edge, free-fermion K).

## Scope statements

24. RESULT "derived to all orders in weak harmonics". **Correct at h_eff = 0, Δ_eff ≈ 1. Gap on the rest of the switch set**, closed by item 19. Corrected statement: "To all orders in weak harmonics, outside resonance tongues at q ∈ {0, π}, the quantum switch set is the KT point (Δ_eff, h_eff) = (1, 0) and the plateau edge h_eff = hz_c1(Δ_eff). At the first, the staggered and spiral orders are one SU(2)_1 primary and the coexistence operator has dimension ≥ 8. At the second, the chain is a one-boson PT edge with no bound magnon. In both cases there is no coexistence and c ≤ 1."
25. Corrected A_S: "in N, with harmonics comparable to the exchange, along the quantum switch set (KT point near Δ_eff = 1, h_eff = 0, and the plateau edge h_eff = hz_c1(Δ_eff) for Δ_eff > 1), no low-energy sector independent of the liquid's boson (in particular no bound magnon below the two-spinon threshold) while the liquid survives." Item 17 adds that at order-one amplitude the only thing to exclude is a second sector, since the selection rule itself is exact.
26. RESULT Task 1 bullet 3, PROOF §6 item 2 and REPORT §9.124 all say "DM and fields give no quartic term" or "DM and fields do not enter". **Wrong for DM** (item 9). Fields: correct.

## Verdict on the load-bearing claims

- Exact map to spin-1/2 XXZ (§1.2): correct.
- Rigid-cone linearity (§2.1): correct.
- Classical quartic c_2 − c_1 (§2.2): wrong as stated (DM omitted, Γ–DM interference omitted). Corrected formula above. The qualitative statement (charge 2 is the only convex source) is correct.
- One SU(2)_1 primary, dimension ≥ 8 (§4.1): correct at h_eff = 0, Δ_eff = 1.
- All orders in weak harmonics (§4.2): correct at the KT point, and nonperturbative within the SU(2)_1 basin. Gap on the spin-flop/plateau-edge line, now closed by a derived argument. "Quantum shift O(1)" wrong at hz = 0.
- Plateau-edge numerics (§4.3): wrong (metastable VUMPS). This affects the A2 design of the pending screen, not the derivation.
- Overall conclusion (no coexistence, c ≤ 1 near the switch for weak harmonics): survives.
