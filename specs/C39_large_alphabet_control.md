**Task.** The local dimension may be any fixed number. Use that freedom to make the remaining all-n gap (distinct Fermi-sea centres and non-resonant margins for every n, with exact U(1) and a frustration-free code) provable by construction. For example: give each flavour an independently tunable chemical potential or hopping phase through a fixed-alphabet mechanism whose effect depends on the flavour's position in a controlled, explicitly computable way (a frustration-free ruler or gradient track, a second walker whose resolvent supplies position-dependent fields, a history-state controller from `../work_C8/` or `../../contrib/exact_route/` that writes a lacunary field profile, or a per-flavour flux from distinct loop lengths); C28 showed that simple diagonal readouts give at most four independent field profiles, so something richer is needed. Prove that the resulting logical chain has distinct centres and Sidon or lacunary margins for every n, compute the effective bond exactly for n = 1, 2, 3 by two- and three-cell ED, and state the alphabet (any fixed size is acceptable) and both frozen and mobile versions.

Rules: theoretical-physics standard; finite-dimensional statements proved or rigorously certified. Tag every claim as proved, numerical (with sizes), heuristic, or assumed; a precisely located obstruction counts as progress.
