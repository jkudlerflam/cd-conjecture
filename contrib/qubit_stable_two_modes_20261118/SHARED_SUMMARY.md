# Proposed shared-summary text

The focused qubit two-mode assignment now has a concrete interacting
quartet calculation. In the full leading blockaded model, a complete
intermediate-state spectral sum gives nonzero finite N to N+4 amplitudes;
the L=14,kappa=2 coefficient is 0.06381310621 in units g3^2/t. Literal
microscopic qubit spectra approach its predicted Delta^4/V^3 avoided
crossing. This is finite spectral matching, not a thermodynamic C4 or a
V4 exponent.

An exact L=12 same-sublattice dark-state cancellation survives all orders
in the truncated leading pair model, but fails in the literal microscopic
chain: rational elimination gives the next off-diagonal term
(6sqrt(30)/5)Delta^6/V^5. The matching field has
mu-bar=-2+(17/5)(Delta/V)^2+... . This is a controlled cancellation test,
not a two-mode phase.

Within the four-term family the balanced quartet momentum remains
resonant. A fifth monomial vXX detunes it, but also generates the
separation-two pair source. A fixed rational example passes the stated
bare-band and momentum-pi pair-energy checks and has a poor literal
coefficient spread, 4064; no matched stable c=2 region is claimed.
The connected pi-pair estimator is calibrated on exact XX-leg
correlations, and an exhaustive integer-lattice stability routine catches
mixed instabilities. No saved V4 data were fitted and no production was
restarted. The V4 connected pair exponent and full matched stability
matrix remain decisive missing inputs.

`contrib/qubit_stable_two_modes_20261118/`. Root status files and existing
