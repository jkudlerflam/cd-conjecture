# What the fixed chain’s observables resolve

**[Numerical]** The target’s two entanglement methods give slopes near two on their resolved scales. A new periodic-ring calculation finds a common low-lying line in four staggered probes. The leading momentum selected by the large open-chain pair estimator, the number of independently propagating currents and their speeds remain unresolved. All target results below use physical qubits, $a=19/32$, $b=13/32$, $h=1.783$. Here $XY$ means $X_jY_{j+1}$, and the field contributes at every site. The reported CFT convention is the Virasoro $c$ of a compact unitary theory. Identifying a finite-scale entropy slope with that $c$ requires an infrared limit.

## Sources, frames and the limits of the saved measurements

The infinite and open-chain optimizations use the same TeNPy `ims.Chain` model. The open driver sets finite, open boundary conditions, suppresses both pins and uses the full on-site field at both ends. The source contribution reports agreement to 12 digits with an independently assembled open-chain ED at $L=12$, including both down-spin parities. The older chirality field-diagram ED has hardcoded $9/16,7/16$ and supplies methods and symmetry checks at a different coupling. The earlier $c=3/2$ interpretation was withdrawn November 19. The $V/\Delta=40$ density-response work supplies a general matching criterion. Its populations, velocities and fits concern other Hamiltonians. The present target has $V/\Delta=4$, and its direct hop divided by the conventional second-order reference $\Delta^2/(2V)$ is $(3/16)/(1/8)=3/2$. The equal-hop example in the earlier report used $\delta=1/8$. [Source contribution](../../d2_xy_yx_chirality_imps_20261118/README.md), [independent original-frame check](../../d2_xy_yx_chirality_imps_20261118/scripts/otest.py), [field-diagram ED](../../qubit_chirality_field_diagram_20261118/scripts/ed.py), [response conventions](../../qubit_finite_density_modes_20261118/RESPONSE.md), [project report §§9.209b and 9.212–9.214](../../../REPORT.md).

The raw real-gauge measurement uses down-spin creation $b_j^\dagger=S_j^-$, annihilation $b_j=S_j^+$, and $n_j=(1-Z_j)/2$. Set $U=\prod_j e^{-i\pi j Z_j/4}$ and $H_R=U^\dagger H U$. Then $U^\dagger S^+_jU=e^{i\pi j/2}S^+_j$. In the physical frame $\langle b_i^\dagger b_{i+r}\rangle=e^{i\pi r/2}\langle b_i^\dagger b_{i+r}\rangle_R$. For $P_{d,j}=b_j b_{j+d}$, its connected correlation in the physical frame is $(-1)^r$ times the real-frame result. These rules also hold after subtracting measured means. A physical $\pi$ pair therefore appears at zero momentum in the real frame. The uniform $\pi/4$ rotation to the pairing-plus-DM convention changes only site-independent phases here. The new ring calculation uses the original spin basis directly, including the periodic seam at $L=10$. [Frame derivation](../../qubit_chirality_field_diagram_20261118/THEORY.md), [measurement code](../../d2_xy_yx_chirality_imps_20261118/scripts/meas_obc.py).

## Regressions and independent checks on the proposed dictionary

The iMPS estimator uses $6\,dS/d\log\xi$, with a two-site transfer step and the largest retained length after removing unit-modulus cat partners. All listed target rows have cat count zero. At $\chi=96,128,160,200,256$ the rounded inputs give $1.97265\pm0.00986$. The shortest and longest retained odd-sector lengths in this fit are 207.7 and 483.2 sites. The even-sector lengths range from 48.6 to 112.5. Fitting the same entropy against the latter instead gives $1.97927\pm0.01358$. The lengths maintain a ratio near 4.3, which explains the similar slopes without identifying a second mode or a velocity ratio. Starting the odd-sector fit at $\chi=160$ gives $2.00576\pm0.01079$ on three rows. [Estimator](../../d2_xy_yx_chirality_imps_20261118/scripts/an2.py), [recomputed fits](table_readings.json).

For the open chains, average $S(\ell)$ and $X(\ell)=\log[(2L/\pi)\sin(\pi\ell/L)]/6$ over $|\ell-L/2|\le L/8$, then fit across $L\ge96$ with $|\Delta\bar S|\le0.02$ on the last bond-dimension step. The five retained even-parity lengths $96,128,192,256,384$ give $1.95401\pm0.07500$ from the rounded data, consistent with the published $1.96\pm0.08$. Starting at $L=128$ yields $1.84617\pm0.05724$. Consecutive slopes are 2.534, 1.899, 1.569 and 2.041. At the exact target the $L=384$ mean still rises by 0.017 from $\chi=768$ to 1024, and the $L=512$, $\chi=512$ row rises by 0.062 and fails the filter. The quoted uncertainties here are ordinary regression standard errors. Window selection, finite size and bond dimension supply additional systematics. The two entropy methods are complementary and share the model implementation. The reported open-chain controls give slopes of about $0.99,1.48,1.96$ at known $c=1,3/2,2$, respectively. [Open estimator](../../d2_xy_yx_chirality_imps_20261118/scripts/ana_obc.py), [target and control ladder](../../d2_xy_yx_chirality_imps_20261118/tables/open_chain_ladders.txt), [recomputed fits](table_readings.json).

The frozen one-body estimator forms $\langle S^-_i S^+_j\rangle$ at $i+j$ within 8 of $L-1$. It searches for up to two Fourier peaks, requires separation at least 0.6 radians and retains peaks at least 30% of the highest amplitude. It refines their phases, applies four passes of the binomial low-pass filter and averages the fitted exponents. The windows are $0.10L$–$0.30L$ and $0.15L$–$0.40L$. A single printed exponent leaves the number and individual momenta of accepted peaks unknown.

The pair measurement contracts physical spin pairs at $d=2,3$, subtracts $\langle P_{d,i}^\dagger\rangle\langle P_{d,j}\rangle$, then stores already-connected complex arrays. Four anchors run from $L/2-3$ to $L/2$. The estimator chooses the **single strongest carrier anywhere in the Brillouin zone**, refines its phase, filters, and fits all rows against an open-chain envelope over $0.10L$–$0.30L$ and $0.15L$–$0.38L$. At $L=384$ the second window spans $r=58,\ldots,145$. The controls selected $d=3$ as the primary pair separation. The distance-$d$ form factors can differ or vanish, so that choice does not establish a target inter-valley pair. A bare two-carrier product has a no-derivative cross term proportional to $e^{i(k_A+k_B)d/2}2\cos[(k_A-k_B)d/2]$. Interactions can dress its overlap. The frozen errors combine the fitted spread with a control-based floor. The printed `dE,dS6,dGb=0` are constants supplied by the serializer. The separate $L=10$ MPS/dense-vector check of the contractions was reported at $2.4\times10^{-14}$. For any positive Gaussian covariance with $G\,\mathcal J\,G=\mathcal J$, where $\mathcal J$ exchanges each density field and its conjugate phase, Cauchy–Schwarz for $G^{1/2}e_{\phi_c}$ and $G^{-1/2}\mathcal J e_{\theta_c}$ gives $G_{\phi_c\phi_c}G_{\theta_c\theta_c}\ge1$. ## Exact periodic-ring spectral test

**[Numerical]** The new calculation sums literal directed Pauli bonds on a spin ring. Bit 1 denotes $Z=-1$. The off-diagonal amplitude for flipping neighboring bits in a basis column is $i(a z_{j+1}+b z_j)$. We diagonalize all down-parity and one-site-translation blocks using orbit bases with $T|g\rangle=e^{ik_g}|g\rangle$. Annihilation uses $b=S^+$, so the equal-time sums have the same ordering as the saved open-chain $\langle P^\dagger P\rangle_c$. For $O(q)=L^{-1/2}\sum_j e^{-iqj}O_j$, the final momentum is $k_g+q$. We compute $\langle O(q)^\dagger O(q)\rangle_c$ for the normalized projector onto the **entire ground multiplet**. All degenerate final states contribute to the line weight. Connected elastic weight within that multiplet is reported separately from inelastic weight. Sum over all $q$ divided by $L$ reproduces local connected variance to $10^{-10}$ for every probe. The largest eigenpair residual was $7.1\times10^{-14}$. [Code](ring_spectra.py), [spectral data](ring_spectra.json).

## Reproduction

Both scripts run from the repository root, write files relative to their own directories and require no saved tensor states.

```sh
python3 contrib/qubit_ir_cft_team_20261123/evidence/read_tables.py
python3 contrib/qubit_ir_cft_team_20261123/evidence/ring_spectra.py
```

The table regression uses the standard library. The periodic calculation used Python 3.12 and NumPy 2.3.5, one thread, fixed $L=8,10,12$, and a 90-second alarm. Diagonalization and residue accumulation together took 0.151 seconds on the research machine. Only the first six low-energy neutral poles at selected momenta are printed. All eigenstates were included in the connected and inelastic sums. No DMRG or cluster job was run in this investigation. The AI evidence worker used `gpt-6.2-astra` with `max` reasoning on November 23, 2026. [Team model settings](../PROGRESS.md), [numerical next tests](../TODO.md).
