## Active checks before claiming a multichannel RG theorem

1. **Normalized covariance and dependent ratios.** Explicit marked shell,
   Gram and ratio formulas and a triangular normalization resolvent have
   been derived. Their assembly must use one consistent cutoff convention.
2. **Arbitrary-degree localization.** The factorial and field-label sums
   have explicit bounds. The endpoint proof is undergoing adversarial
   review of simultaneous coordinate-zero and field-derivative routing.
   Taylor interpolation must preserve the anchor incidence at each field's
   first contraction; it cannot be treated as an arbitrary coordinate
   substitution with unit Jacobian.
3. **Reference comparison.** The published reference theorem freezes
   quartic histories after velocities are solved. Transferring it to the
   full running-history convention requires an explicit velocity matching
   and cutoff argument. A smooth running-velocity cutoff must not be
   assumed holomorphic for complex velocities.
4. **Sources and conformal identification.** Full-rank two-point response
   alone does not prove an Abelian current algebra or its central charge.
   The source campaign is retaining higher source information and the
   Gaussian operator-product structure. The thermal campaign separately
   retains scalar RG roots and finite-temperature normalization factors.

The conditional sequence theorems do not replace items 1--3, and bounded
running couplings do not replace item 4. A conclusion of c=23, c=24, or
unbounded c is not asserted while these obligations remain.
